AI Datacentres Plan Massive On-Site Gas Power Stations Despite Climate Risks

The Guardian AIArtificial IntelligenceSocietySource published2026-11-11 20:00 SGT
Media reportStage not established

Background As the artificial intelligence boom drives up electricity demand, data centre developers face severe delays in connecting to national power grids. To bypass these bottlenecks and achieve faster power delivery, companies in the US, the UK, and mainland Europe are turning to fossil fuels. Key Details According to research by BloombergNEF, planned data centres across these regions propose building on-site gas-fired capacity totaling 181 gigawatts. About 90% of these projects are planned in the US, where they could emit roughly 345 million metric tonnes of carbon dioxide equivalent annually, matching the emissions of about 72.8 million cars. By 2035, global AI data centre electricity demand is predicted to nearly quadruple to 2,354 terawatt-hours, accounting for 7% of global demand. Impact While developers claim these gas generators are temporary measures while waiting for grid access, experts warn that economic incentives once they are online could keep them running for years. In the UK, the government has even greenlit temporary on-site fossil fuel generation for fast-tracked data centres, sparking strong criticism from environmental campaigners over long-term climate commitments.

Hollywood is fighting back against AI as storyboard artists face displacement

The Guardian AIArtificial IntelligenceSocietySource published2026-11-10 21:00 SGT
Media reportStage not established

The Threat to Storyboards While the prospect of fully AI-generated movies is still on the horizon, generative AI tools are already stepping on the toes of Hollywood's storyboard artists. Traditionally, these illustrators work hand-in-hand with directors, cinematographers, and crew members to map out scenes, serving as the blueprint for the entire production. Now, text-to-image software threatens to replace this foundational step, with directors like Martin Scorsese even partnering with AI firms for their upcoming projects. The Human Collaborative Process Artists emphasize that storyboarding is much more than just producing pretty pictures; it is a vital part of human collaboration. British storyboard artist Alex Clark points out that hashing out ideas, acting things out, and discussing details with the crew creates a shared creative vision. Legendary directors like Denis Villeneuve and Bong Joon-ho view storyboarding as an extension of the director's brain and a crucial form of cinematic writing that fosters spontaneity and emotional connection. Future Outlook Despite the creeping influence of AI, experts and artists believe that the core of filmmaking relies on human touch. Some academics suggest that the friction and difficulty of traditional artistic collaboration are actually the whole point of making art. As AI tools become more common, industry insiders predict that audiences and creators will place an even higher value on human authenticity and the unique imperfections of hand-crafted cinema.

AI-generated complaints are overwhelming UK public bodies

The Guardian AIArtificial IntelligenceSocietySource published2026-11-08 16:00 SGT
Media reportStage not established

The Rise of AI Clog Chatbots like ChatGPT and Claude have made it easy for ordinary people to generate formal complaints, legal challenges, and freedom of information requests. However, this has led to a massive influx of correspondence received by UK public bodies, businesses, and courts, severely straining their limited resources. Impact on Public Services Local authorities report that complaints have grown from an average of two pages to up to 200 pages, often filled with legal jargon, irrelevant case law from other jurisdictions, and occasional AI hallucinations. Employees must still review these manually, leading to exhaustion and delays, while diverting funds away from essential public services. Potential Solutions Experts suggest that organizations might eventually need to deploy their own AI tools to filter out the automated flood, or implement stricter rules such as word limits and submission fees. Meanwhile, some offices have started ignoring automated mass-email campaigns to focus on genuine constituents.

Political Backlash The recent midterm elections saw a wave of pushback against data centers across the United States. Future Challenges Despite these attempted PR makeovers, critics remain skeptical that rebranding will solve the underlying friction. At the same time, tech giants face a financial squeeze, as Walluring investors grow increasingly weary of the astronomical costs associated with building out massive AI infrastructure. Navigating stricter local regulations while managing skeptical investors will likely pose a major hurdle for the industry moving forward.
