Transient beta oscillations precede voluntary movement stopping in rats

Nature Brain-Machine Interfaces ↗Source published 2026-11-14 08:00 SGTResearch paperNeurotechnology & Human Interfaces

Researchers have discovered that a single beta wave cycle occurs in the motor cortex about 200 milliseconds before a rat voluntarily stops running. Previous studies struggled to pin down this timing because traditional tests had hidden stopping times. The team also found that while this brain signal clearly indicates a plan to stop, it is not reliable enough for real-time action decoding.
