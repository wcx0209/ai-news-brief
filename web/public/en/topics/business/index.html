Artificial Intelligence

The Rapid Rise of AI in Food R&D A new report by agritech consultancy Bright Green Partners reveals that roughly 90% of food and beverage R&D leaders routinely use artificial intelligence. The most successful food companies are starting small to identify bottlenecks, demonstrating clear value, and thoughtfully reimagining their workflows to thrive in an AI-driven market.

Amazon SageMaker AI Adds Usage-Based Pricing for Real-Time Streaming

AWS Machine LearningArtificial IntelligenceBusinessSource published2026-11-11 23:10 SGT
Official releaseStage not established

According to AWS Machine Learning: Background Real-time speech AI applications like voice agents and live transcription rely on bidirectional streaming. However, billing for these long-lived sessions was difficult because traditional AWS Marketplace software metering only worked for standard request-response APIs, where the billable quantity is known instantly at the end of a request. For live streams lasting 30 minutes, hourly pricing often charged customers even during quiet periods, failing to reflect true consumption. Technical Details To fix this, Amazon SageMaker AI now opens a second WebSocket connection alongside the main data stream. This companion metadata connection operates on a dedicated path, allowing model servers to send JSON text frames reporting incremental usage. Providers send metrics using the inference.count dimension and ConsumedUnits quantities. Developers can choose whether a dropped metadata connection should terminate the inference session or continue in data-only mode, balancing reliability with metering continuity. Industry Impact This update fundamentally aligns software charges with actual consumption for real-time AI workloads. Enterprise customers benefit from consolidated AWS billing and private offers without managing separate vendor relationships, while model providers gain a standardized path to monetize streaming AI seamlessly through AWS Marketplace.

GeekPark 极客公园Artificial IntelligenceBusinessSource published2026-11-11 13:23 SGT
