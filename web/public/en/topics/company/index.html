Automattic launches Spacefast to help users deploy AI-generated apps

Automattic, the company behind WordPress, is adapting to the AI era by launching Spacefast, a platform designed to host and deploy vibe-coded apps and websites. Headed by Batuhan Icoz, the product evolved from an internal hackathon project sparked by non-technical friends sharing Vercel links. Spacefast provides crucial backend infrastructure including databases, authentication, and analytics. It differentiates itself from competitors like Lovable by allowing users to bring their choice of AI agents and integrate with Automattic's existing ecosystem, such as WooCommerce and enterprise-grade tools. Despite challenges like user churn and dwindling traditional web traffic caused by AI search summaries, Automattic hopes Spacefast will capture the growing market of non-technical app creators.

NVIDIA Partners with Korean Giants to Boost Secure AI Agents

As autonomous AI agents start handling more workplace tasks, keeping company data secure has become a major challenge. NVIDIA is addressing this by rolling out its Open Agent Safety Platform, which features a secure runtime called OpenShell. This tool uses isolated execution zones and strict rules to manage what AI agents can do. In South Korea, major players are already jumping on board. Hyundai Motor Group is testing NVIDIA's new blueprints, while local security firm AIM Intelligence is combining its Starfort platform with OpenShell to block harmful instructions and data leaks. Meanwhile, LG AI Research is opening API access to its K-EXAONE-2.5 model, which was built using a massive dataset of 7 million synthetic Korean personas developed with NVIDIA. This collaboration helps ensure that AI tools are tailored to local contexts while keeping enterprise workflows secure.

Kokuyo eliminates scattered documents in development with Notion and AI

As Kokuyo's engineering organization expanded to about 100 people, requirements, designs, and meeting minutes were scattered across multiple cloud services and file servers, making it hard to find information. They have also integrated Claude with Notion MCP, allowing AI to retrieve and update information directly. Moving forward, Kokuyo plans to expand this AI-driven office setup across the entire company to accelerate decision-making.

GitHub, Google, and Siemens Share 15 Design Patterns for AI Agents

Background As AI agents move beyond simple chat interfaces into complex, multi-step workflows, ensuring reliability and structural control has become a major challenge for developers. Key Details GitHub, Google, and Siemens have released a comprehensive guide detailing 15 design patterns for agentic systems. These include data validation schemas, error-handling loops, human-in-the-loop verification, and advanced context management using tools like Model Context Protocol (MCP). The patterns address common failure points such as infinite loops, formatting errors, and unverified data modifications. Impact These standardized patterns provide a practical roadmap for companies looking to deploy AI agents securely. By establishing clear boundaries and verification steps, they minimize the risk of costly errors and pave the way for autonomous AI systems in software development and enterprise operations.

NYT Cooking’s AI assistant saved my weeknight chicken, but almost ruined my cake

The NYT Cooking app recently introduced an AI assistant trained on user comments and its extensive recipe database, designed to help users customize recipes on the fly. How NVIDIA Open Source Tools Are Helping Researchers Build Next-Generation 6G Networks

Over the past year, NVIDIA's open source 5G and 6G tools, such as Aerial and Sionna, have surpassed one million downloads and are used by over 8,000 research and academic institutions globally. These tools connect simulation and AI training with real-world wireless experiments, helping researchers tackle key challenges in future networks. At King's College London, researchers used NVIDIA Sionna to develop goal-oriented semantic communications for robots. Instead of sending full images, robots transmit compact object descriptions, reducing task completion time by up to 52.6% in dynamic environments. Meanwhile, SoftBank and ETH Zurich demonstrated that AI-powered neural receivers can significantly improve signal reception and throughput under interference. Energy management and simulation speed are also major areas of improvement. Researchers at the University of Leeds used a portable 5G setup powered by NVIDIA to predict network demand, cutting base station power consumption by 45.6%. Additionally, the Centre Tecnològic de Telecomunicacions de Catalunya used GPU acceleration to reduce network simulation runtimes from hours to minutes, speeding up the path from innovation to real-world deployment.
