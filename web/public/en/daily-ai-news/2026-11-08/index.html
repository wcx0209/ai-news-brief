GitHub's Next Scaling Challenge: Rebuilding the Software Stack for Agent Workloads

In a recent discussion following his GitHub Universe keynote, Microsoft CoreAI EVP Jay Parikh outlined how agent workloads are fundamentally changing GitHub's scaling requirements. With the platform hosting 225 million developers and 34.3 billion commits, Parikh discussed model orchestration through Copilot Fusion, cross-device AI sessions, and the necessity of measuring AI spending against actual business outcomes. These insights point toward an industry-wide transition where infrastructure must adapt from human-centric to agent-centric software development.
