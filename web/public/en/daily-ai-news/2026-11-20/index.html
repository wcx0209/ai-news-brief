Hugging Face ↗Source published 2026-11-20 08:00 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

Thinking Machines Lab has released Inkling 2 Mini, a multimodal mixture-of-experts model designed for local AI setups. With around 26 billion total physical parameters and 3 billion active parameters per token, it uses a clever looping mechanism to simulate a much larger model. It supports a 1-million-token context window and handles both text and images, delivering top-tier performance for its size.
