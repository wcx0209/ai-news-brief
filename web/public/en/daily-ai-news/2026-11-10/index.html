Instead of traditional clusters wasting time on cross-node communication delays, supernodes tightly link hundreds or thousands of GPUs together into what acts as a single giant chip, dramatically boosting training efficiency. This massive shift transforms the AI hardware market from a game of raw chip specs to an intense race for system integration and interconnect protocols.
