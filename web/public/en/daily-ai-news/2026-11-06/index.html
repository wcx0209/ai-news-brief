Google Research introduces AI system to detect hypertension using smartwatches

Google Research has developed an artificial intelligence system that uses everyday smartwatch sensors to passively detect signs of high blood pressure without needing traditional cuff calibration. Published in Nature, the research shows the tool achieves accuracy levels comparable to clinical standards, potentially helping bridge the global awareness gap for a condition that affects over one billion people.

Exploring Weight-Space Programming: Capabilities, Generalization, and Limitations

This preprint investigates Weight-Space Programming (WSP), an alternative to gradient-based adaptation where reasoning agents directly inspect and modify neural network parameters. Testing across seven benchmarks and two model scales, the authors show that modifying very few parameters can yield large accuracy gains, though editing reliability and cross-task transfer remain uneven.
