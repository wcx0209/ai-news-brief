Google Research uses wearables and AI to forecast personalized health habits

Google launches Gemini Decisions API for fast, low-cost multimodal choices

Google has introduced the Gemini Decisions API, powered by a new Gemini Decision 1 model. It is built for fast, bounded choices like tool selection and policy checks, processing multimodal inputs and up to 1,000 questions in a single forward pass without generating answer text token by token. This slashes end-to-end latency to 150-250 ms and cuts costs significantly.
