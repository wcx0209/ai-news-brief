ChatGPT Wallet lets OpenAI's Dots agents buy things for you

The Verge AI ↗Source published 2026-11-14 04:45 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

OpenAI is launching ChatGPT Wallet for its US Pro subscribers, allowing its Dots AI agents to make online purchases. The system uses biometric verification for every transaction and generates payment tokens so your actual credit card details are never shared. In the future, OpenAI plans to let users fully delegate purchases by setting strict limits and pre-approval rules.
