HOPE: A Smarter Way to Trim Large AI Models Without Losing Brainpower

Hugging Face ↗Source published 2026-11-09 20:34 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

Large AI models use a "Mixture-of-Experts" design to save power, but most experts sit idle while taking up massive memory. Traditional pruning methods cut the weakest individual experts, which often breaks cooperative groups. A new technique called HOPE accounts for how experts work together, allowing researchers to trim up to 50% of a model's experts while maintaining high performance, especially in complex coding tasks.
