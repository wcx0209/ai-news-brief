Introducing Inkling 2 Small: A Powerful New Open-Weight AI Model

Thinking Machines LabArtificial IntelligenceModelsSource published2026-11-05 08:00 SGT
Official releaseStage not established

According to Thinking Machines Lab: Inkling 2 Small represents a major leap forward, featuring 594B total parameters and 17B active parameters, with robust support for image inputs and variable execution effort. In terms of training, the model underwent extensive pre-training across multiple data phases, followed by a massive 9-day reinforcement learning run using thousands of GPUs. It excels significantly in long-horizon autonomous tasks, such as agentic coding, automated research, and complex financial analysis, often rivaling or outperforming existing open and closed-weight competitors on benchmark tests. Security and alignment were core priorities during its development. The team implemented rigorous multi-layer monitoring and specialized alignment techniques to curb undesirable behaviors like reward hacking and covert actions, ensuring safer autonomous operations. While initial weights are restricted for safety testing, the complete release under an Apache 2.0 license aims to provide the developer community with a state-of-the-art open foundation model.

Apple Researchers Train Neural Networks to Speed Up RAG Retrieval

Apple Machine LearningArtificial IntelligenceResearchSource published2026-11-05 08:00 SGT
Official releaseStage not established

According to Apple Machine Learning: In a paper presented at ICML 2026, Apple researchers introduced a technique called amortized maximum inner product search (MIPS) to improve retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and search systems. Traditional systems often rely on geometric approximations, such as clustering keys and comparing queries to cluster centroids, which can lead to errors because queries (short user questions) and keys (long curated texts) differ significantly. To solve this, the researchers train small neural networks offline—specifically SupportNet and KeyNet—using supervised learning on typical query distributions. SupportNet learns the mathematical support function of data clusters to route queries more accurately, helping the system find the right partition up to 1.9 times faster than traditional centroid routing. Meanwhile, KeyNet acts as a query rewriter that transforms incoming questions directly into predicted key vectors, allowing existing index systems to retrieve true keys up to 11 times fewer database scans. This method effectively trades a one-time training cost for significantly faster inference, demonstrating how knowing the query distribution in advance can dramatically optimize computational resources in production machine learning systems.

Official releaseStage not established

According to Mistral AI News: Mistral has announced major upgrades to Mistral Studio, its enterprise AI platform, alongside the release of its new flagship model, Mistral Large 4 (affectionately nicknamed Le Chonk). The updated Studio platform introduces managed agents, automated workflows, and enterprise application building tools designed to move corporate AI projects out of the pilot phase and into active production. Core Features The new Studio platform brings together agents, workflows, and apps on a shared infrastructure. Agents can investigate complex tasks and take actions, workflows coordinate multi-step business processes with human approvals, and custom apps give employees direct access to these AI systems. The platform provides OpenTelemetry traces for full observability, allowing companies to audit model calls, tool usage, and approval steps to ensure safe and reliable operations.

Atlas World Model Gets Real-Time Exploration with Frameboy

World LabsArtificial IntelligenceModelsSource published2026-11-05 08:00 SGT
Official releaseStage not established

According to World Labs: Background Two months ago, the team introduced Atlas, an autoregressive world model capable of generating frames one at a time with precise camera control. However, generating videos previously required waiting tens of seconds or minutes, forcing users to be very cautious with their inputs and severely limiting creative freedom. Details and Features The newly released Frameboy demo streams optimized Atlas-generated frames from cloud GPUs directly to web browsers at up to 15 fps. Additionally, the team integrated this capability into Viewfinder, a web app that allows users to build custom scenes frame-by-frame using a single input image or text caption, and then explore them live. Impact By dropping latency and making generation nearly instantaneous, the cost of a bad idea becomes zero. Users no longer perceive a distinct act of waiting for generation, turning what used to be a tedious trial-and-error process into an intuitive exploration experience that paves the way for future generative 3D renderers.

Cerebras CEO Pushes Back Against Short Seller Report on Chip Lifespan

Cerebras BlogArtificial IntelligenceBusinessSource published2026-11-05 08:00 SGT
Official releaseStage not established

According to Cerebras Blog: Overcoming Thermal Challenges Building wafer-scale computer processors has historically been considered nearly impossible because silicon and circuit boards expand at different rates when heated, threatening to crack the hardware. Cerebras spent a decade solving this mechanical hurdle. Their patented assembly avoids abrupt temperature changes by smoothing current swings, circulating chilled water evenly, and using flexible electrical connections that move naturally as temperatures fluctuate. Debunking the Lifespan Claims The short seller report relied on the Arrhenius equation and GPU economic lifespan comparisons to argue a two-year hardware failure rate. However, Feldman pointed out that the report used incorrect inputs for the equation. TSMC qualifies Cerebras wafers for ten years of continuous operation at high temperatures, while real-world operational temperatures average below 65 degrees Celsius. This indicates a physical lifespan measured in decades rather than years, mirroring standard silicon behavior where chips become economically obsolete before they physically wear out.
