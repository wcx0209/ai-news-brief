Why India and Pakistan urgently need to rewrite their historic water treaty

The historic Indus Waters Treaty between India and Pakistan is facing unprecedented suspension following regional conflicts. Experts argue that the decades-old agreement has systemic flaws regarding environmental and social sustainability. Revising the treaty using modern international water laws and sustainable development goals could help reduce regional tensions and pave the way for lasting peace between the two nuclear-armed neighbors.
