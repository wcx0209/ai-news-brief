EngineAI Open-Sources Robot Brains and Muscles with a 1 Billion Yuan Ecosystem Fund

QbitAI 量子位
Source published 2026-11-27 22:07 SGT
Media report
AI

At its 2026 developer conference, EngineAI announced the open-sourcing of its core brain and cerebellum technologies to support global developers. The company reported that increasing human motion data from 5,000 to 50,000 hours boosted its embodied foundation model's task success rate from 56.7% to 90%. Additionally, EngineAI introduced a 1 billion yuan industry support fund and a CoRun cross-team collaboration mechanism to accelerate commercialization and multi-industry applications.

Expand summary

Background

EngineAI founder and CEO Zhao Tongyang notes that 2027 will mark a critical transition year for humanoid robots moving from mass production to commercial deployment. Because no single company can master the unique demands of thousands of different industries, EngineAI believes that the next phase of growth relies heavily on ecosystem collaboration.

Core Technologies and Open Source

EngineAI has officially opened up its self-developed brain and cerebellum assets. On the brain side, the company shared its human-like brain architecture EngineAI Awaken, along with a 50,000-hour human motion dataset that significantly improved the base model's task success rate. On the cerebellum side, EngineAI released its behavior foundation model EngineAI-BFM, covering agile and general motor control across various robot form factors.

Ecosystem Support and CoRun Mechanism

To help developers overcome the steep barriers to entry in robotics, EngineAI launched the CoRun collaborative development mechanism, allowing validation results from one team to become the starting point for another. Backed by a 1 billion yuan industry support fund over the next five years, the initiative covers developer resources, academic research, talent cultivation, and commercial partnerships to foster a sustainable network effect.

Goldman Sachs gives Unitree Robotics a sell rating and sees 48% downside

SCMP China Tech
Source published 2026-11-27 22:15 SGT
Media report

Goldman Sachs has initiated coverage of Unitree Robotics with a sell rating, warning that the humanoid robot maker's valuation may have run ahead of its ability to turn hardware leads into lasting industrial profits. While the bank praised its motion control and revenue growth, it noted risks from rising competition and unproven factory adoption.

Expand summary

Valuation Concerns

Goldman Sachs set a 12-month price target of 347.10 yuan, about 48 per cent below Unitree’s closing share price. The bank stated that the current valuation assumes the company will remain a top global player with over 15 per cent shipment share and high margins, which may be difficult as market demand shifts toward practical industrial applications.

Market Position and Financials

According to research firm Smart Analytics Global, Unitree ranked third globally in third-quarter humanoid shipments with 27 per cent of the market. Unitree's revenue rose 55.7 per cent in the first nine months of 2026, driven by academic and research demand, though net profit fell 17.4 per cent in the third quarter.

Future Outlook and AI Investment

Unitree is heavily investing in embodied AI, with R&D spending jumping nearly 200 per cent. However, Goldman cautioned that the company's proprietary AI capabilities are less differentiated than leading peers, and predicted that price competition would gradually reduce its profit margins over the next decade.

Why China's Embodied AI Data Gold Rush Struggles to Take Off

TMTPost 钛媒体
Source published 2026-11-26 09:34 SGT
Media report
AI

Training robots requires massive real-world data, sparking a global data collection gold rush. While the US has built a clear supply chain and independent financing model for data providers, China's data collection mostly relies on local government projects and internal tech incubation, lacking independent valuation and facing high contract compliance risks.

Expand summary

As embodied AI models rapidly evolve, training robots requires millions of hours of real-world video and physical interaction data. In the United States, startups like Maxinsights and Mecka have secured major funding rounds and achieved massive revenue growth because tech giants and VCs view data collection as a distinct, critical supply chain layer for physical AI. Meanwhile, Chinese data collection companies face three major hurdles: the inability to independently validate data value, heavy compliance and payment collection risks, and a lack of independent capital exit pathways. Although China's national data agency initiated national standards for embodied AI data to unify metrics, the domestic industry remains split between internal tech incubation and a fragmented market.

Whole-Body Mobile Manipulation with ROS 2: Integrating MoveIt2, PINK, and Controller Chaining

Clearpath Robotics
Source published 2026-11-26 23:21 SGT
Official release

According to Clearpath Robotics: At ROSCon 2026, engineers presented a practical approach to achieve whole-body control for mobile manipulators using ROS 2. By combining MoveIt2 and the Python Inverse Kinematics solver PINK, robots can coordinate their mobile base and robotic arm simultaneously, bridging the gap between trajectory planning and velocity execution.

Expand summary

According to Clearpath Robotics:

Background and Motivation

Traditionally, mobile manipulators control their mobile base and robotic arm independently using separate frameworks like Nav2 and MoveIt2. At ROSCon 2026, Stephanie Eng and Luis Camero demonstrated a whole-body planning approach that treats the base and arm as a single system. This allows robots to reach targets more efficiently and execute coordinated motions across all available degrees of freedom.

Technical Implementation

To overcome the limitation of MoveIt2's default KDL solver, which cannot handle multi-degree-of-freedom planar joints, the team integrated PINK (Python Inverse Kinematics) via a ROS service interface. For execution, they leveraged ROS 2 controller chaining to translate joint trajectories from MoveIt into velocity commands for the mobile base, ensuring smooth operation across coordinate frames.

Requirements and Availability

While this method enables collision-free whole-body motion, it requires accurate 3D collision models of the environment rather than standard 2D LiDAR mapping. Full details and simulation examples are available in the open-source clearpath-community-demos repository.
