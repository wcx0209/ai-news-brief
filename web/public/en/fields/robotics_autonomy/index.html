Media reportStage not established

AI agents are great at demos, but they frequently fail in real-world corporate environments because they can't handle unexpected scenarios. A startup called Plurai has raised $13 million to fix this problem by applying autonomous driving simulation techniques to business software. By creating a digital twin of a company's systems, Plurai helps train smaller, open-weight models that perform better and cost significantly less than big frontier models.
