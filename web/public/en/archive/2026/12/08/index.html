NIST to Host Second Semiconductor Traceability and Provenance Workshop

NIST Manufacturing ↗Source published 2026-12-08 21:30 SGTOfficial releaseAdvanced Manufacturing & Industrial Infrastructure

According to NIST Manufacturing: NIST is organizing a targeted workshop on December 8 to develop industry-wide guidance for semiconductor traceability. The event aims to define practical data elements and shareable evidence to secure and verify the complex chip supply chain from design to deployment.
