NIST to Host Second Semiconductor Traceability and Provenance Workshop

NIST ManufacturingAdvanced Manufacturing & Industrial InfrastructurePolicySource published2026-12-08 21:30 SGT
Official releaseStage not established

According to NIST Manufacturing:

The semiconductor supply chain is notoriously complex, spanning design, fabrication, assembly, and deployment across the globe. To ensure products are authentic and secure, NIST is collaborating with industry leaders, government agencies, and academia to build standardized frameworks for tracking chips.

Building upon previous workshops held in 2024, 2025, and early 2026, the upcoming December 8 workshop moves past identifying high-level needs into actionable implementation. Participants will pinpoint specific data elements, artifacts, and concepts needed to establish reliable provenance without compromising sensitive proprietary information.
