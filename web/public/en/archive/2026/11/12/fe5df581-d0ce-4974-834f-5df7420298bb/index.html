Zimbabwean Author Cleared of Using AI for Award-Winning Story

Following a month-long investigation, the prestigious Caine Prize concluded that Zimbabwean author Simbarashe Kundizeza's winning short story was human-written. The inquiry was launched after an article questioned the story's authenticity based on AI-detection software, prompting widespread debate over fairness in literary awards.

The Guardian AI ↗Source published 2026-11-12 21:14 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

Investigation Details

Simbarashe Kundizeza won the £10,000 Caine Prize for his short story *The Blue Fighter*. Shortly after, *The Atlantic* published an article suggesting the story was artificially generated, citing the Pangram detection tool.

Resolution and Impact

In response, the prize organizers conducted a thorough review, including interviews and metadata analysis of drafts. Chair Ellah Wakatama confirmed the story is human-written. The incident also sparked online outrage regarding whether minority writers face unfair algorithmic scrutiny.
