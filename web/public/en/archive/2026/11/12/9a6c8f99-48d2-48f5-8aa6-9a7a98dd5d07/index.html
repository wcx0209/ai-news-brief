Is Claude Fable 5.2 worth it for office workers? Comparing it with Opus and Sonnet

Anthropic's top-tier AI model Claude Fable 5.2 was tested against Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5 on tasks like summarizing lengthy government reports and preparing presentation slides. While Fable 5.2 excels at understanding user intent and crafting user-friendly slides, it is significantly more expensive and time-consuming, making Opus and Sonnet more practical choices for everyday tasks.

ITmedia AI+ ↗Source published 2026-11-12 07:00 SGTNews summaryArtificial Intelligence

The article evaluates Anthropic's new flagship AI model, Claude Fable 5.2, against Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5 across two typical office tasks: summarizing a 268-page government white paper into a 15-minute presentation, and conducting background research for a media interview.

In the presentation-making task, Fable 5.2 followed the time constraint best, structuring the slides neatly with well-thought-out actionable takeaways and speaker notes. However, it took about 1.6 times longer and cost roughly twice as much as Opus 5.5, and 3.6 times as much as Sonnet 5.5. In the research task, all three models performed similarly well in grasping key contexts, though Fable 5.2 provided deeper localized insights at a higher cost.

Third-party benchmarks indicate that Fable 5.2 leads in general intelligence but slightly trails in specific knowledge-work tasks like briefcase handling. Ultimately, while Fable 5.2 offers a more human-like output and better intent-matching for creative presentation structuring, its high cost means office workers should primarily rely on Opus 5.5 or Sonnet 5.5 for daily routines, reserving Fable 5.2 for high-stakes, heavy-duty information processing.
