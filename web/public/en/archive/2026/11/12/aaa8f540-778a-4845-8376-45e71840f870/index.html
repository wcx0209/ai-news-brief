Alnylam faces pressure to solve its heart drug challenges

STAT BiotechBiotech & Programmable LifeBusinessSource published2026-11-12 19:30 SGT
Media reportStage not established

In his newsletter column, biotech writer Adam Feuerstein reflects on a previous comparison between Alnylam and Vertex from August 2025. While Alnylam showed rapid growth driven by its cardiomyopathy treatment Amvuttra, aiming for a market value comparable to Vertex's $100 billion, Feuerstein points out that the company now faces critical hurdles. Specifically, Alnylam needs to navigate its heart drug issues to justify its ambitious valuation and maintain investor confidence in a competitive biotech market.
