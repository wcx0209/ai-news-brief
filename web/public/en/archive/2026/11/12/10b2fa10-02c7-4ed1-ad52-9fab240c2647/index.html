Ex-employee sues Eli Lilly over alleged off-label marketing of weight loss drug

According to the lawsuit, the former sales representative alleges that Eli Lilly pushed its diabetes drug Mounjaro for weight loss purposes prior to proper regulatory clearance. The worker claims she was terminated after raising internal concerns about this practice. This case brings fresh attention to the intense commercial pressures and regulatory boundaries surrounding the rapidly expanding market for GLP-1 and obesity medications.
