Elon Musk and 165,000 others sign open letter asking Nvidia to bring back Jensen Huang

Elon Musk and over 165,000 others have signed an open letter calling for Jensen Huang to be reinstated as Nvidia's CEO. Huang recently resigned following reports of extramarital affairs, including one with a woman accused of spying for China.

The Guardian AI ↗Source published 2026-11-16 19:30 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

Following Jensen Huang's resignation after a 33-year tenure at Nvidia due to a personal conduct scandal, a massive pushback has emerged from Silicon Valley. An open letter demanding his reinstatement has gathered over 165,000 signatures, including prominent figures like Elon Musk, Epic Games CEO Tim Sweeney, and Y Combinator president Garry Tan. Supporters argue that while Huang made personal mistakes, his contributions to the company and the broader AI industry are irreplaceable.

However, Washington is taking a starkly different stance. US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent stated that Nvidia needs a dramatic shake-up and should look outside the company for its next leader. Bessent also raised serious national security concerns, demanding answers within 10 days regarding whether Nvidia knowingly allowed its most advanced AI chips to reach China, pointing to Megaspeed, a Singapore-based company that bought nearly $5 billion in hardware and is linked to the scandal.

Meanwhile, analysts remain skeptical about a potential comeback. Dan Ives of Yorkville Ives described the petition as a distraction, noting that external candidates or interim CEO Colette Kress are much more viable options for moving forward. Huang has reportedly agreed to appear before US congressional committees to address these mounting questions.
