European Commission hosts Apply AI Summit to boost AI innovation

According to European Commission Digital Strategy: According to the European Commission, nearly 1,000 policymakers, startup founders, and industry leaders are gathering in Brussels for the Apply AI Summit.

European Commission Digital Strategy ↗Source published 2026-11-16 20:43 SGTOfficial releaseArtificial Intelligence

According to European Commission Digital Strategy:

According to the European Commission, the Apply AI Summit in Brussels brings together nearly 1,000 in-person participants, including policymakers, startup founders, industry leaders, and researchers, with another 2,500 joining via an online platform from November 16 to 20. Summit Goals and Context The summit focuses on discussing how artificial intelligence can strengthen Europe's competitiveness across 10 strategic sectors as well as the public sector. It showcases concrete European success stories and innovative projects. Henna Virkkunen, Executive Vice-President for Technological Sovereignty, Security and Democracy, is hosting the event and presenting the inaugural Apply AI Startup Award to three European startups developing solutions in strategic areas. Broader Policy Milestones This event marks the one-year anniversary of the Apply AI Strategy, which was adopted to accelerate technology adoption in key European industries and public services. It also concludes the first European AI Innovation Month, which ran from October 14 to November 17, 2026, organized by the European Commission in partnership with the Irish Presidency of the Council of the European Union.
