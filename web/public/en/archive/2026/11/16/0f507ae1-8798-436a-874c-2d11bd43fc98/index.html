Qatar Partners with SignQuantum and QANplatform to Test Quantum-Resistant Public-Sector Security

Qatar's Ministry of Communications and Information Technology has signed a collaboration agreement with SignQuantum and QANplatform to evaluate post-quantum cryptography workflows for protecting government data. Announced at MWC26 Doha, the initiative pairs quantum-resistant document signing with immutable ledger technology to help safeguard public records against future quantum threats.

Quantum Computing Report ↗Source published 2026-11-16 08:39 SGTMedia reportQuantum Technology

Background and Partnership Scope

Qatar's Ministry of Communications and Information Technology has entered into an agreement with local cybersecurity firm SignQuantum and quantum-resistant blockchain developer QANplatform to assess post-quantum cryptography for public-sector data. The collaboration was unveiled on November 10, 2026, at MWC26 Doha, aligning with the long-term goals outlined in the Qatar National Vision 2030 and Digital Agenda 2030.

Technical Mechanism and Roles

SignQuantum, a provider of self-hosted enterprise trust software, will supply PQC document signing and business continuity tools designed for authenticated digital records. Meanwhile, QANplatform will provide tamper-evident time-stamping using its distributed ledger to verify signature longevity across multi-decade lifecycles. According to QANplatform CTO Johann Polecsak, combining these technologies mitigates future key-compromise risks for government records.

Future Outlook

The agreement involves technical knowledge sharing and workflow assessments directed by officials including MCIT Digital Economy Department Director Faraj Jassim Abdulla and SignQuantum CEO Nazmath Nazeer. However, the parties noted that any future pilot deployments or commercial contracts remain subject to separate government evaluation and procurement approvals.
