RealMan Robotics CEO: Quality, Economy, and Stability Are the Three Core Standards for Robot Deployment

At the 2026 Changzhou Industrial Embodied AI Innovation Conference, RealMan Robotics CEO Zheng Suibing stated that the key challenge for robots is transitioning from demonstrations to performing stable, continuous work in real industrial scenarios.

QbitAI 量子位 ↗Source published 2026-11-16 18:57 SGTMedia reportRobotics & Autonomous Systems

During the roundtable dialogue at the Changzhou Industrial Embodied AI Innovation Conference, RealMan Robotics CEO Zheng Suibing shared insights on the current industry bottleneck of having many demonstrations but few factory adoptions. Drawing from the company's practical experience since 2018, Zheng noted that robots are ultimately a form of labor, and success depends on working continuously, steadily, and economically in real environments.

He outlined three core dimensions for deployment: quality determines if a product can be used, economy expands the application boundaries, and long-term stability ensures tasks can be performed reliably over days, months, and years. Furthermore, RealMan signed an agreement with Ant (Changzhou) Precision Machinery during the event, deploying RealBOT robots into actual production lines for parts handling and loading, creating a closed-loop system of real work and data iteration.
