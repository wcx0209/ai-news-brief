Are Your Data Center Facilities Ready for the AI Era?

According to Siemens Blog: IDC reports that the rapid rise of AI workloads is exposing major gaps in traditional data center facilities management. According to IDC, operators utilizing integrated software tools experience fewer outages and faster problem resolution.

Siemens Blog ↗Source published 2026-11-16 18:58 SGTOfficial releaseAdvanced Manufacturing & Industrial Infrastructure
Artificial Intelligence

According to Siemens Blog:

According to IDC research, data center facilities management software has historically been an overlooked operational area, with many operators failing to monitor power and cooling assets with automated sensors. The rapid adoption of AI workloads has transformed this gap into an urgent operational challenge. IDC notes that operators using automated, well-integrated management tools experience fewer unplanned outages and spend less time firefighting.

Supporting specialized AI hardware requires integrating entirely new systems, including dedicated management for high-density liquid cooling and comprehensive power systems. Consequently, buyer evaluation criteria are shifting. Vendors are now assessed on whether their cooling and power management are purpose-built for high density rather than retrofitted, how effectively they converge IT and operational technology under a unified security model, and whether their sustainability commitments are measurable and independently certified.

The vendor ecosystem is also evolving through mergers and acquisitions, spanning traditional hardware companies, pure-play infrastructure specialists, and liquid-cooling firms. In the IDC MarketScape 2026 vendor assessment, Siemens has been recognized as a Leader for its capabilities and strategy in this fast-evolving market.
