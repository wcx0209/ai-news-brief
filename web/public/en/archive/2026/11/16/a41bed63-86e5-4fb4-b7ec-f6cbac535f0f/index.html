Cohere introduces Video Intelligence in Compass for enterprise video search

According to Cohere Blog: Cohere has introduced Video Intelligence in its Compass platform, allowing AI applications to search through video collections and retrieve precise moments alongside traditional documents. According to Cohere, the tool understands both audio and visual content, returning timestamped clips rather than lengthy recordings.

Cohere Blog ↗Source published 2026-11-16 23:32 SGTOfficial releaseArtificial Intelligence

According to Cohere Blog:

Background

Enterprises store vast amounts of information in recorded meetings, training sessions, and media archives, but videos have traditionally been difficult for AI applications to search effectively compared to text documents. Details Cohere's new Video Intelligence feature in Compass analyzes both spoken words and on-screen visuals. Instead of matching keywords in transcripts alone, it determines exact start and end times to answer user queries with precise clips, transcripts, and visual descriptions. It integrates video into the same retrieval system as documents, featuring permission-aware access control. According to Cohere's evaluations across 11 datasets, Compass ranks first on average with an nDCG@10 score 4.4 points ahead of alternative models. Impact The tool aims to unlock video data for enterprise retrieval-augmented generation and agentic workflows, with applications spanning recorded meetings, education, sports archives, and compliance review.
