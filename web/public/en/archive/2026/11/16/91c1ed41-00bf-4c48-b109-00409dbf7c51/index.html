Materialise Calls for Coordinated Defence Additive Manufacturing Network in Europe

At the Formnext 2026 summit, 3D printing company Materialise urged European defence manufacturers and policymakers to build a coordinated cross-border additive manufacturing network.

TCT Magazine ↗Source published 2026-11-16 21:07 SGTMedia reportAdvanced Manufacturing & Industrial Infrastructure

Speaking at the AM Innovation and Standards Summit at Formnext 2026, Materialise CTO Bart Van der Schueren argued that Europe's existing additive manufacturing expertise and industrial capacity are currently underutilized as a cohesive defence capability.

Instead of treating 3D printing merely as a quick fix for isolated spare parts or emergency supply chain disruptions, the company suggests that the defence sector should recognize it as a flexible and scalable production capacity. Achieving this vision requires European governments and institutions to establish common frameworks across companies and national borders.

Materialise stresses that shared approaches to standards, qualification, security, digital production processes, and the harmonization of export control regulations are essential prerequisites for connecting individual production resources into a reliable, distributed manufacturing network.
