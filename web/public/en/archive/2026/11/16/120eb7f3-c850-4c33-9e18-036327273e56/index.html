China plans 50-60 GW of new energy storage for renewable bases by 2030

China's National Development and Reform Commission and National Energy Administration released the implementation plan for new energy storage during the 15th Five-Year Plan period. The plan states that by 2030, national new energy storage capacity will reach 300 million kilowatts, with about 50 to 60 million kilowatts allocated to large renewable energy bases.

China Energy Network Storage 中国能源网 ↗Source published 2026-11-16 09:42 SGTMedia reportEnergy & Storage

According to the National Development and Reform Commission and the National Energy Administration, the newly issued implementation plan for new energy storage during the 15th Five-Year Plan period outlines the strategic transition of new energy storage from scaled development to market-driven growth by 2030, targeting an average storage duration of about 3 hours.

Key deployments include allocating around 50 to 60 million kilowatts of new energy storage to large renewable energy bases and about 140 million kilowatts of grid-side independent storage capable of peak power supply. The plan also encourages integrated participation in electricity market trading and promotes the local consumption of distributed renewable energy sources.
