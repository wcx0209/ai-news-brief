People and Vertical AI: The new operating model for energy and utilities

According to SEW.AI, the energy and utility industry is facing unprecedented complexity due to electrification, renewable energy, and grid demands. The solution is not generic AI, but Vertical AI working alongside human experts. By connecting data across customers, workforces, businesses, and the grid, this approach aims to scale human judgment rather than replace it.

Artificial Intelligence

As outlined by SEW.AI, the traditional energy and utility sector relies heavily on human expertise, but growing operational complexity—driven by distributed energy resources, electric vehicles, extreme weather, and rising customer expectations—demands a shift. According to recent research from SEW.AI based on work across 470+ organizations globally, the industry is moving from isolated digital tools to connected intelligence.

Connected Intelligence Across Four Dimensions

SEW.AI emphasizes that Vertical AI is designed specifically for the industry's realities, rather than being a generic model. This model brings together four key dimensions: Customer, Workforce, Business, and Grid. By connecting these separate systems, utilities can give AI and humans a shared context to make better, more reliable decisions.

Real-World Applications

Case studies from major Canadian and U.S. utilities demonstrate this framework in action. By unifying customer experience, smart EV charging, and energy management, these organizations are streamlining the energy transition while strengthening trust and engagement with millions of customers. SEW.AI offers its COSMOS platform as a native solution to support this operating model.
