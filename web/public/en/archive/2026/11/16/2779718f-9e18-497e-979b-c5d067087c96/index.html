APEC CEOs urge common AI safeguards to boost trade

Deloitte's Asia-Pacific CEO Robert Hillard is urging regional governments to agree on common AI rules and cybersecurity standards as companies accelerate adoption. A recent Deloitte survey shows that cybersecurity and risk frameworks are the top priority for regional cooperation, with Chinese enterprises leading the adoption curve in embedding AI into core workflows.

SCMP China Tech ↗Source published 2026-11-16 20:00 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

Ahead of the Apec CEO summit in Shenzhen, Deloitte released a report based on a survey of 1,278 executives across 18 Apec economies and India. According to Deloitte's Asia-Pacific CEO Robert Hillard, cross-border coordination is vital because over 70 percent of global trade originates, ends, or takes place within the region.

The survey highlights that about half of the respondents view cybersecurity and risk frameworks as the top priority for regional cooperation, while 44 percent call for collaborative investment in digital infrastructure. Furthermore, Deloitte China CEO Dora Liu noted that other economies can learn from Shenzhen by integrating AI with real-world industrial capabilities.

The report also shows that more than half of surveyed executives rank AI as their top priority, surpassing revenue growth. Chinese enterprises lead this adoption curve, with 78 percent deploying AI across multiple functions, driven largely by the integration of physical AI into the country's manufacturing sector.
