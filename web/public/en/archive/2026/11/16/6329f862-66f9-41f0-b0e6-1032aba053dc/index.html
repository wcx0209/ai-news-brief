Tech News Roundup: Nvidia, Apple, OpenAI, and SpaceX Updates

This week's major tech updates include Jensen Huang reportedly considering a return to Nvidia, Apple planning its first smart glasses for 2027, and OpenAI dissolving its independent robotics team to integrate researchers into core departments.

Robotics & Autonomous Systems

Nvidia and Apple Updates According to Financial Times, allies of Jensen Huang are pressuring Nvidia's board to have him return as CEO, though he lacks a controlling stake. Meanwhile, Bloomberg reports Apple plans to release its first AI smart glasses in June 2027, codenamed N50, acting as an AI assistant driven by Siri rather than a standalone AR display. OpenAI and Huawei Moves OpenAI confirmed it has dissolved its robotics research team, reallocating staff to core research and applied divisions to accelerate R-development, despite active hiring earlier this year. Concurrently, Huawei announced it will release its first Ascend-native embodied world model, CloudRoboWM1.2, aiming to scale embodied AI across industries.
