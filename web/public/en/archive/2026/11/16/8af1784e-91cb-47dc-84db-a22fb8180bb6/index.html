SAP and Infosys Executives Discuss Scaling AI Agents Across the Enterprise

As companies move AI pilots into actual business workflows, leaders from SAP and Infosys emphasize that AI must be treated as a core enterprise platform rather than a disconnected project. They highlight that a solid data foundation, clear impact metrics, and robust governance are essential to prevent failure and scale autonomous agents successfully.

MIT Technology Review AI ↗Source published 2026-11-16 21:00 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

Enterprise AI is shifting from isolated proofs of concept to autonomous agents running core business processes. However, industry data shows that the vast majority of organizations remain stuck in pilot mode. According to Thorsten Leiduck of SAP and Satinder Pal Singh of Infosys, this bottleneck stems from treating AI as a department-level technology initiative rather than an operating model transformation.

To bridge this gap, executives recommend evaluating use cases through three lenses: impact, feasibility, and scalability. Furthermore, companies must build a connected, semantically rich data layer to ensure high accuracy in mission-critical operations like supply chains and financial closes. Finally, integrated governance tools—such as SAP's Agent Hub—are required to maintain trust, security, and oversight as digital coworkers take on greater responsibilities.
