Helus' Psychedelic Depression Drug Succeeds in Phase 3 Trial But Faces Safety Scrutiny

According to Helus Pharma, its psychedelic drug candidate HLP003 significantly reduced major depression symptoms in a Phase 3 clinical trial. However, the study also recorded a number of serious adverse events, casting doubt on the overall safety of the treatment.

STAT Biotech ↗Source published 2026-11-16 21:07 SGTMedia reportBiotech & Programmable Life

Helus Pharma announced that its psychedelic drug candidate, HLP003, showed positive results in a Phase 3 clinical trial for major depression. In the study, participants received one dose on the first day and a second dose three weeks later alongside standard antidepressants. At six weeks, the treatment group achieved a 19.5-point reduction on the MADRS depression rating scale, compared to an 11.9-point reduction in the placebo group. At 12 weeks, the effect was largely sustained, with a 16.9-point reduction for the treatment group versus 9.9 points for the placebo group, with all differences being statistically significant.

Despite the promising efficacy data, the trial recorded a number of serious adverse events. These safety concerns have raised questions regarding the risk-benefit profile of utilizing psychedelics for psychiatric conditions, a therapeutic avenue currently being explored by multiple companies in the biotechnology sector.
