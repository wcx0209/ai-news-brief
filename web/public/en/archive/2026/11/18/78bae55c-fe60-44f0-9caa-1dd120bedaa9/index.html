OpenAI announces GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol with lower costs and better performance

According to OpenAI News: OpenAI reports the release of GPT-6.2 Astra for complex professional tasks and GPT-6.2 Sol for everyday work. According to OpenAI, both models deliver better results with fewer tokens, lowering API prices for Astra while maintaining Sol's pricing.

OpenAI News ↗Source published 2026-11-18 01:45 SGTOfficial releaseArtificial Intelligence

According to OpenAI News:

OpenAI reports the launch of GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol, designed to improve efficiency and performance across professional and everyday workflows. According to the company, GPT-6.2 Astra sets a new quality bar for complex professional work, achieving higher scores on benchmarks like Agents' Last Exam and Terminal-Bench-Science 0.1 at a fraction of the cost of previous versions. OpenAI states that Astra features lower input and output token prices in the API alongside a 60% discount on cached input.

For everyday use, GPT-6.2 Sol is presented as a smarter and more efficient alternative to GPT-6.1 Sol, performing well on coding and everyday life evaluations while maintaining the same API token prices. Both models include updated computer use capabilities, enabling applications in CAD, 3D design, and mechanical simulation.

In terms of safety, OpenAI reports that both models meet critical cybersecurity thresholds and high biological capability thresholds under their preparedness framework. Consequently, the company is implementing tighter safeguards and specialized access programs, such as Daybreak, to manage dual-use risks.
