Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella says he is now AGI-pilled

Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella stated that he now believes AGI is coming, particularly regarding its potential to reshape the political economy and disrupt traditional company structures. However, he remains skeptical about AI consciousness, agreeing that companies shouldn't treat models as if they have moral status.

The Verge AI ↗Source published 2026-11-18 07:41 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

In a roundtable discussion following his Microsoft Ignite keynote, Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella shared his evolved perspective on artificial general intelligence. While he admitted to being 'AGI-pilled' in terms of how advanced AI could reorganize the global economy, alter political structures, and collapse human capital into token capital, he pushed back against the idea that current frontier models are conscious. Discussing the broader market implications, Nadella warned against viewing the AI race as a narrow competition between tech firms over market cap. Instead, he argued that if intelligence exhibits network effects and models can learn to operate across healthcare, finance, or government, it threatens the traditional definition of a firm and the stability of democratic labor markets. To counter a potential monopoly scenario where a single AI model dominates everything, Nadella emphasized Microsoft's enterprise strategy: providing customers with the tools to choose cost-effective models, manage their own data, and maintain sovereign control to ensure market diversity.
