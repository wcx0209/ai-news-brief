Xihe Additive showcases green laser 3D printing for copper cold plates at Formnext 2026

At Formnext 2026, Xihe Additive showcased its upgraded M350 series and batch-manufactured copper cold plates using green laser 3D printing. This technology improves copper absorption from 5% to about 40%, addressing the heat dissipation demands of AI chips and optical modules.

Xihe Additive, backed by its parent company's in-house green fiber laser technology, presented its industrialization progress at Formnext 2026 in Frankfurt. The company's upgraded M350G system features a 532nm single-mode continuous green laser with 500W or 1000W options, designed for high-reflectivity and difficult-to-fuse metals. According to previous company disclosures cited in the text, printed pure copper parts can achieve a density of up to 99.9% and an electrical conductivity of 101.5% IACS.

Batch Manufacturing Shift

Industry reports indicate that green laser 3D printing is transitioning from small samples to scalable mass production driven by optical modules, commercial aerospace, and humanoid robotics. Xihe's latest hardware adjustments focus on compact footprints to fit standard production lines, shifting the competitive edge from raw wavelength advantages to reliable mass production systems.
