Major Clean Energy Deals Announced at RE+ Trade Show

During the RE+ trade show in Las Vegas, major clean energy companies announced massive battery storage deals and acquisitions. Samsung SDI secured a 16GWh supply agreement with Jupiter Power, EQT acquired BESS developer Flatiron Energy, and Canadian Solar's e-Storage sealed a 1.43GWh deal with X-Elio.

Energy-Storage.news ↗Source published 2026-11-18 22:53 SGTMedia reportEnergy & Storage

As the RE+ clean energy trade show unfolds in Las Vegas, several significant transactions have reshaped the battery energy storage system (BESS) landscape. Samsung SDI's Massive LFP Deal Samsung SDI announced a multi-year, 16GWh lithium iron phosphate battery supply agreement with Jupiter Power, running for three years from 2029. The batteries will be manufactured in Indiana, leveraging Samsung SDI's domestic production lines. This brings Jupiter Power's total booked orders with Samsung SDI to approximately 27GWh. EQT Acquires Flatiron Energy Private equity firm EQT's energy transition infrastructure arm reached an agreement to acquire US utility-scale BESS developer Flatiron Energy from Hull Street Energy. Flatiron boasts a 24GWh total pipeline across the Northeast and Mid-Atlantic US, reinforcing EQT's growing global footprint in energy storage alongside its previous investments in Statera and Ju:niz Energy. Canadian Solar Expands Storage Footprint Canadian Solar's BESS subsidiary, e-Storage, signed a 590MW/1,430MWh deal with global developer X-Elio to supply systems for five projects in the US and Australia. The agreement includes 22-year long-term service agreements, underscoring Canadian Solar's strategic pivot as its storage shipments surge while traditional photovoltaic module shipments decline.
