IQM Launches Upgrade Plan to Help Quantum Systems Reach Over 1,000 Qubits

IQM has introduced an upgrade programme for its Halocene error-correction product line, allowing customers to transition from current 150-qubit systems to systems exceeding 1,000 qubits. The initiative aims to lower the risk of early adoption by enabling seamless, downtime-free upgrades while integrating with existing AI and high-performance computing infrastructure.

The Quantum Insider ↗Source published 2026-11-18 03:34 SGTMedia reportQuantum Technology

Upgrade Path to 1,000 Qubits IQM announced an upgrade programme for its Halocene quantum product line, designed to take users from a 150-qubit baseline all the way to over 1,000 qubits. This program allows research centers and enterprises to run hybrid workloads combining classical AI and high-performance computing alongside quantum processors without interrupting their daily operations. Seamless Integration and Continuity A major concern for quantum computing adopters is hardware obsolescence. To mitigate this, IQM installs new Halocene hardware while the existing system stays active, removing the older unit only after the new one is fully operational. This approach builds on IQM's past deployments with organizations like VTT and the Leibniz Supercomputing Centre, ensuring that software and expertise carry forward smoothly across generations.
