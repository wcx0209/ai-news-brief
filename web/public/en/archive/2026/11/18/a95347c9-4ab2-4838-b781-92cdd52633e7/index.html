World's First Halal-Certified Cultivated Meat Launches in the US

According to Good Food Institute APAC: UPSIDE Foods has launched the world's first halal-certified cultivated chicken product in the U.S. after evaluation by Circle H International. This milestone is crucial because over a billion people worldwide follow halal dietary standards, opening up vital pathways for cultivated meat to enter broader markets.

Good Food Institute APAC ↗Source published 2026-11-18 08:49 SGTOfficial releaseAgriculture & Food Technology

According to Good Food Institute APAC:

Following fatwas from Islamic authorities in Singapore, South Korea, and Malaysia recognizing cultivated meat under certain conditions, UPSIDE Foods has secured the first official halal certification for a cultivated meat product. Circle H International, whose standards are recognized globally by major institutions including the Organization of Islamic Cooperation, completed a year-long evaluation of the product.

To meet market demands, UPSIDE's new formulation combines cultivated chicken with plant-based ingredients like soy protein isolate and pea fibre, with cultivated meat making up a double-digit percentage of the final product. The company reports that its revamped production system can produce over 1 million pounds of chicken annually on less than half an acre of land. Early consumer reviews at participating restaurants like Los Angeles' Chimmelier described the product as crispy and delicious.
