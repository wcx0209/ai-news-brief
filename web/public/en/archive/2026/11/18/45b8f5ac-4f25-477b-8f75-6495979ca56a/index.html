Jimeng Seedance 2.5 Major Update Brings Native 4K Pipeline to AI Video Generation

According to ifanr, Jimeng Seedance 2.5 has introduced a 4K version, bringing up to 30-second single-generation capabilities, full-modal references, and video editing into higher-resolution standards.

iFanr 爱范儿 ↗Source published 2026-11-18 12:00 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

According to fanr's evaluation, Jimeng Seedance 2.5's 4K upgrade addresses major pain points in commercial video production, such as brand TVCs, high-ticket e-commerce displays, and offline large-screen exhibitions. The model supports 10bit BT.709 color format, offering finer color gradations and wider color adjustment room for post-production compared to standard 8bit videos. It also accommodates six different aspect ratios, allowing teams to generate vertical, horizontal, square, and ultra-wide materials from a single creative concept.

In addition to resolution, the update integrates with other Jimeng tools like Seedream 5.5 and Seedance 2.5 Flash, forming a connected production workflow from asset preparation to video generation and editing. Furthermore, Jimeng has launched an enterprise solution aimed at helping corporate teams integrate AI content generation into their daily production pipelines.
