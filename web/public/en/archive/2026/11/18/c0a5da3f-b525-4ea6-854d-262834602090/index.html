Microsoft and Salesforce Team Up to Launch Copilotforce

According to Microsoft AI: Microsoft and Salesforce have announced Copilotforce, a major partnership integrating Salesforce customer data with Microsoft Copilot and Teams. This collaboration allows enterprise sales teams to use CRM insights directly inside their everyday productivity tools while keeping existing security rules intact.

Microsoft AI ↗Source published 2026-11-18 02:10 SGTOfficial releaseArtificial Intelligence

According to Microsoft AI:

Microsoft and Salesforce report they are partnering to introduce Copilotforce at Microsoft Ignite, connecting Salesforce customer context directly into Microsoft 365 experiences like Teams and Copilot. According to the companies, the integration features Salesforce in Copilot, a public preview plugin providing over 38 prebuilt sales skills designed to help employees manage pipeline reviews, risk assessments, and follow-up drafts without constantly switching applications.

Additionally, the partnership establishes a two-way Zero Copy connection between Salesforce Data 360 and Microsoft Fabric. This technical link lets organizations query and analyze data across both platforms without duplicating datasets or building cumbersome pipelines. Accenture is participating as the design partner, implementation guide, and an initial pilot customer across its global sales organization.
