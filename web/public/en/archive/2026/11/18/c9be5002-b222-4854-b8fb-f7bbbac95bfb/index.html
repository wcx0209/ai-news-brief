Nvidia reports doubled revenue as board rules out returning Jensen Huang

Nvidia reported a quarterly revenue of $115.84bn, doubling from a year ago, during its first earnings report since Jensen Huang's resignation. Despite external calls for his return, board chairman Harvey Jones stated the company does not intend to reinstate Huang, while interim CEO Colette Kress assured investors that business continues as usual.

The Guardian AI ↗Source published 2026-11-18 07:32 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

Nvidia announced that its quarterly revenue reached $115.84bn, beating Wall Street expectations and doubling from the same period last year, alongside earnings of $2.68 per share. The financial update follows the abrupt ouster of co-founder Jensen Huang due to allegations of extramarital affairs, which initially caused the company's stock to tumble and wiped out billions in valuation.

During the earnings call, board chairman Harvey Jones firmly stated that the company does not intend to reinstate Huang as CEO, despite public calls for his return from figures including Donald Trump and Silicon Valley insiders. Huang recently issued a public apology and expressed a willingness to return if asked, but Jones emphasized that Huang voluntarily stepped down and pointed to interim CEO Colette Kress's long tenure.

Alongside the leadership transition, Nvidia faces growing scrutiny, including upcoming transcribed interviews with US House committees and an investigation by the US Securities and Exchange Commission regarding its business in China. Meanwhile, executives reported strong talent commitment and steady financial performance as the company navigates the turbulent leadership shift.
