Brazil Receives Parts for Two Quantum Computers from China

Brazil has received $14.2 million in parts for two quantum computers through a partnership with Chinese tech company CETC. According to the Brazilian Ministry of Science, Technology and Innovation, local researchers will begin assembly under Chinese supervision on November 24, with testing scheduled for January 2027 to build domestic expertise.

The Quantum Insider ↗Source published 2026-11-18 22:24 SGTMedia reportQuantum Technology

According to the Brazilian Ministry of Science, Technology and Innovation, the equipment arrived on November 16 in João Pessoa, valued at about 83 million Brazilian reais, and was transported to the newly established International Center for Computing and Quantum Technologies (Ciquanta). Around 15 Brazilian researchers will work alongside visiting Chinese specialists starting November 24 to handle the physical structure, cooling systems, and software configuration.

The project aims to transfer technology and train local experts in operating the machines and developing quantum algorithms. While testing is expected to begin in January 2027 pending the arrival of essential cooling supplies like helium-3, actual scientific research projects are slated for the first half of 2027, with officials hoping to decentralize research funding and retain regional talent.
