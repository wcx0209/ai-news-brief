Australia's Meliora Foods Launches Plant-Based Liquid Egg Made from Mung Beans

Australian brand Meliora Foods, owned by My Co, has launched a pourable plant-based egg substitute made from locally sourced mung beans. The cholesterol-free product is already on the shelves of Coles supermarkets, priced at A$8.50 per 500ml bottle to compete with conventional eggs.

Green Queen ↗Source published 2026-11-18 18:31 SGTMedia reportAgriculture & Food Technology

Meliora Foods, a subsidiary of investment firm My Co, has rolled out a liquid vegan egg alternative utilizing mung bean protein isolate supplied by its sister company, Australian Plant Proteins. The product is designed to pour, scramble, and bake similarly to conventional eggs and is sold in 500ml bottles equivalent to about 11 eggs, priced at A$8.50.

My Co representatives state that the launch aligns with record-high per capita egg consumption in Australia, though the company emphasizes the product was developed over several years independently of recent supply pressures like avian flu. Following its retail debut at Coles, the company plans to target the commercial foodservice sector and expand its product portfolio over the coming year.
