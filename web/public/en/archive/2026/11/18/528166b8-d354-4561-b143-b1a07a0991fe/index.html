Will Wright’s next game uses AI to map your memories

Will Wright, the legendary creator of *The Sims*, is developing a mobile social simulation game called *MyMems* scheduled for release in 2027. According to Gallium Studios, players can speak short stories about their lives into their phones, and generative AI will turn those memories and personal contacts into a playable, customizable world.

The Verge AI ↗Source published 2026-11-18 22:00 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

Will Wright, creator of *SimCity* and *The Sims*, is working on a mobile social simulation game called *MyMems* through his developer team at Gallium Studios, with a planned launch in 2027. The title allows users to input autobiographical anecdotes by speaking short stories into their mobile devices. Generative AI then processes these narratives to construct interactive environments and populate them with AI-powered avatars representing real-world acquaintances, essentially building a digital map of the player's psyche.

While generative AI faces widespread skepticism and backlash across the gaming industry, including opposition from indie developers and players, the Gallium Studios team believes user reception can remain positive if the technology is applied appropriately. Product manager Anthony Maitz explains that because players continuously author the world piece by piece in a lightweight style, the experience feels organic rather than derivative. Executive producer Virginia McArthur emphasizes that the goal is to create a cozy world builder rather than an automated output generator, following through on the natural tendency players historically showed in *The Sims* to map their own real-life relationships into virtual spaces.
