October EV Penetration Reaches 64.3% with Over 200,000 Battery Enterprises

According to data released by the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, China's new energy vehicle sales accounted for 64.3% of total new car sales in October. Driven by surging demand, the number of active or surviving battery-related enterprises nationwide has surpassed 200,000, as manufacturing precision and cost control become the core of market competition.

China Energy Network Storage 中国能源网 ↗Source published 2026-11-18 08:46 SGTMedia reportEnergy & Storage

Data released by the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology shows that in October, China's new energy vehicle production and sales reached 2.017 million and 1.94 million units respectively, with the monthly penetration rate hitting 64.3%. From January to October, cumulative production and sales completed 14.509 million and 14.411 million units, with the cumulative market share rising to 54.8%.

This robust growth has strongly driven downstream and upstream segments. According to the China Automotive Power Battery Industry Innovation Alliance, domestic power battery installations reached 576.3 GWh from January to September, while storage battery sales hit 595.4 GWh, representing a significant 112% year-on-year increase. Because battery and energy storage manufacturing processes share high compatibility, production lines can flexibly switch between orders, stabilizing capacity utilization.

Market competition is shifting heavily toward manufacturing execution. Factors such as coating thickness precision, stacking alignment accuracy, and temperature control during formation directly dictate cell lifespan and cost efficiency. With over 202,000 battery-related enterprises nationwide tracked by enterprise database Tianyancha, surviving and thriving in this crowded market relies entirely on long-term technological refinement rather than simple capacity expansion.
