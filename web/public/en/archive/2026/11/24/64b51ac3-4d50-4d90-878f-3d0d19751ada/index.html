EVE Energy Debuts All-Scenario Energy Storage Solution for AIDC at RE+ 2026

EVE Energy unveiled its comprehensive AIDC energy storage solution at the RE+ 2026 exhibition in Las Vegas. Driven by the explosive demand for AI computing power, the company launched a three-tier product matrix covering the rack, distribution, and source-grid sides to provide reliable power infrastructure for data centers.

China Energy Network Storage 中国能源网 ↗Source published 2026-11-24 10:57 SGTMedia reportEnergy & Storage

At the RE+ 2026 international solar and energy storage exhibition in Las Vegas, EVE Energy showcased its strategy targeting AI data center power demands. According to the company, AIDC energy storage is transitioning from backup power to core electrical infrastructure as market scale expands.

EVE Energy introduced several key new products, including the industry's first 20-foot 5MWh 1P high-rate energy storage system, the S5-1P 300+Ah frequency regulation dedicated cell, the Pandun 6C/10C backup cabinet supporting 10C continuous discharge, and a rack-level ultra-high-rate BBU+CBU system designed for millisecond-level power smoothing.

The company reports that its one-stop full-stack delivery model helps clients simplify procurement, clarify accountability through in-house development of cells, BMS, and systems, and reduce operational burdens for 24/7 data center environments.
