Anthropic Mathematician Clarifies That 250,000-Page Riemann Hypothesis Proof Was a Joke

Anthropic mathematician Levent Alpöge clarified on social media that his claim of a 250,000-page proof for the Riemann Hypothesis was actually a joke, though the project did generate 17.37 million lines of Lean formal code. Alpöge admitted that the code contains a lot of redundant and messy parts, sparking discussions on how AI-generated proofs outpace human comprehension.

iFanr 爱范儿 ↗Source published 2026-11-24 10:10 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

Recently, Anthropic mathematician Levent Alpöge clarified several controversies surrounding the formal proof of the Riemann Hypothesis conducted in collaboration with Claude. Alpöge admitted that his earlier claim about the proof translating to over 250,000 pages was meant as a joke, as he thought his verbose description of real numbers would make the humor obvious. However, the accompanying 17.37 million lines of Lean formal code are very real. While the Lean and Mathlib community expressed confidence in the verification, Alpöge acknowledged that the code contains significant redundancy and low-quality parts that need sorting.

Beyond the code controversy, Alpöge emphasized that the ultimate goal is to build more natural and unified mathematical frameworks rather than just churning out complex proofs. He noted that as AI accelerates mathematical derivations, a pressing challenge emerges: AI can generate proofs much faster than humans can fully comprehend them, meaning mathematicians now face the heavy task of turning these machine-checked answers into intuitive human knowledge.
