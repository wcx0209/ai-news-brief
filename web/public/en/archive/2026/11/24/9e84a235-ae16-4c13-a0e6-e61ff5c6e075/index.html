Four European Space Companies Team Up to Offer End-to-End Dual-Use Satellite Missions

Four European space companies have signed an agreement to provide a complete Earth observation mission solution. According to the partners, the collaboration combines Simera Sense's imaging payload, Spacemanic's satellite platform, Zaitra's onboard AI, and World from Space's analytics, offering customers a single coordinated path from image to insight.

SpaceNews ↗Source published 2026-11-24 23:17 SGTMedia reportSpace Economy

Simera Sense, Spacemanic, Zaitra and World from Space have signed a Memorandum of Understanding in Bremen to offer an integrated dual-use satellite mission solution. The agreement builds upon a prior cooperation between Simera Sense and Spacemanic.

According to the partners, the end-to-end solution covers the full chain of operations. Simera Sense provides its xScape200 Dual-Use camera, delivering 1.5-meter resolution. Spacemanic supplies the CORVUS satellite platform and SPACECANNON deployer. Zaitra contributes its SKAIDOCK computer running onboard AI and image compression to process data directly in orbit and reduce downlink volume. World from Space handles calibration, quality assessment, and data distribution through its ORBIS platform.

Representatives from all four companies state that the partnership aims to make space-based imaging easier to adopt by delivering actionable intelligence rather than raw data through a single consortium.
