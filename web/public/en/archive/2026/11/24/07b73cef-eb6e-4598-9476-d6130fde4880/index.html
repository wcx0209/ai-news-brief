Singapore and US cooperate on rerouted AI chips investigation

Singapore is cooperating with the US to prevent companies from exploiting its territory to reroute restricted AI chips to other countries. Prime Minister Lawrence Wong stated that Singapore is conducting its own investigation into local law breaches, following a scandal involving US chip giant Nvidia and a Singapore-based firm suspected of steering advanced chips toward China.

Le Monde AI ↗Source published 2026-11-24 10:49 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

Singapore Prime Minister Lawrence Wong announced during a visit to Australia that the country is closely collaborating with the US administration to stop businesses from using Singapore as a transit hub to reroute US microchips to other destinations, including parts of Southeast Asia and China. While clarifying that Singapore is not part of the US-led probe into Nvidia, Wong emphasized that local authorities are conducting their own investigations into potential breaches of Singaporean law.

The international scrutiny follows a high-profile scandal involving Nvidia, where US intelligence officials reportedly suspect a Singapore-based company named Megaspeed of steering advanced AI chips to China. Amid these concerns, the US government has tightened export controls, requiring data center operators in several countries, including Singapore, to secure specific licenses before shipping American advanced chips to clients in China. Singapore is now sharing information to help the US establish effective enforcement mechanisms.
