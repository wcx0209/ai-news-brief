Jensen Huang returns as Nvidia CEO after dramatic political intervention

Jensen Huang has been reinstated as Nvidia CEO following a turbulent period involving a personal scandal and a stock market drop. Supported by white house intervention, his return triggered a massive stock market surge as shares jumped 32%, though lawmakers continue to question the ethical and national security implications.

The Guardian AI ↗Source published 2026-11-24 09:34 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

Jensen Huang returned to Nvidia's Silicon Valley headquarters on Monday to a hero's welcome from employees, just hours after being reinstated as CEO. His dramatic comeback followed a series of high-profile events, including an alleged romantic affair with a suspected Chinese spy, a sharp drop in company stock, and a direct intervention from Donald Trump, who reportedly dialed into Nvidia's Sunday board meeting to advocate for Huang's return.

Following the board's decision, which Nvidia acknowledged included input from senior US government officials, the stock market reacted strongly. Nvidia shares surged 32% on Monday, adding over $1tn to the company's market value. Tech leaders and employees celebrated the news, cementing Huang's central role in the artificial intelligence boom.

Despite the market enthusiasm and strong internal support, Huang's reinstatement has drawn sharp criticism from lawmakers. US Senator Jack Reed called for full disclosure of communications between Huang and Trump, raising serious concerns regarding ethics, national security, and whether corporate giants like Nvidia are becoming too big to fail.
