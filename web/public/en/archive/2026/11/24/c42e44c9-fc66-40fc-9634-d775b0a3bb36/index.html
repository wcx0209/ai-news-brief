PollyPolymer Secures Over 500 Million RMB in Consecutive Funding Rounds to Scale 3D Printing for Embodied AI

Chinese 3D printing enterprise PollyPolymer has completed its B2 and B+ rounds of equity financing, raising a combined total of over 500 million RMB. The company plans to use the fresh capital to advance high-performance material R&D, expand equipment production capacity, develop industrial applications for embodied AI, and build a global delivery network.

Established in 2017, PollyPolymer specializes in high-molecular elastomer materials and ultra-high-speed 3D printing mass manufacturing. According to the company, it has accumulated over 16,000 polymer material formulations and developed a proprietary HALS ultra-high-speed 3D printing technology platform that increases molding efficiency by 20 to 100 times compared to traditional methods.

In terms of commercial expansion, PollyPolymer has applied its technology from shoe manufacturing to the robotics sector. The company reports that it has delivered over 10 million terminal parts globally and launched dedicated industry solutions such as RoboFab for robotics and PollyAlign for foot customization. In the robotics field, it currently provides elastic components like bionic muscles, silent foot pads, and anti-interference shells to dozens of robotics firms including UBTECH, with cumulative deliveries reaching tens of thousands of sets.
