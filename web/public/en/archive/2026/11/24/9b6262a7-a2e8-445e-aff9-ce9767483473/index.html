Enterprise AI Readiness: A Framework for Success

According to Cohere Blog: Cohere outlines a structured framework for evaluating enterprise AI readiness, emphasizing that success requires alignment across strategy, technology, data, governance, and organizational culture rather than just adopting the latest tools.

Cohere Blog ↗Source published 2026-11-24 05:44 SGTOfficial releaseArtificial Intelligence

According to Cohere Blog: David Kim and Sandra Kublik from Cohere discuss why many enterprises struggle with AI initiatives due to a lack of preparation across capabilities, risks, and skills gaps. They propose a structured AI readiness assessment spanning seven distinct dimensions: strategy and leadership alignment, AI technical expertise resources, technology and infrastructure, governance, risk and compliance, data readiness, organizational change, and use case discovery. The report notes that regulated industries face additional hurdles regarding data sovereignty, and points out that external resources like forward deployed engineers can help bridge talent gaps before companies build in-house teams. Ultimately, starting with a comprehensive assessment helps organizations move beyond fragmented pilot projects toward sustainable, long-term business impact.
