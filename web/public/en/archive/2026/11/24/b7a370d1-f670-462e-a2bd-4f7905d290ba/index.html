Google Search Connects Wallet and Drive to AI Mode for Personal Intelligence

According to Google AI: Google is updating its Search AI Mode to connect with Google Wallet and Google Drive, allowing the search engine to use personal spending, documents, and chat history for tailored responses. Users must opt in to use these features, and Google states that connected services like Wallet cannot move or manage money.

Google AI ↗Source published 2026-11-24 01:00 SGTOfficial releaseArtificial Intelligence

According to Google AI: According to Google Search VP of Product Robby Stein, Google is expanding its Personal Intelligence features in AI Mode to help users find more relevant information. Starting rollout, users can connect Google Wallet to review financial milestones, trim everyday spending, and optimize credit card rewards. Support for Google Drive and the Gemini app will follow in the upcoming weeks, bringing document context and chat history directly into Search. Google notes that users retain full control, with options to turn connections on or off at any time and view the specific context used via a personalization icon.
