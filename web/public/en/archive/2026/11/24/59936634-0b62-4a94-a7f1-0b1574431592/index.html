Bambu Lab Releases P3C Desktop 3D Printer with Seven Tool Slots Priced Around 7,000 RMB

Bambu Lab has released its third-generation desktop 3D printer, the P3C, bringing the Swaptek seven-tool-head architecture from its flagship H3C down to a consumer price point. With prices starting at around 7,000 RMB after subsidies, the machine supports multi-material and multi-color printing, aiming to shift industry competition toward material freedom and automated tool changes.

Nanjixiong 南极熊3D打印 ↗Source published 2026-11-24 00:00 SGTMedia reportAdvanced Manufacturing & Industrial Infrastructure

According to reports from Nanji Xiong, Bambu Lab launched the P3C desktop 3D printer on November 24, 2026, integrating the Swaptek seven-tool-head architecture previously found only in the H3C flagship model into a more affordable device. The P3C standard version starts at 6,374 RMB after subsidies, while the seven-tool flash-cut version is priced at 7,207 RMB, and a multi-diameter filament version is available at 7,292 RMB.

Technically, the P3C features five pre-installed 0.4mm PrintTools and two open slots for custom additions. Each tool slot has independent feeding and power interfaces, allowing for material switches in about 6 seconds without the need for traditional retraction and re-feeding. Paired with the AMS 3P feeding system and an optional dual-slot 75-degree-Celsius drying module, the machine can handle up to 22 colors or materials simultaneously, including soft filaments like TPU via the FlexFeed extruder.

To showcase its practical application, Bambu Lab collaborated with creators to release a modular glasses model on MakerWorld, demonstrating how soft and hard materials can be printed in a single run. This unexpected launch puts immediate pressure on competing manufacturers in the desktop 3D printing market.
