AI Singapore Teams Up with ASEAN Foundation to Fight AI Scams

According to AI Singapore: AI Singapore is partnering with the ASEAN Foundation as a local partner for Scam Ready ASEAN, a regional program backed by Google.org's USD 5 million funding. Running until 2027, the initiative aims to train Master Trainers and educate the public on recognizing emerging AI-driven online scams.

AI Singapore ↗Source published 2026-11-24 16:06 SGTOfficial releaseArtificial Intelligence

According to AI Singapore:

Program Background and Goals

According to AI Singapore, the Scam Ready ASEAN program is a regional initiative running from 2025 to 2027, backed by USD 5 million from Google.org and implemented by the ASEAN Foundation. Across 11 ASEAN member states, the program aims to equip 3 million people with practical knowledge to counter online scams, including deepfakes and AI-generated phishing.

Implementation in Singapore

AI Singapore will act as the local implementing partner in Singapore through Q4 2027. The organization plans to train Master Trainers, localize educational modules, engage community volunteers, run public awareness campaigns, and organize a national convening to foster cross-sector collaboration against online scams.
