OpenAI Expands Free ChatGPT Access Program for 200,000 Researchers

According to OpenAI News: OpenAI is expanding its academic program to provide up to 200,000 researchers worldwide with free access to ChatGPT Plus, aiming to support scientific discovery and gather usage feedback.

OpenAI News ↗Source published 2026-11-17 01:00 SGTOfficial releaseArtificial Intelligence

According to OpenAI News:

Program Expansion

OpenAI announced the expansion of its ChatGPT for Academic Researchers program, aiming to reach a total of 200,000 researchers globally. The next application round opens on November 23, offering 50,000 new grants on a first-come, first-served basis. Each grant covers the primary recipient and up to two collaborators with free access to ChatGPT Plus.

Pilot Insights and Impact

According to an OpenAI Economic Research report based on 1,800 participants from the summer pilot, surveyed researchers estimated that AI tools helped cut research time in half while maintaining quality. Early participants are utilizing the technology for various applications, such as identifying drug targets for tuberculosis and converting museum specimens into searchable 3D databases.

Broader Access Initiatives

In addition to individual researcher grants, OpenAI provides broader institutional access through ChatGPT Edu for universities and the GPT-Rosalind plan for biology labs. The company also contributed $5 million to a Simons Foundation initiative to support mathematical research using AI.
