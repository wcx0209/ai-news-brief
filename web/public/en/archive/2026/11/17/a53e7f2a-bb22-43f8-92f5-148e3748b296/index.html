Cybersecurity AI startup Corma hits $1B valuation in 3 months

AI cybersecurity startup Corma has raised a $125 million Series A round at a $1 billion valuation just three months after launching its product. The company develops AI models and agents designed specifically for defense against cyber threats, riding a wave of massive industry demand sparked by recent major security vulnerabilities.

TechCrunch AI ↗Source published 2026-11-17 07:32 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

Background and Funding

AI cybersecurity startup Corma announced that it has raised a $125 million Series A round at a $1 billion valuation, led by NEA. The company came out of stealth in August and has now raised a total of $185 million from prominent backers including Sequoia Capital, Khosla Ventures, and Coatue, according to the company.

Market Catalysts

Interest in Corma's AI defense products surged following a chaotic day of security incidents on October 21. Critical flaws discovered in PHP and Node.js, alongside a security incident in LiteLLM and other disclosures, left chief information security officers scrambling. The events highlighted the scale of digital attacks and the growing need for automated defense mechanisms enabled by AI.

Industry Landscape

Corma operates in a crowded and rapidly growing sector. Other notable entrants include Kevin Mandia's startup Armadin, tech giants like Anthropic, OpenAI, and Microsoft, alongside numerous other startups. Corma positions itself uniquely as a frontier AI research lab dedicated entirely to digital protection.
