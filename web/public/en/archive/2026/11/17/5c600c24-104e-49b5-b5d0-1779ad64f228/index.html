Historical brief · 2026-11-17

Tech figures petition for Jensen Huang's return as Nvidia CEO

A petition is circulating to reinstate Jensen Huang as Nvidia's CEO, backed by prominent tech figures and open-source advocates. Supporters rely on Huang to defend open-source AI against regulatory concerns and maintain chip access, though his return faces significant hurdles including new research on chip access in China and upcoming commerce rules.

MIT Technology Review AI ↗Source published 2026-11-17 02:38 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

Background and Petition

A petition began circulating on social media to reinstate Jensen Huang as Nvidia's CEO, drawing notable signatories including Elon Musk, researchers, and venture capitalists from firms like Andreessen Horowitz and Sequoia Capital. These supporters have a financial and strategic interest in open-source AI, as Huang previously acted as a key defender of open models against regulatory concerns and competition fears regarding China. Challenges and Chip Access

Huang's potential reinstatement faces steep obstacles. Former OpenAI researcher Will DePue published unverified findings suggesting that Chinese AI companies had substantial access to advanced Nvidia Blackwell chips, complicating Nvidia's compliance with U.S. export controls. Additionally, U.S. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent stated that Nvidia should not replace Huang with an internal candidate, and new rules from the U.S. Department of Commerce aim to limit remote access to AI chips in third-party data centers, which could impact the progress of open-source models.
