84% of Brits Support Plant-Based Action Plan to Help Farmers Transition

A new 600-person survey by Rooted Research Collective and Bryant Research shows that 84% of British consumers support a national plant-based action plan, and 89% back the Farm Adaptation Network. The poll indicates strong public backing for helping farmers shift toward sustainable protein crops while addressing financial risks.

Green Queen ↗Source published 2026-11-17 17:29 SGTMedia reportAgriculture & Food Technology

According to the survey by Rooted Research Collective and Bryant Research, British consumers are increasingly in favor of a plant-rich food system and policy interventions to assist farmers. The findings reveal that 68% of respondents believe farmers are treated worst within the food system, facing severe financial and production risks. Meanwhile, 71% of UK consumers support farmers diversifying into alternative proteins, although concerns remain over taxpayer costs, with 53% expressing worries about the financial burden. The report's authors suggest several measures for policymakers, such as making capital more accessible, funding protein crop trials, and tying public co-investment to domestically grown inputs.
