Tech leaders are convinced that Jensen Huang’s exit was a plot against him

Following Jensen Huang's stunning resignation from Nvidia, prominent tech figures like David Sacks and Tim Sweeney are questioning the circumstances surrounding his exit. Many in Silicon Valley believe the scandal involving unconfirmed spy allegations was orchestrated to remove opposition to strict export control policies.

The Verge AI ↗Source published 2026-11-17 07:17 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

Jensen Huang's unexpected resignation after more than 33 years at the helm of Nvidia has triggered intense debate across the tech industry. The events were initially set off by reports regarding relationships with employees, including former Megaspeed executive Alice Huang, amid allegations that she was a Chinese spy. However, US agencies have not officially confirmed these spy allegations.

Prominent figures, including venture capitalist David Sacks and Epic Games CEO Tim Sweeney, have raised skepticism, suggesting that rumors were weaponized by lobbyists or interested parties to push out a visionary leader. Semiconductor research firm SemiAnalysis further speculated that Huang's removal serves to eliminate opposition to the Remote Access Security Act (RASA), which aims to tighten AI chip export controls.

In response, a "Stand with Jensen" letter has garnered over 185,000 signatures from high-profile tech leaders like Elon Musk, Garry Tan, and Tobias Lütke, demanding facts rather than unverified suspicions and calling for Huang's reinstatement.
