AMD and Rancher Government Solutions Bring Security-Focused HPC and AI to Kubernetes

According to AMD Blogs: AMD and Rancher Government Solutions are partnering to launch a production-ready reference architecture that simplifies AI and HPC workloads on Kubernetes. Powered by the RGS Carbide Platform and AMD Instinct GPUs, this initiative aims to help research and security organizations improve hardware utilization while maintaining high security standards.

AMD Blogs ↗Source published 2026-11-17 05:21 SGTOfficial releaseArtificial Intelligence

According to AMD Blogs: AMD reports that its partnership with Rancher Government Solutions (RGS) introduces a hardened architecture tailored for government, research, and national security organizations. The solution combines the RGS Carbide Platform with high-performance AMD Instinct GPUs and open ROCm software, addressing the complexities of scheduling demanding computing tasks. According to the announcement, the architecture aims to streamline the deployment of high-performance computing and artificial intelligence on Kubernetes, ensuring efficient GPU resource management within a secure framework.
