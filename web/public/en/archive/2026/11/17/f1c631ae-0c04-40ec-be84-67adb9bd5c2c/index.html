Faresin unveils electric Eramix feeding robot at Eima

Italian manufacturer Faresin presents the Eramix prototype, an autonomous and electric feeding robot that handles silage removal, mixing, and distribution. The machine features wireless charging via induction and is slated for a phased market release around 2028 or 2029.

The Eramix Prototype

Italian manufacturer Faresin is showcasing the Eramix autonomous feeding robot at the Eima trade fair in Bologna. The machine is designed to drive to the silage clamp, extract silage using a front milling arm, mix it in a 9 m³ tub with twin vertical augers, and distribute it to livestock entirely on electric power.

Wireless Charging and Testing

A notable feature of the Eramix is its wireless induction charging system, which allows the robot to charge automatically without manual plugging in once parked in a designated spot. While Faresin has not disclosed specific navigation details, one unit is currently undergoing on-farm testing, with a phased market introduction expected in countries like the Netherlands by 2028 or 2029.
