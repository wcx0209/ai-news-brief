AI wipes out $206 billion in market value for 10 listed companies

According to a report by European Internet Ventures, 10 listed companies in sectors like travel, creative software, and online education lost $206 billion in market value between December 2021 and November 2026.

Mint AI ↗Source published 2026-11-17 00:20 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

The Scope of AI Disruption A new report by European Internet Ventures highlights that 10 listed companies operating in AI-exposed sectors saw their combined market capitalization plummet from $305 billion to $99 billion between December 2021 and November 2026. While nine of these businesses actually recorded revenue growth during parts of this period, investors are increasingly worried about long-term earnings as generative AI reshapes their foundational business models. Sector Breakdown The disruption hits hardest in industries built around information discovery and content creation. In travel, Tripadvisor's market value fell 77% and Trivago dropped 67% as AI assistants began comparing options directly, though Booking Holdings gained 20% due to its role in final payment processing. In creative and freelance sectors, companies like Shutterstock and Fiverr suffered steep valuation losses as users turned to AI for instant images and digital services. Meanwhile, education and developer platforms like Chegg and Stack Overflow faced massive declines as AI chatbots replaced subscription-based homework help and coding Q&A. Future Resilience The report suggests that platforms merely providing leads or information are vulnerable, while businesses that facilitate actual financial transactions and deliver end-to-end outcomes in fields like customer service and legal work may prove more resilient moving forward.
