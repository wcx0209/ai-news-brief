ICEYE Launches €2.05B Takeover Bid for Italian Rocket Builder Avio

Finnish satellite company ICEYE has launched a €2.05 billion all-cash takeover bid for Italian rocket builder Avio to create a unified European space and defense player. ICEYE also promised to invest €1 billion in Italy if the deal succeeds, though the transaction still faces multiple regulatory hurdles across Europe and the US.

European Spaceflight ↗Source published 2026-11-17 16:08 SGTMedia reportSpace Economy

Acquisition Details Finnish satellite builder and operator ICEYE has made a €2.05 billion all-cash offer to acquire Italian rocket builder Avio through its Italian subsidiary, Iris 2. The offer of €41 per share represents a 21.3% premium over Avio's closing share price, and ICEYE intends to delist Avio from the Milan stock exchange following the acquisition. Strategic Rationale ICEYE CEO Rafal Modrzewski stated that the merger aims to build a European space and defense champion capable of manufacturing satellites, launching them, and providing intelligence services. The combination addresses reciprocal needs, as ICEYE requires secure launch capacity for its constellation while Avio benefits from long-term launch demand visibility. Investment and Hurdles Beyond the buyout, ICEYE plans to invest an additional €1 billion in Italy over five years to boost manufacturing, R&D, and local suppliers, potentially creating up to 400 jobs. The transaction will be financed through existing cash and debt, following ICEYE's recent €450 million funding round, but it still requires regulatory clearances from Italy, France, the UK, the US, and the EU.
