Stratasys launches larger H550 SAF 3D printer for factory production

Stratasys is expanding its SAF 3D printing lineup with the new H550 machine, designed to handle larger industrial applications like drone and automotive components. The company states that the printer offers a 34.2-litre build space and aims to reduce total cost of ownership by up to 30 percent compared to similar systems.

Background and Design

Stratasys introduced the H550 3D printer at the Formnext event, building on its existing SAF (Selective Absorption Fusion) technology. According to the company, previous models like the H350 faced limitations regarding part size, prompting the development of this larger system to accommodate longer industrial components.

Capabilities and Cost

The H550 features a build space of 34.2 liters and a length of 450mm, targeting sectors such as robotics, medical devices, and aerospace. Stratasys reports that the system achieves up to 30 percent lower total cost of ownership than comparable print-and-fuse systems and provides faster production times than standard SLS alternatives.

Workflow Automation

Alongside the printer, Stratasys announced the PowderEase T2, an automated system handling powder retrieval, sieving, and dosing that supports up to six H550 units. The company claims this workflow requires less than 30 minutes of total operator time per build.
