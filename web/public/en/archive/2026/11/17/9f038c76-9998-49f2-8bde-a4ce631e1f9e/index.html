Reflection launches Trusted Access Program with autonomous penetration-testing tool Laser

According to Reflection AI: Reflection has launched a Trusted Access Program to provide vetted organizations with early access to selected defensive cybersecurity AI capabilities. The program starts with Laser, an autonomous penetration-testing harness designed to help security teams adversarially test their applications and networks.

Reflection AI ↗Source published 2026-11-17 08:00 SGTOfficial releaseArtificial Intelligence

According to Reflection AI:

Reflection announced the launch of its Trusted Access Program, an initiative designed to grant vetted organizations early access to advanced AI capabilities and tools focused on defensive cybersecurity. The program debuts with Laser, an autonomous penetration-testing harness built for Reflection's terminal agent, Mirror.

According to Reflection, Laser is designed to provide security teams with a scalable way to conduct authorized penetration tests on web vulnerabilities and network targets. The system combines scoped testing, a shared evidence ledger, and a separate supervisor to guide actions, verify findings with attack graphs, and document observations. Laser is currently available as a research preview exclusively for commercial partners accepted into the program, serving as part of Reflection's broader Project Iris initiative.
