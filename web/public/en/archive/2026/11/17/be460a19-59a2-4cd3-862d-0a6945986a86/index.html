SIASUN Robot: Pushing Core Technologies and Embodied AI Forward

According to SIASUN 新松机器人: According to the Economic Information Daily, Chinese robotics company SIASUN Robot has achieved key breakthroughs in industrial automation, including deploying nearly 100 heavy-duty domestic robots in automotive welding lines and advancing into semiconductor equipment and embodied AI.

According to SIASUN 新松机器人:

According to the Economic Information Daily, SIASUN Robot has spent years developing core technologies to overcome technological bottlenecks in industrial automation.

In the automotive manufacturing sector, SIASUN collaborated with Geely Auto to deploy nearly 100 domestic heavy-duty industrial robots in a Yiwu assembly plant, covering over 95% of welding processes and achieving a major milestone for domestic welding robots in main automotive production lines. Meanwhile, its vacuum manipulators for semiconductor equipment have entered the global first tier, driving the company's semiconductor equipment orders past 1 billion yuan in the first three quarters of 2026.

Looking toward future industries, SIASUN established an embodied intelligence research institute in April 2025 and released two wheeled humanoid robots along with a multi-robot collaboration system in August 2026. The company plans to leverage its reliable hardware foundation and deep manufacturing experience to tackle non-standard flexible scenarios first, aiming to bridge the gap between laboratory performance and real-world industrial tasks.
