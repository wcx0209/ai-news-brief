MUFG Partners with Yutaka Matsuo's AI Startup to Adopt Ubiquitous AGI

MUFG and Mitsubishi UFJ Bank have partnered with Third Intelligence, an AI startup founded by University of Tokyo Professor Yutaka Matsuo, to implement ubiquitous AGI in financial services. This new AI model uses a unique algorithm that self-structures during learning, differing from standard Transformers, and is currently being tested for complex inheritance tasks.

ITmedia AI+ ↗Source published 2026-11-17 17:00 SGTNews summaryArtificial Intelligence

According to reports by ITmedia, Mitsubishi UFJ Financial Group (MUFG) and Mitsubishi UFJ Bank announced a partnership with Third Intelligence and the AI commercialization firm Matsuo Research on November 17. The collaboration focuses on implementing Third Intelligence's ubiquitous AGI within financial services. Unlike conventional models built on fixed Transformer architectures, the ubiquitous AGI dynamically structures itself during the learning process, allowing for more efficient learning with less data. MUFG is already running proof-of-concept tests for inheritance services, highlighting the technology's long-term memory and personalization capabilities for complex family situations. Additionally, Matsuo Research will support MUFG through startup connections and talent development programs, following MUFG's initial investment and memorandum of understanding with Third Intelligence signed in November 2025.
