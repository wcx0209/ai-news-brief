Jensen Huang's exit from Nvidia turns into a political storm

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang has resigned following a personal scandal, sending shockwaves through the tech industry and wiping out billions in market value. Meanwhile, the fallout has sparked a bitter feud within the US administration over export controls on China. At the same time, a wave of unrelated global tech outages has left critical infrastructure struggling, fueling public anxiety.

The Guardian AI ↗Source published 2026-11-17 22:50 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang resigned on Thursday following revelations of multiple affairs, including one with an executive at Megaspeed, a datacenter firm investigated by US intelligence for allegedly spying for China. The departure cost Nvidia hundreds of billions in market value and triggered intense debate over 'key man risk' and the 'genius-founder' business model, as Huang left behind no obvious succession plan.

The scandal has exacerbated tensions within the Trump administration. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent accused Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick of failing to block Nvidia chip sales that allegedly bypassed export controls via Megaspeed, which funneled chips to Chinese clients through south-east Asia. Lutnick has favored softer trade stances, while Bessent pushes for hawkish restrictions. The controversy coincides with a broader wave of critical infrastructure failures, including a massive Microsoft Outlook outage, an FAA ground stop caused by maintenance issues involving old copper wires, and banking glitches at JPMorgan Chase, leaving observers grappling with widespread system fragility.
