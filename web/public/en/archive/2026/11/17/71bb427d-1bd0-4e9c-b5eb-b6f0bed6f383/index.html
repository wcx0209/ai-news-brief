PV Tech Power Vol. 46 Out Now: Inside US Solar Growth and Data Centre Batteries

Energy-Storage.news reports that the latest edition of PV Tech Power is now available, focusing on the rapid growth of the US solar manufacturing sector and its rise to third globally in module production. The issue also explores AI data centre batteries and the geopolitical shift from petrostates to electrostates.

Energy-Storage.news ↗Source published 2026-11-17 23:20 SGTMedia reportEnergy & Storage

The latest edition of PV Tech Power highlights the rapid expansion of America’s photovoltaic (PV) manufacturing sector, which has propelled the US to third place in global module production rankings. The issue features data on emerging capacity pipelines, technology splits, and interviews with new US solar manufacturers discussing their market niches.

Additionally, the journal covers a geopolitical analysis of the clean energy transition by writer Chris Nelder, examining which nations are leading the race for supremacy. It also includes a deep dive into AI data centres and their relationship with battery storage technologies, alongside evaluations of bankable market strategies for European battery energy storage systems.

Other topics in the special edition include cybersecurity, supply chain risks, Europe's solar and storage tenders, and China’s 15th Five-Year Plan. The journal is normally reserved for premium subscribers, but this edition is temporarily available for free.
