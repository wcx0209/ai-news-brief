Stark Future and Farsoon Team Up to Build Metal 3D Printing Production Line

According to Farsoon Technologies 华曙高科: Electric motorcycle maker Stark Future and 3D printing company Farsoon Technologies have agreed to deploy multiple FS4X systems to build a dedicated series production line. The partnership focuses on developing an end-to-end workflow to meet the strict quality and cost requirements of replacing conventional manufacturing.

Farsoon Technologies 华曙高科 ↗Source published 2026-11-17 00:00 SGTOfficial releaseAdvanced Manufacturing & Industrial Infrastructure

According to Farsoon Technologies 华曙高科:

Stark Future and Farsoon Technologies have entered an agreement at Formnext 2026 to implement multiple Farsoon FS4X metal additive manufacturing systems, creating a dedicated high-volume series production line. The collaboration extends beyond equipment installation, involving joint development of an end-to-end workflow that covers material parameters, heat treatment, downstream finishing, and full part traceability to reduce scrap and downtime.

At the center of this initiative is the Farsoon FS4X, a metal laser powder bed fusion platform designed for industrial series production with multi-laser processing and stable build conditions. This new venture scales up a partnership that began in 2024 when Stark Future utilized Farsoon's large-format printing platform, following milestones such as the KLINGA project which validated titanium serial production across more than 1,000 parts. According to Stark Future CEO Anton Wass, metal 3D printing allows the company to reduce component weight by up to 50% while optimizing vehicle performance.
