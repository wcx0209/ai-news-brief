MIT School of Engineering Faculty and Staff Receive Awards in Summer 2026

According to MIT Advanced Manufacturing: The MIT School of Engineering highlights several faculty members and researchers who received prestigious awards in summer 2026 for their contributions to research, technology, and education.

MIT Advanced Manufacturing ↗Source published 2026-11-17 04:45 SGTOfficial releaseAdvanced Manufacturing & Industrial Infrastructure
Artificial Intelligence

According to MIT Advanced Manufacturing:

The MIT School of Engineering has announced a series of notable honors received by its faculty and researchers in summer 2026, spanning multiple engineering and scientific disciplines.

Key recognitions include Srini Devadas receiving the ACM-IEEE CS Eckert-Mauchly Award for pioneering contributions to computer and digital systems architecture, and Robert Langer being honored with the Global Distinguished Engineer Award and the AIChE Shaping the Future Award for his transformative career in chemical engineering and human health.

Additionally, several early-career and mid-career researchers were celebrated for their specific projects. Jelena Notaros earned multiple accolades including an AFOSR Young Investigator Research Program Award for quantum computing research and a spot on MIT Technology Review's 35 Innovators Under 35 list. Daniela Rus was named to TIME's list of the 100 Most Influential People in AI and received Bavaria's High-Tech Prize. Other honorees include Faez Ahmed, Elfatih Eltahir, William Green, Neville Hogan, Dina Katabi, Kristala L. Jones Prather, Lindsey Raymond, César Terrer, and Federico Tessari, reflecting the diverse impact of MIT's research community.
