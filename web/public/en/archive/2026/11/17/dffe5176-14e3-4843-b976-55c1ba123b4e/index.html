Shanghai Silicate Institute Develops Ah-Class Iron Fluoride Solid-State Battery

According to CAS Shanghai Institute of Ceramics: Researchers at the Shanghai Institute of Ceramics report the development of an Ah-class soft-pack battery using a high-capacity iron fluoride positive electrode.

CAS Shanghai Institute of Ceramics ↗Source published 2026-11-17 13:50 SGTOfficial releaseAdvanced Materials & Nanotechnology
Energy & Storage

According to CAS Shanghai Institute of Ceramics:

Lithium metal solid-state batteries paired with high-capacity conversion-type positive electrodes are seen as a promising path for next-generation energy storage. According to the researchers, iron fluoride possesses an exceptionally high theoretical specific capacity, but suffers from severe side reactions, interface evolution, and mechanical mismatch, which historically restricted these batteries to small laboratory buttons.

To overcome this, the research team from the Shanghai Institute of Ceramics designed a composite fluorine-rich catalyst to enable controllable cationic ring-opening in-situ polymerization. This approach simultaneously engineers the interfaces on both the lithium metal negative electrode and the iron fluoride positive electrode, improving lithium ion flux uniformity and reaction reversibility. Building on this chemistry, the team successfully scaled up the technology to produce a 20-layer stacked soft-pack battery, crossing the one-ampere-hour discharge capacity threshold.
