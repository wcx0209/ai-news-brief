QUDORA Integrates NVIDIA CUDA-Q into Its Quantum Software Development Kit

Quantum tech company QUDORA has integrated NVIDIA CUDA-Q into its software development kit. This allows developers to build hybrid quantum programs and test them on the Qamelion quantum emulator before physical hardware becomes available.

The Quantum Insider ↗Source published 2026-11-17 23:02 SGTMedia reportQuantum Technology

According to QUDORA Technologies, the integration of NVIDIA CUDA-Q into its software development kit enables developers to write hybrid quantum-classical programs. These programs can then be executed on Qamelion, QUDORA's quantum emulator featuring adaptive noise modeling. This emulator realistically simulates QUDORA's planned trapped-ion hardware, letting developers examine how operational noise impacts program behavior. The integration is available now through the QUDORA SDK, providing a practical environment for algorithm testing ahead of physical processor deployment.
