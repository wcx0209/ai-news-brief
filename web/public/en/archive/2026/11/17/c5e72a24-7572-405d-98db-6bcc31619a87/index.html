Claude Solves Two Long-Standing Theoretical Physics Problems Regarding Black Holes

According to Anthropic Research: Anthropic reports that its AI system Claude has independently solved two open problems in theoretical physics concerning black holes and quantum gravity.

Anthropic Research ↗Source published 2026-11-17 08:00 SGTOfficial releaseArtificial Intelligence

According to Anthropic Research:

According to Anthropic, researchers tested Claude's capabilities by setting it loose on theoretical physics problems using a small, self-coordinated research group of independent AI agents. The first breakthrough addresses a calculation first proposed in 2003 regarding the Hawking-Page phase transition. By running thousands of terms and verifying math across multiple iterations, Claude's agents proved that the phase transition from a particle gas to a black hole remains abrupt at both weak and strong couplings, suggesting this behavior is built into the underlying quantum theory.

The second breakthrough involves the modular bootstrap method used to study 3D quantum gravity. By spotting a missed pattern in numerical results and applying a mathematical tool known as a spectral curve, Claude calculated the exact ceiling for the lightest black hole mass in simplified universes, proving the absolute limit of this specific technique. Both preprints have been released and formally verified in the Lean proof checker, with physicists validating the primary findings.
