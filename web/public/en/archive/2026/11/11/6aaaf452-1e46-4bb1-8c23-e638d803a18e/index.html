AMSIS Appoints Steffen Reinfurth as Co-CEO

TCT MagazineAdvanced Manufacturing & Industrial InfrastructureCompanySource published2026-11-11 23:54 SGT
Media reportStage not established

AMSIS GmbH has restructured its leadership by appointing Steffen Reinfurth as co-CEO alongside company founder Vasily Ploshikhin. Reinfurth, who previously served as Managing Director and CSO, brings two decades of experience in the additive manufacturing industry and previously founded AMECOS GmbH.

In the shared leadership model, Reinfurth will concentrate on sales, marketing, and product strategy. Meanwhile, Ploshikhin will direct his efforts toward innovation, research, and new technology development.

The leadership shift comes as AMSIS looks to transition from technology development to commercial industrialization. The company specializes in simulation software that connects the physics of metal additive manufacturing with practical factory decisions, helping manufacturers optimize their production processes.
