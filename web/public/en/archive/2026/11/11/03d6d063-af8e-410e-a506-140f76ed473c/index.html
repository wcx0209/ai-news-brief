Fire Damage Delays Installation of Simulated Lunar Greenhouse in Germany

A fire at a contractor's facility has delayed the installation of Germany's simulated lunar greenhouse. The project, designed to test crop growth for future space missions, is now pushed back to 2027.

European Spaceflight ↗Source published 2026-11-11 15:00 SGTMedia reportSpace Economy

Background

The EDEN-LUNA greenhouse is being developed by the German Aerospace Center to study technologies for growing fresh food during extended missions to the Moon and Mars. It builds on the successful EDEN ISS greenhouse project operated in Antarctica.

Details

A fire broke out at an external contractor's facility while the greenhouse container was being refurbished. Although the German Aerospace Center previously stated in September that the timeline would shift to 2027, the specific reason of the fire was only revealed recently through a parliamentary inquiry. Officials have not yet disclosed the exact extent of the damage.

Impact

The incident has pushed back the installation at the LUNA facility in Cologne from 2026 to the second quarter of 2027. Once completed, the upgraded greenhouse will test robotic assistance and nutrient recovery systems to prepare astronauts for future space exploration.
