Google brings Gemini Intelligence to more devices with new features

Google AIArtificial IntelligenceCompanySource published2026-11-11 03:00 SGT
Official releaseStage not established

According to Google AI: Google is aggressively expanding its Gemini Intelligence ecosystem to a broader range of hardware, including the newly supported Pixel 10 series and Motorola Signature 27, while bringing features like Rambler and task automation to more regions. Key new additions include Create My Widget, which allows users to design personalized home screen widgets using just a text prompt without any coding, and Device Assistance, turning Gemini into a built-in expert that can manage phone settings, clear storage, and handle privacy checks through natural conversation. Additionally, Gemini is breaking into new form factors and scenarios, integrating proactive suggestions into Android Auto to connect messages with emails on the go, and bringing custom widgets to the Pixel Watch.
