AI100 2026 report highlights embodied intelligence as a grand challenge

Background The AI100 is a long-term study that checks in every five years on how artificial intelligence is changing society. The 2026 edition looks at everything from generative AI scaling to cybersecurity, international governance, and scientific discovery. Key Details Experts highlighted six grand challenges, putting embodied intelligence at the top for robotics. Over the past five years, robotics has seen massive leaps by combining natural language processing with vision-language-action models. Notably, the report highlights that China leads in industrial robots and humanoid robot production. Real-World Impact Despite cool demos, a huge gap exists between testing capabilities and actual deployment outside controlled environments like warehouses. Overcoming the real-world complexity requires breakthroughs in long-horizon planning, dexterous manipulation, and potentially new learning paradigms.
