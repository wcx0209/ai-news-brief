Alibaba's T-Head Seeks Hong Kong IPO to Fund AI Chip Ambitions

Founded in 2018, T-Head has evolved from an IoT chip explorer into a full-stack data center chip supplier covering computing, storage, and networking. Supported by Alibaba's heavy investments, its Zhenwu AI chips have been deployed across more than 650 external enterprise customers, making it a leading domestic player. However, as Alibaba's capital expenditures surge for AI development, spinning off T-Head provides a way for the chipmaker to raise funds independently while easing the parent company's balance sheet.
