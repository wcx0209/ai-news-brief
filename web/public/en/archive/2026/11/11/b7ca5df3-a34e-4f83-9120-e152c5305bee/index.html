How to Use Gemini Notebook to Slash Internal Support Costs

Gemini Notebook lets companies build custom AI helpers by simply uploading existing manuals and documents. It answers routine employee questions with citations and generates FAQs or handover materials instantly, saving countless hours for HR and administrative teams.

ITmedia AI+ ↗Source published 2026-11-11 11:50 SGTNews summaryArtificial Intelligence

Managing repetitive internal inquiries regarding expense claims, HR rules, and IT systems often drains administrative bandwidth. Gemini Notebook addresses this by allowing teams to ingest PDFs, documents, and web pages without requiring new content creation. When staff ask questions, the tool provides direct answers along with exact source citations, ensuring high transparency and preventing hallucinations when information is missing. Furthermore, the system can automate the creation of FAQ drafts and staff handover materials complete with presentation outlines. To ensure safe deployment, companies are advised to use Google Workspace for information governance, verify document accuracy before uploading, establish clear access rules, and start small by targeting a single repetitive administrative process.
