OpenAI Buys AI Travel Startup Odessia and Hires Founder

Background Odessia was a one-year-old startup that raised $6 million in pre-seed funding led by Sequoia. Its founder, Francis Davidson, previously built the short-term rental unicorn Sonder, which unfortunately went bankrupt after the pandemic and a failed partnership. Acquisition Details Although Odessia's travel product has already been shut down and the tool is no longer running, Davidson and his expertise were brought on board by OpenAI. This looks largely like an acqui-hire to bolster OpenAI's talent pool in the travel sector. Future Impact OpenAI has pointed out that travel is one of the most common tasks users ask ChatGPT about. While there are no official announcements of a dedicated travel product yet, bringing in a seasoned travel entrepreneur suggests that OpenAI wants to make its personal assistants much better at handling real-world trip planning.
