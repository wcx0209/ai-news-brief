MIT School of Engineering welcomes new faculty members for 2026

According to MIT Biological Engineering: MIT's School of Engineering is welcoming new faculty across multiple disciplines, bringing fresh perspectives to research and teaching.

MIT Biological Engineering ↗Source published 2026-11-11 06:00 SGTOfficial releaseBiotech & Programmable Life

According to MIT Biological Engineering: The MIT School of Engineering has announced its newest cohort of faculty members for 2026, spanning six academic units and covering a wide range of advanced disciplines including mechanical engineering, aerospace, biological engineering, and quantum computing. These new appointments bring diverse expertise from top institutions, aiming to push boundaries in areas like autonomous systems, computational biology, and quantum error correction. The school also mourned the passing of Professor Thomas Rose, a newly joined materials science expert whose research focused on ancient metallurgy.
