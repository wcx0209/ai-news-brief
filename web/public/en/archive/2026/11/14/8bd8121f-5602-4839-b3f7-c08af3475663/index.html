Historical brief · 2026-11-14

Jensen Huang leaves Nvidia after 33 years

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang has left the company following scandals, marking the end of an era for Big Tech. While analysts say Nvidia's product roadmap and supply chain remain solid through 2027, the company faces a major challenge in finding a successor with his visionary leadership and willingness to make massive long-term bets.

The Verge AI ↗Source published 2026-11-14 20:00 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

The Exit and Its Background

On January 6th, 2025, Jensen Huang made his final high-profile appearance as Nvidia's CEO. Shortly after, he left the company he cofounded 33 years ago amid reports of chip smuggling scandals and personal misconduct. As the leader of the world's most valuable chipmaker, Huang was unique in his absolute centralization of power, with around 60 employees reporting directly to him.

Business and Technology Impact

Industry analysts emphasize that Nvidia's immediate future is not in jeopardy. The company's hardware roadmap and manufacturing partnerships are locked in through 2027, and its supply chain dominance remains unmatched. However, experts note that Huang was irreplaceable as a culture setter and risk-taker who pushed through historical gambles like the CUDA platform, which ultimately powered the modern AI boom.

The Search for a Successor

Finding a new CEO will be difficult because Huang operated without deputies and built the entire organization around himself. Observers believe the company will likely need to look outside for a leader who can maintain investor confidence and protect the CUDA ecosystem during this transitional vacuum.
