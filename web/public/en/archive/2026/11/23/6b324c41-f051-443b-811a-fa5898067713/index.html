Sakana AI launches Applied Research Lab to tackle complex real-world systems

According to Sakana AI: Sakana AI announces a new Applied Research Lab focused on applying its technology to large, complex real-world systems. Its first program targets computational finance and economics, combining human knowledge with AI agents to better model markets and economic activity.

Sakana AI ↗Source published 2026-11-23 23:00 SGTOfficial releaseArtificial Intelligence

According to Sakana AI:

Sakana AI announced the launch of the Sakana AI Applied Research Lab, an internal group dedicated to solving large-scale, complex real-world systems and delivering value to clients. According to Sakana AI, complex systems like markets and economies cannot be resolved by a single AI agent alone, requiring a systematic approach that combines human expertise with AI's capability to implement and test models at scale.

The Lab's first program, Computational Finance and Economics (COFE), looks at both visible asset prices and the underlying real economy. The group builds upon previous company research, including financial benchmarking tools like EDINET-Bench and JFinBench, coffee supply-chain multi-agent simulations like CoffeeBench, and quality-diversity search algorithms like QD-Rec. Key technological foundations include agent-based frameworks and automated research tools such as Shachi and The AI Scientist.

The Lab is based in Tokyo and is actively recruiting interdisciplinary quantitative researchers and infrastructure engineers through its internship and visiting researcher programs, while also welcoming external industrial and academic partners.
