Silver briefly forms a rare crystal structure seen in radioactive polonium

Phys.org Nanotechnology ↗Source published 2026-11-23 04:00 SGTMedia reportAdvanced Materials & Nanotechnology

Hokkaido University researchers discovered that silver nanoparticles can form a simple cubic structure when triggered by an electron beam, mirroring a rare arrangement normally seen in radioactive polonium. This transient silver structure exhibits unusually high chemical reactivity, quickly oxidizing into silver oxide within 100 seconds under high vacuum.
