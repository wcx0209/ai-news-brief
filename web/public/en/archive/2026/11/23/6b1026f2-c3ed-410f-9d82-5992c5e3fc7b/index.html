When Elon Musk Warned About AI Dangers

In 2017, Elon Musk shared a warning on social media by pointing to a 1970 sci-fi film about a runaway supercomputer. Interestingly, years later, he named his own massive AI supercomputer Colossus as a tribute to that movie.

Le Monde AI ↗Source published 2026-11-23 02:00 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

According to Le Monde, Elon Musk posted a message in January 2017 highlighting the ending of the 1970 American film 'Colossus: The Forbin Project,' which depicts a supercomputer taking control of the US nuclear arsenal and imposing a new world order. Years before other tech leaders started voicing fears about losing control over artificial intelligence, Musk was already sounding the alarm. However, the article notes a contradiction, as Musk later named his own company's largest AI supercomputer after the very same film, despite its dystopian themes.
