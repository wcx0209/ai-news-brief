Quantum computers use qubits that exist in superposition, meaning they can represent both 0 and 1 at the same time until measured. These fragile states require temperatures colder than outer space inside dilution refrigerators to prevent disruption, and while they will not replace ordinary laptops, they could transform molecular simulation and break standard encryption.
