US Lawmakers Summon Ex-Nvidia CEO After Spy Scandal

US lawmakers have summoned Nvidia's former CEO to face questions in December following his resignation over extramarital affairs, including one with a suspected Chinese spy. The scandal has wiped nearly half a trillion dollars off Nvidia's market value and triggered national security concerns.

Le Monde AI ↗Source published 2026-11-13 15:37 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

Following the resignation of Nvidia's long-time leader after reports of extramarital affairs, US lawmakers have moved quickly to demand answers. Republican Senator Tim Scott requested that the former CEO and Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick testify before the Senate Banking Committee in December, while three House committees also called for a closed-door interview.

Reports allege that one of the individuals involved was an asset of China's Ministry of State Security, raising serious concerns regarding the protection of sensitive US technology. The unfolding situation has wiped almost half a trillion dollars off Nvidia's market valuation, thrown the company into leadership transition with CFO Colette Kress serving as interim CEO, and left the broader AI industry navigating uncharted political and security waters.
