llama.cpp reaches v1.0.0, establishing a stable API for local AI development

llama.cpp and ggml have released their v1.0.0 versions, marking an important milestone for running artificial intelligence locally across various hardware devices. The project team announces that future updates will strictly follow semantic versioning to protect downstream developers who rely on their libraries for private and cross-platform applications.

Hugging Face ↗Source published 2026-11-13 22:05 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

After three and a half years of growth, the llama.cpp project has become a foundational tool for running multi-modal large language models locally on everything from consumer computers to enterprise servers. With the release of version 1.0.0 for both llama.cpp and its tensor library ggml, the team aims to provide a more reliable foundation for the expanding ecosystem of local AI applications.

To support this growth, the project is establishing strict semantic versioning rules. Any breaking changes to the public C application programming interface will now trigger major version bumps, making interface boundaries clearer for developers who integrate the software into their products. Additionally, the project continues to expand its distribution channels and hardware support, utilizing infrastructure from Hugging Face alongside hardware partnerships with major chipmakers to ensure code quality and broad platform compatibility.
