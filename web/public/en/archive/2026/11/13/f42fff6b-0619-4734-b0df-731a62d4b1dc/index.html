LG Energy Solution scales up ESS strategy to target AI data center power demand

LG Energy Solution is expanding its energy storage system (ESS) business to meet rising power demands driven by renewable energy growth and AI data centers. The company highlights its North American manufacturing capacity, local production footprint, and software solutions as key competitive edges as it prepares to showcase new platforms at RE+ 2026.

Energy-Storage.news ↗Source published 2026-11-13 22:14 SGTMedia reportEnergy & Storage
Artificial Intelligence

As the global energy storage market shifts from small-scale renewable balancing to a critical component of power infrastructure, LG Energy Solution is focusing on grid-scale ESS and next-generation power solutions for AI data centers (AIDCs). Chang Beom Kang, Head of the ESS Battery Division, outlines the company's strategy in a recent Q&A, highlighting how hyperscalers are prioritizing 'Speed to Power' and driving demand for turnkey solutions, uninterruptible power supplies (UPS), and battery backup units (BBUs).

To capture this market, LG Energy Solution is leveraging its North American production network, which includes wholly owned and joint-venture sites, with ESS production capacity in the region expected to reach around 70 GWh by the end of next year. The company is also promoting its AEROS software suite for site optimization and preparing to showcase new products at RE+ 2026, including the JF3 LINK integrated LFP system and a concept combining Solid-State Transformers with sodium-ion batteries.
