Oura's acquisition of Counsel Health raises conflict-of-interest concerns for Eli Lilly

Smart ring maker Oura is acquiring telehealth startup Counsel Health, a platform previously promoted by drugmaker Eli Lilly for GLP-1 prescriptions.

STAT Biotech ↗Source published 2026-11-13 17:30 SGTMedia reportBiotech & Programmable Life

Smart ring maker Oura announced its acquisition of Counsel Health, a telehealth startup that connects patients with clinicians for popular GLP-1 weight-loss drug prescriptions. Previously, pharmaceutical giant Eli Lilly featured Counsel Health on its curated list of provider options for patients seeking these treatments.

Conflict Concerns

According to industry experts, the transaction raises conflict-of-interest questions for Eli Lilly and similar drugmakers. By integrating hardware tracking with direct telehealth diagnosis and prescription services under one corporate umbrella, the acquisition blurs the lines between objective health data collection and commercial treatment pathways, prompting a closer look at how pharmaceutical companies partner with digital health platforms.
