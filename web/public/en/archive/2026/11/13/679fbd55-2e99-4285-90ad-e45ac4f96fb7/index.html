Nvidia processors power Chinese AI data centre projects worth $3 billion despite US export controls: Report

According to a Reuters report, China has developed AI computing projects worth over $3 billion that rely on restricted Nvidia processors like the H100 and H200.

Mint AI ↗Source published 2026-11-13 20:22 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

Project Details According to a Reuters report, multiple regions in China have incorporated restricted Nvidia processors into their AI infrastructure plans. Projects using H100 and H200 chips span across Fujian, Shaanxi, Jilin, Inner Mongolia, and Gansu provinces, with investments ranging from billions of yuan. Some facilities, such as a computing cluster in Wuxi using H800 processors, are already operational, while institutions like Shenzhen University and Northwestern Polytechnical University have also made smaller chip purchases. Potential Impact The heavy reliance on restricted American technology exposes Chinese developers to significant supply disruptions, construction delays, and rising costs as Washington tightens export controls. Furthermore, developers might eventually be forced to redesign their facilities to accommodate domestically produced alternatives.
