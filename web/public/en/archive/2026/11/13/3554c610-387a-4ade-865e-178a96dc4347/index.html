Cohere Expands Sovereign AI Platform with AISLE and cubic

According to Cohere Blog: Cohere announces the completion of its acquisition of AISLE and the onboarding of the team behind cubic. These additions form the core of Cohere's newly established cybersecurity business and secure coding vertical, aimed at helping enterprises and governments deploy AI while retaining full control over data and infrastructure.

Cohere Blog ↗Source published 2026-11-13 22:00 SGTOfficial releaseArtificial Intelligence

According to Cohere Blog:

Background and Acquisitions Cohere states that organizations increasingly require AI systems that are powerful, secure, and under their control, a concept the company refers to as sovereign AI. To advance this goal, Cohere has completed its acquisition of AISLE, a builder of autonomous systems for vulnerability discovery and remediation, and welcomed the team behind cubic, an AI-powered code review platform. These transactions mark Cohere's fourth and fifth strategic M&A deals this year, following Aleph Alpha and Reliant AI. Cybersecurity and Prague Hub Following the acquisition, AISLE co-founders are taking key leadership roles: Ondrej Vlcek becomes Senior Vice President of Cohere Cyber, Jaya Baloo becomes Chief Information Security Officer, and Stanislav Fort becomes Vice President of AI Security. Cohere plans a long-term investment in Prague, establishing the city as its global center for AI cybersecurity and autonomous security systems, leveraging AISLE's expertise to build cyber-resilient AI capabilities. Secure Coding and Enterprise Scale Cubic's team, led by founder and CEO Paul Sanglé-Ferrière, brings expertise in AI, security, and developer tools. Their review agents support environments such as on-premises and air-gapped deployments. According to Cohere, this team will establish the company's secure coding vertical, accelerating the integration of enterprise-grade coding and vulnerability remediation capabilities into its platform.
