Southern Co. considers $100 billion spending plan for natural gas, nuclear, and other investments

Southern Co. executives told investors that the company's five-year capital plan could reach $100 billion, driven by surging demand from data centers and other commercial customers.

Utility Dive ↗Source published 2026-11-13 22:45 SGTMedia reportEnergy & Storage

During a third-quarter earnings call, Southern Co. executives outlined plans that could expand their baseline capital expenditure to $100 billion, up from an earlier $81 billion plan. The company reported a 3.5% year-over-year increase in weather-normalized electricity sales for the third quarter, with commercial sales jumping 7.3%. This growth is heavily anchored by data center usage, which surged 59% during the quarter.

To meet this rising demand, the company has accumulated a portfolio of 21 GW of signed large-load contracts across states like Alabama, Georgia, and Mississippi. Company leaders downplayed concerns that recent midterm election results and political debates over electricity costs would disrupt their plans, emphasizing that their signed agreements include strict minimum-bill provisions ensuring full cost recovery prior to power delivery.
