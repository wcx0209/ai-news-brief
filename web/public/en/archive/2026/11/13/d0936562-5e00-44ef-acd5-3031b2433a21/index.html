AI-related stocks send Taiwanese investors into a frenzy

The global boom in artificial intelligence has made Taiwanese stocks a massive obsession for ordinary citizens. Driven by the critical role of Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company, people from all walks of life are closely tracking markets as chips become the ultimate strategic resource.

Le Monde AI ↗Source published 2026-11-13 03:30 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

Background

In Taiwan, taxi drivers and everyday citizens are increasingly fixated on the stock market every morning as trading begins in Taipei. Although their daily jobs have no connection to high finance, many keep a watchful eye on the performance of Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company to safeguard their personal savings and future. Core Details Local residents often refer to the company as the sacred mountain that protects the country, a sentiment that has intensified with the massive adoption of artificial intelligence. While companies like Nvidia design advanced AI processors in California, the actual physical production relies entirely on TSMC's manufacturing facilities on Taiwan's west coast. Market Impact Having long been essential for producing popular consumer tech products like Apple's iPhone processors, the sudden explosion of the artificial intelligence sector has pushed the company to unprecedented heights. Its market capitalization has surged dramatically over a three-year period, establishing it as a dominant economic force outside the United States.
