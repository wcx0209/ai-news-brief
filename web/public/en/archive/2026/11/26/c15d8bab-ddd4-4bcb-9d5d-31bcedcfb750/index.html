OpenAI combines ChatGPT Chat and Work under a shared limit, worrying Pro users

OpenAI is merging chat and work features into a single interface for Pro subscribers, sharing one usage cap. While heavy users might hit limits faster, the Instant model stays unlimited.

Mint AI ↗Source published 2026-11-26 11:33 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

OpenAI has started rolling out a unified ChatGPT experience to a small group of Pro subscribers, bringing conversational and work-focused features under a shared usage cap. According to product lead Thibault Sottiaux and statements given to TechCrunch, a wider rollout is planned for December 2026. While OpenAI notes that most subscribers will not see frequent interruptions, heavy users who rely heavily on advanced models for both chat and work may exhaust their combined allowance sooner.

This integration aims to make the platform more user-friendly by eliminating the need to switch tabs between different tasks. However, the change has sparked criticism and debate on platforms like X, Reddit, and GitHub, with some subscribers expressing concern over receiving less overall value for the same subscription fee. In response, OpenAI confirmed that conversations using the Instant model will not count toward the shared limit and will remain accessible even after the cap is reached. The move mirrors a broader industry trend, following Anthropic's similar merger of its chatbot and work services in September 2026.
