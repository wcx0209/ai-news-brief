Anthropic researchers shrink Riemann hypothesis proof by 99.5 percent using AI

QbitAI 量子位 ↗Source published 2026-11-26 08:40 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

The original proof, created by mathematician Levent Alpöge and Claude, was so massive that it took over 21 hours to compile on high-end AWS hardware. In contrast, the newly streamlined version builds in just one to two hours on a standard 12-core, 24GB machine and has passed multiple independent verifications.
