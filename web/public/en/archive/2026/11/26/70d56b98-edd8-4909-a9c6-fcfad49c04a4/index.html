XPeng Tech Day 2026: Robotaxi Unmanned Operations and Humanoid Robot IRON Debut

At its recent tech day, XPeng announced that its humanoid robot IRON delivered an unscripted speech, and its second-generation VLA technology is expanding into L4 Robotaxi operations and subscription-based driving assistance. These developments mark a significant shift as the company transitions its core autonomous driving capabilities into broader commercial applications.

iFanr 爱范儿 ↗Source published 2026-11-26 10:25 SGTMedia reportRobotics & Autonomous Systems

XPeng's latest technology showcase highlighted significant advancements across autonomous driving and robotics. The company reported that its humanoid robot IRON walked on stage and completed an unscripted bilingual speech without manual remote control, with hardware mass production slated for January 2027 and initial deployment as store guides expected in the first quarter. On the automotive front, XPeng is extending its second-generation VLA technology to L4 autonomous driving. According to the company, it took just eleven months from business launch to securing L4 demonstration operation qualifications, with public testing already underway and a custom terminal model, YOYO-01, planned for 2027. Additionally, XPeng plans to introduce subscription services for its Turing AI driving system starting in the second quarter of 2027, while pushing out OTA updates that enhance battery charging efficiency and active safety features.
