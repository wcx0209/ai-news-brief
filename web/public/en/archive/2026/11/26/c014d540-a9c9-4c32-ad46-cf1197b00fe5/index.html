Threatened by AI Agents, Indian IT Giants Forge Strategic Partnerships with Anthropic and OpenAI

Faced with the threat of autonomous AI agents disrupting traditional services, Indian IT giants like TCS, Infosys, and Cognizant have formed deep alliances with leading AI labs such as Anthropic and OpenAI. This pivot aims to integrate frontier AI models into enterprise operations and train tens of thousands of employees, turning a potential existential crisis into a new business model.

Analytics India Magazine ↗Source published 2026-11-26 20:58 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

Early in 2026, the rise of autonomous AI agents triggered a major selloff in Indian IT stocks, dubbed the 'SaaSpocalypse', wiping billions in market value as investors feared for the future of outsourced services. To survive and adapt, major Indian IT firms quickly reversed course, partnering with the very AI companies threatening their models.

TCS, HCLTech, Cognizant, and LTM have all forged global partnerships with Anthropic, establishing specialized business units, centers of excellence, and massive employee training programs centered on Claude. Meanwhile, companies like Infosys and TCS have also teamed up with OpenAI to leverage Codex for software engineering and infrastructure development, while Wipro has expanded workflows with ServiceNow and Palo Alto Networks.
