AI can now make customer service calls for you, but the person on the other end is also an AI

Personal AI assistants are starting to handle everyday consumer tasks like negotiating bills and requesting refunds. While this helps people save time, it leads to a new scenario where AI agents negotiate with corporate AI systems on behalf of users.

GeekPark 极客公园 ↗Source published 2026-11-26 17:04 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

As generative AI evolves, companies and startups are exploring how AI agents can interact with the real world. According to Pine AI co-founder and CTO Sun Yurun, consumer AI can retrieve policies and handle administrative complaints that people often abandon due to high time costs. Although AI agents are getting better at managing multi-step workflows, challenges remain in maintaining reliability and knowing when to ask human users for approval. Meanwhile, high-frequency users such as small business owners and contractors are emerging as primary beneficiaries, using AI to handle inbound requests and operational chores.
