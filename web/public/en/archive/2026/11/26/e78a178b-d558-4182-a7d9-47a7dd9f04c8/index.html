Snapmaker U1 Brings Purge-Free, Four-Toolhead Printing to the Desktop for Under $900

According to a review by 3D Printing Industry, the Snapmaker U1 is a $899 desktop 3D printer featuring four independent toolheads. Unlike traditional multi-color systems that waste large amounts of plastic through purging, the U1 swaps entire hotends to eliminate color bleed and enable mixed-material prints.

3D Printing Industry ↗Source published 2026-11-26 22:11 SGTMedia reportAdvanced Manufacturing & Industrial Infrastructure

The Snapmaker U1 is a CoreXY desktop fabrication machine equipped with four independent toolheads that dock at the rear of the frame. According to the review, the machine achieved the highest funding record in Kickstarter history by raising $20.6 million from over 20,000 backers, with retail units launching at $899 in April 2026.

During extensive testing by the 3DPI engineering team, the U1 scored an impressive 92.05 on their benchmark suite. The hardware features carbon fiber X-axis rails, a 270 x 270 x 270 mm build volume, and the SnapSwap kinematic coupling system which completes tool changes in about five seconds. Process capability tests showed exceptional precision with a Cp of 6.94 and Cpk of 3.59, though minor software instability and a systematic offset in measurements were noted during the evaluation.
