China's Energy Storage Shifts from Scale Expansion to Value Realization

China has released a new implementation plan for energy storage, shifting its focus from rapid capacity expansion to market-driven value realization. As installed capacity has surged ahead of schedule, the industry now faces spatial mismatches and unclear revenue mechanisms, prompting a push toward independent regulation resources and rational pricing.

China Energy Network Storage 中国能源网 ↗Source published 2026-11-26 09:42 SGTMedia reportEnergy & Storage

The newly issued implementation plan sets a goal for China's energy storage to reach around 300 million kilowatts by 2030, with an average duration of about 3 hours. Although nationwide installed capacity has exceeded 160 million kilowatts, the industry struggles with two main challenges: a spatial mismatch between where storage is built and where power regulation is actually needed, and difficulties in securing stable revenues due to narrow spot price spreads and incomplete capacity tariff mechanisms. To solve this, the new policy prioritizes diverse application scenarios, links capacity compensation with supply-guarantee responsibilities, and encourages regional pilots like capacity subsidies and flexible market trading to ensure storage is deployed where it is most effective.
