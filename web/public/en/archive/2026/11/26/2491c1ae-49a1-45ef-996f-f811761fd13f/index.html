Zelestra and Statkraft Sign 8-Year Battery Storage Deal in Italy

Energy companies Zelestra and Statkraft report signing an 8-year agreement for a large battery storage project in Italy. This deal highlights how private bilateral contracts are growing alongside government auctions as companies look for reliable energy storage options.

Energy-Storage.news ↗Source published 2026-11-26 23:03 SGTMedia reportEnergy & Storage

According to Zelestra, the 8-year physical tolling agreement covers the entire capacity of its 175MW/700MWh battery system in Central Italy, which is scheduled to start commercial operations in 2028. This marks Statkraft's first storage tolling agreement in Southern Europe and Zelestra's fourth in Italy.

The announcement follows Italy's second MACSE government auction managed by transmission operator Terna, which procured 16GWh of storage capacity. Eliano Russo, CEO of Zelestra Italy, reports that a parallel private bilateral market is developing to reward solid projects as the regulated market advances.
