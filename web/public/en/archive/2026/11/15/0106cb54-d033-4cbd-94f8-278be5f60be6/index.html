Lynxel launches two LMS resin 3D printers supporting high viscosity

According to Nanjixiong, startup Lynxel has introduced a layer-membrane stereolithography platform alongside the LynBeam LB1 and LynChrom LC1 3D printers. These machines are designed to process resins with high viscosity up to 300,000 cP and will launch on Kickstarter.

Background and Technology

According to Nanjixiong, tree-dimensional printing startup Lynxel has developed a layer-membrane stereolithography (LMS) platform. Traditional desktop resin printers often struggle with thick formulas because they rely on passive fluid flow. The LMS platform uses an active moving film to spread and feed resin, which helps control separation forces and allows the equipment to handle standard resins as well as higher viscosity, flexible, and composite materials up to 300,000 cP.

Printer Models and Features

The company is preparing two initial devices for a Kickstarter pre-sale campaign, though final pricing and delivery timelines are not yet disclosed. The LynBeam LB1 acts as the primary functional model with a build volume of 200×400×300 mm. Meanwhile, the LynChrom LC1 incorporates an integrated CMYK inkjet system to perform direct full-color surface printing during the build process, reducing the need for manual painting afterward.

Software and Exhibition

The companion software, Lynxel Studio, handles model preparation and includes an AI assistant named Lenny. Lynxel plans to demonstrate the LMS platform and display physical samples at the upcoming Formnext 2026 exhibition in Frankfurt.
