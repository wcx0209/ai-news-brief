How ChatGPT helped trace a Maharashtra woman missing for 2.5 years and reunite her with family

A 45-year-old woman in India who had been missing for two and a half years was finally reunited with her family after social workers used ChatGPT and Google Search to piece together fragmented clues and track down her hometown.

Mint AI ↗Source published 2026-11-15 22:25 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

Background

Julekha Mukheed Shaikh, a 45-year-old woman suffering from mental distress, went missing and was later admitted to an ashram in Sindhudurg district, Maharashtra, following a court order in April 2024.

Details

During her stay, social workers from Jeevan Aanand Sanstha tried repeatedly to uncover her background. By combining personal interactions with digital tools like Google Search and ChatGPT, they analyzed localized information to pinpoint her native place in Parbhani district and coordinated with local police to contact her relatives.

Impact

This case highlights how AI chatbots can assist in humanitarian efforts by processing scattered details to reconnect separated families, echoing similar recent instances involving missing persons in India and abroad.
