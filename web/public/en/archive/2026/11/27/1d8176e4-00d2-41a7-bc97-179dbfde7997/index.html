Historical brief · 2026-11-27

GTA VI makes billions without AI as Anthropic goes public

As Anthropic celebrates its massive IPO, Grand Theft Auto VI has broken entertainment records by making $3.79 billion on its first day. Developed entirely without generative AI over 13 years, the game highlights how human inefficiency and meticulous craftsmanship can create cultural masterpieces that automated tools struggle to replicate.

TechCrunch AI ↗Source published 2026-11-27 00:00 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

The Dual Milestones

As the tech industry witnessed Anthropic’s historic $81.3 billion IPO, the gaming world experienced a parallel blockbuster moment with the release of *Grand Theft Auto VI*. According to Business Wire, Rockstar Games' new title shattered box-office records by pulling in $3.79 billion on day one, surpassing massive hits like *Avengers: Endgame* and the Eras tour.

The Cost of Craftsmanship

Unlike the efficiency-driven tech landscape where Large Language Models instantly spit out content, GTA VI was built over a decade by roughly 5,000 people without generative AI. Former Take-Two Interactive executive Ben Feder noted that creative masterpieces require extensive deletion and trial and error, a process that is inherently inefficient and expensive yet yields a level of meticulous detail and human intention that automated tools cannot match.

Industry Implications

While tech leaders like Elon Musk and Mark Zuckerberg envision a future where AI generates personalized video games and content instantly, the massive cultural resonance of GTA VI proves that audiences still crave artistic friction and worlds crafted with slow, deliberate human care. In an era dominated by automated speed, GTA VI succeeds precisely because it forces players to slow down and relish the mundane.
