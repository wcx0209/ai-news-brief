Envision GEN 8 Energy Storage System Wins Best Hardware Product at Energy Storage Awards

Envision reports that its GEN 8 Skid-10100 energy storage system has won the Best Hardware Product award at the Energy Storage Awards 2026 in London. The system integrates power conversion, boosting, and distribution in a standard container, offering grid-forming capabilities and environmental adaptability for large-scale storage projects.

China Energy Network Storage 中国能源网 ↗Source published 2026-11-27 13:41 SGTMedia reportEnergy & Storage

Envision announced that its GEN 8 Skid-10100 10MW AC medium-voltage conversion and boosting integrated machine won the 'Best Hardware Product' award at the Energy Storage Awards 2026 held in London, an event established by Solar Media.

According to Envision, the product integrates four 2.52MW liquid-cooled power conversion systems, a medium-voltage transformer, and a ring main unit into a standard container size, reducing site deployment time and land footprint. The system features short-term overload, short-circuit current support, and inertia support, and is designed to operate in temperatures ranging from -30°C to 60°C with IP65 protection and C5 anti-corrosion levels. Envision stated that its technology has been applied in scenarios such as a black-start test in Mongolia and an off-grid new energy power system in Chifeng. The company reported that its cumulative energy storage shipments have exceeded 80GWh across more than 20 countries.
