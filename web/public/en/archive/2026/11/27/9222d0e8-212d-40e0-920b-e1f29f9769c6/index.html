Continued operating losses for Narada Power as battery OEM falls to lowest Bankability rating

According to PV Tech Research, China-based battery manufacturer Narada Power has fallen to the lowest possible rating in the latest Bankability report. Facing continuous operating losses and severe liquidity issues, the company's financial score dropped to 0, raising concerns over its future warranty support for massive global battery deployments.

Energy-Storage.news ↗Source published 2026-11-27 19:20 SGTMedia reportEnergy & Storage

According to PV Tech Research, China-based battery manufacturer Narada Power has experienced a severe financial downturn, resulting in a 0 financial score and a C bankability rating in the latest Battery StorageTech Bankability Ratings Report. PV Tech analyst Charlotte Gisbourne noted that the company has reported operating losses every quarter since late 2025, culminating in an operating margin of -137.5% in the third quarter of 2026.

This financial pressure has driven Narada's negative working capital to approximately RMB 4.46 billion, while its lithium-ion battery manufacturing capacity utilisation rate plummeted to just 22% in the first half of 2026, compared to over 90% for top-tier competitors like CATL and Sungrow. Consequently, the company's share price on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange has dropped 86% since the beginning of the year. Industry observers are closely monitoring the situation, as the potential failure of a major BESS supplier could jeopardize long-term performance warranties for tens of gigawatt-hours of previously deployed systems.
