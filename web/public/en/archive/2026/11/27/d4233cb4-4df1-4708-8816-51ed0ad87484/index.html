UK Plant-Based Protein Water Startup Nowhey Secures Funding for Expansion

UK startup Nowhey has secured pre-seed funding to expand its plant-based protein drinks and powders. The brand uses pea protein to create clear, juice-like drinks that offer an alternative to traditional, heavy whey protein shakes and help avoid dairy digestive issues.

Green Queen ↗Source published 2026-11-27 16:00 SGTMedia reportAgriculture & Food Technology

Background and Product Details

British startup Nowhey was founded by school friends and athletes Luis Evitt and Jesse Bate. Evitt suffers from a whey intolerance, which inspired the duo to create lighter alternatives to dairy protein. Their drinks use pea protein peptides combined with natural ingredients and flavorings. According to the company, each 330ml can or scoop provides 20g to 25.4g of protein, utilizing pea protein which has a 94% digestibility rate and contains all essential amino acids.

Market Context and Growth Drivers

The funding round includes contributions from investors such as Newcastle United CEO David Hopkinson and entrepreneur Karnika E Yashwant. This investment comes amid a global boom in protein demand, driven by shifts away from ultra-processed foods, changing dietary preferences, and the rise of GLP-1 weight-loss drugs which can cause muscle loss and spur a need for more protein. Additionally, traditional whey protein markets are currently struggling with high prices and supply issues, prompting more brands to explore animal-free alternatives.
