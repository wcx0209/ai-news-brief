Europe Leads in Public Funding for Alternative Proteins, But China Is Coming for Its R&D Crown

A new report from the Good Food Institute Europe reveals that Europe leads global public funding and research output in alternative proteins, committing €1.27 billion between 2020 and 2025. However, China is rapidly closing the gap with surging research publications and patent families, driven by strong government policy support and robust biomanufacturing capabilities.

Funding and Research Landscape

According to an analysis by the Good Food Institute (GFI) Europe, Europe committed a total of €1.27 billion ($1.4 billion) to alternative protein R&D between 2020 and 2025, with the European Commission acting as the single largest public funder. Europe currently holds 30% of the world's researchers in this sector and has historically produced more studies than any other region. However, its innovation ecosystem is being outpaced by China, whose research output grew by 92% from 2023 to 2025, compared to Europe's 60% growth during the same period.

Patents and Institutional Strengths

While Europe leads in total cumulative patent filings, China dominates in unique patent families, filing 2,147 families since 2023 compared to Europe's 1,451. A structural dichotomy exists: 92% of unique patents in Europe and the US come from the private sector, whereas nearly 57% of Chinese patents originate from research institutions or collaborations. Furthermore, half of the top 10 productive research institutes globally are located in China, highlighting its robust institutional R&D prowess.

Strategic Implications

China's future food ecosystem is bolstered by political support and the repurposing of its pharmaceutical manufacturing network to tackle projected protein shortfalls. The report warns that Europe's public funding is not sufficiently targeted toward moving technologies out of the lab and into commercial deployment, meaning Europe risks losing out in building domestic capabilities in bioindustries despite its homegrown scientific excellence.
