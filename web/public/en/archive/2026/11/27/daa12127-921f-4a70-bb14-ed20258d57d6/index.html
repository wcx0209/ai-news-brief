TCT Editors Map 24 Defining Moments in 3D Printing

TCT editors are rolling out a countdown of 24 pivotal milestones that shaped the 3D printing industry. Running throughout December like an advent calendar, the series highlights key historical turning points and unexpected wild cards in additive manufacturing.

TCT Magazine ↗Source published 2026-11-27 14:00 SGTMedia reportAdvanced Manufacturing & Industrial Infrastructure

According to TCT Magazine, editors have put together a curated list of 24 defining moments to trace how the additive manufacturing industry evolved into its current state. The selection is not meant to be an exhaustive catalogue of every invention, but rather a thematic advent calendar where one defining moment, including a few wild cards, is published each day leading up to December 24th. The publication invites readers to share their own perspectives and picks via LinkedIn or email as the series rolls out.
