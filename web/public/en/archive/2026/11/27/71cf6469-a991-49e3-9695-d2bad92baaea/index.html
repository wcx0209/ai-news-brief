EngineAI Open-Sources Robot Brains and Muscles with a 1 Billion Yuan Ecosystem Fund

At its 2026 developer conference, EngineAI announced the open-sourcing of its core brain and cerebellum technologies to support global developers. The company reported that increasing human motion data from 5,000 to 50,000 hours boosted its embodied foundation model's task success rate from 56.7% to 90%.

Background

EngineAI founder and CEO Zhao Tongyang notes that 2027 will mark a critical transition year for humanoid robots moving from mass production to commercial deployment. Because no single company can master the unique demands of thousands of different industries, EngineAI believes that the next phase of growth relies heavily on ecosystem collaboration.

Core Technologies and Open Source

EngineAI has officially opened up its self-developed brain and cerebellum assets. On the brain side, the company shared its human-like brain architecture EngineAI Awaken, along with a 50,000-hour human motion dataset that significantly improved the base model's task success rate. On the cerebellum side, EngineAI released its behavior foundation model EngineAI-BFM, covering agile and general motor control across various robot form factors.

Ecosystem Support and CoRun Mechanism

To help developers overcome the steep barriers to entry in robotics, EngineAI launched the CoRun collaborative development mechanism, allowing validation results from one team to become the starting point for another. Backed by a 1 billion yuan industry support fund over the next five years, the initiative covers developer resources, academic research, talent cultivation, and commercial partnerships to foster a sustainable network effect.
