GXO Doubling Down on Logistics Robots and Humanoids for 2027

GXO Logistics plans to accelerate deployments of adaptive technologies across U.S. facilities in 2027. The rollout features pallet AMRs, robotic arms, AI vision, and humanoids like Apptronik's Apollo, aimed at boosting efficiency and optimizing labor.

The Robot Report ↗Source published 2026-11-27 21:56 SGTMedia reportRobotics & Autonomous Systems

GXO Logistics announced plans to expand its automation footprint across the U.S. starting in 2027, focusing on adaptive technologies such as pallet AMRs, multi-application robotic arms, AI-enabled vision systems, and automated sortation. According to Michael Jacobs, CEO of the Americas & Asia Pacific, this phased rollout aims to deliver immediate value and long-term growth.

Beyond traditional warehouse automation, GXO continues to lean heavily into humanoids. The company is working with Apptronik toward a global rollout of up to 1,000 Apollo humanoids over four years, following tests showing 96% task efficiency. Additionally, GXO is deploying Agility Robotics' Digit humanoids in North Carolina and Tennessee, building on past successes where Digit moved over 100,000 totes in Georgia. Meanwhile, GXO utilizes non-humanoid systems like Exotec's ASRS for fashion fulfillment and Dexory's inventory robots, though the company's CEO acknowledged in August that humanoids have yet to produce a definitive return on investment.
