US approves Elon Musk's plan to launch one million satellites for space AI datacentres

The US government has approved Elon Musk's SpaceX to launch one million Starmind satellites to build data centres in space. Supporters claim this ambitious project will help reduce the environmental impact of the booming AI industry on Earth, though experts are examining how realistic the plan truly is.

The Guardian AI ↗Source published 2026-11-27 02:34 SGTMedia reportSpace Economy
Artificial Intelligence

According to The Guardian's reporting, US federal regulators under the Trump administration have authorized SpaceX to launch one million Starmind orbital satellites. The primary goal of these satellites is to process data directly in space, shifting computational workloads away from the ground.

SpaceX argues that moving AI infrastructure off-planet could significantly alleviate the heavy energy and environmental burdens that terrestrial data centres impose as the artificial intelligence sector rapidly expands. However, the podcast discussion raises critical questions regarding the practical feasibility of such a massive orbital undertaking and what potential consequences it might bring to near-Earth space and global infrastructure.
