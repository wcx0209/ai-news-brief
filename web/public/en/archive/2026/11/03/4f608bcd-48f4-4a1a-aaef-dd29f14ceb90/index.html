EU launches call for proposals to build AI media platform

According to European Commission Digital Strategy: The European Union is seeking proposals to build a modular multilingual platform for news and media. This initiative aims to create public-interest AI infrastructure, helping European news organizations adopt advanced technology to serve diverse language communities effectively.

European Commission Digital Strategy ↗Source published 2026-11-03 18:49 SGTOfficial releaseArtificial Intelligence

According to European Commission Digital Strategy:

Background

The European Union has officially opened a call for proposals focused on creating an AI-powered media platform. This project is designed specifically for European news and audiovisual media organizations. Details The goal is to develop a modular and multilingual infrastructure that serves the public interest. By leveraging this system, media outlets can overcome language barriers and streamline content sharing across borders within the European region. Impact This move represents a strategic effort by the EU to support its domestic media ecosystem through public infrastructure, ensuring that local news providers can harness the power of artificial intelligence while maintaining high standards of public service.
