How to build a knowledge factory for AI agents

According to LangChain Blog: According to LangChain CEO Harrison Chase, making AI work for companies isn't about better models, but better knowledge. Companies need to collect, curate, and maintain tacit business knowledge using a knowledge factory system rather than relying on manual updates.

LangChain Blog ↗Source published 2026-11-22 00:45 SGTOfficial releaseArtificial Intelligence

According to LangChain Blog:

Harrison Chase explains that while general knowledge work is increasingly being handled by AI agents, these tools lack the tacit, scattered, and implicit knowledge that efficient human employees possess. To solve this, companies need to build a knowledge factory to collect, curate, and maintain essential business information.

According to LangChain, the knowledge factory consists of three core components: skills for procedural execution, domain knowledge, and data access guidance. Instead of maintaining this information manually, companies should deploy a curator agent that runs in the background to analyze agent traces, review flagged issues, and update knowledge bases automatically, creating a continuous improvement loop.
