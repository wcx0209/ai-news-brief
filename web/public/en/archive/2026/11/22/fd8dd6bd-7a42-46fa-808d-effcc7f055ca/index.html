Siemens Connects AI Agents with Additive Manufacturing for End-to-End Digital Threads

Siemens Digital Industries Software is developing an end-to-end digital thread for additive manufacturing driven by industrial AI agents. Partnering with AGIBOT, EOS, and AM Solutions, the system uses natural language prompts to automate design, simulation, and manufacturing workflows, slashing custom part design cycles from weeks to about one day.

Artificial Intelligence × Robotics & Autonomous Systems

Siemens Digital Industries Software reports that it is integrating AI agents and prompt-driven engineering with adaptive production environments for additive manufacturing. In a demonstration developed with humanoid robot maker AGIBOT, operators can use natural language to define custom robotic gripper requirements. An AI agent workflow automatically handles configuration matching, parameter adjustments in Siemens Designcenter, structural simulations, and manufacturing data export via Model Context Protocol (MCP) integrations.

To bridge design and physical production, Siemens partnered with EOS and AM Solutions to build an adaptive manufacturing unit at the Erlangen electronics plant. This setup integrates 3D printing, automated material handling, and post-processing steps into a continuous workflow to handle high-mix, low-volume custom parts. Siemens also introduced the AM I Navigator tool to help companies adopt these industrial AI capabilities step-by-step.
