Dawa Technology's AI Video Strategy: Focus on Control Rather Than Rendering

Dawa Technology reports a cumulative financing of about 500 million RMB and adopts a control-over-rendering approach for AI video generation. By separating the control layer from the rendering layer and utilizing structured process data from real film sets, the company aims to reduce trial-and-error costs and enable real-time world models on consumer-grade hardware.

GeekPark 极客公园 ↗Source published 2026-11-22 10:03 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

Dawa Technology, headquartered in Yongchuan, Chongqing, originated from a tech-oriented film studio operation and transitioned into AI models. The company recently completed its Pre-A and A round financings, totaling around 500 million RMB, and reports an annual ARR of about 100 million USD.

Addressing the high computation and trial-and-error costs of mainstream video generation models, Dawa proposes a 'control layer versus rendering layer' architecture. According to CTO Zhu Ziwei, the rendering layer acts like a camera to output pixels, while the control layer acts like a director, understanding physical spaces, actors, lighting, and camera movements. This setup is powered by approximately 180TB of structured process data collected from real film shoots, recording the entire production timeline rather than just final pixels.

To address computational limitations, CPO Li Zongxing notes that the company developed a sparse diffusion autoregressive architecture that skips unchanged areas in a scene, allowing real-time execution at 720P on consumer-grade single GPUs. Dawa's current offerings include FilmOS for professional offline generation and Scenes AI for real-time interactive world modeling, with future expansions targeted toward live-streaming and interactive gaming.
