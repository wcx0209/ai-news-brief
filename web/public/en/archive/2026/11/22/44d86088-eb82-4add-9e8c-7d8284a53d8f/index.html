Zeekr's battery arm ranks 8th nationwide in installations

According to the China Automotive Power Battery Industry Innovation Alliance, Zeekr's battery division reached 16.48GWh in domestic installations from January to October 2026, rising to 8th place nationwide. Meanwhile, its cumulative cell shipments for the Brick Battery surpassed 100 million units.

China Energy Network Storage 中国能源网 ↗Source published 2026-11-22 12:16 SGTMedia reportEnergy & Storage

According to data from the China Automotive Power Battery Industry Innovation Alliance, Zeekr's battery business entity, Jiyaotongxing, reported a domestic power battery installation volume of 16.48GWh from January to October 2026, advancing to the 8th spot in the industry. Within the new energy passenger vehicle segment, its installations reached 16.33GWh, ranking 6th. Specifically, pure electric passenger vehicle installations climbed to 6th place, while plug-in hybrids secured 7th place.

In terms of manufacturing scale, Jiyaotongxing announced that cumulative shipments of its Brick Battery cells exceeded 100 million units by October 2026, adding over 20 million units in about four months. The company stated that the product maintains a record of zero major safety incidents and is supplied to nearly 108 countries and regions.

Following Geely Holding Group's strategic integration of its battery business in April 2025, Jiyaotongxing was established as a first-tier group headquartered in Zhejiang Quzhou. The company has laid out 10 intelligent manufacturing bases and 3 research institutes across 10 cities, with a planned total capacity reaching 150GWh upon completion.
