Opposition to data centers grows worldwide, from India to Chile, via Ireland and Japan

As the AI boom drives a surge in demand, data centers are facing growing resistance worldwide. Residents in countries from India to Chile and Scotland are protesting against these massive facilities due to concerns over excessive energy consumption, natural resource depletion, and rising electricity bills.

Le Monde AI ↗Source published 2026-11-22 01:26 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

According to Le Monde, massive data centers fueling the artificial intelligence revolution are triggering a global backlash that extends far beyond the United States. In places like Scotland, thousands recently demonstrated against the proposed construction of over 20 mega data centers, prompting local governments to mandate stricter environmental impact assessments. Residents globally are increasingly worried that these electricity-hungry and resource-intensive facilities offer limited local job creation while putting severe pressure on local power grids and natural resources.
