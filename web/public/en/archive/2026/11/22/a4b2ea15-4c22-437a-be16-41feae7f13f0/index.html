Historical brief · 2026-11-22

Tencent worked with international groups to safety-test its latest open-weight AI model Hy4

Tencent reported that it teamed up with international artificial intelligence safety organisations to assess the risk level of its new open-weight model, Hy4, prior to release. According to the company, this marks the first time a major Chinese tech firm has disclosed using independent third-party evaluators to test a new model.

SCMP China Tech ↗Source published 2026-11-22 21:30 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

According to an 82-byte safety report published by Tencent Holdings, the company collaborated with Chinese start-up Fangcun AI, Israeli-American firm Alice, and Singaporean entities including Neo Research to evaluate its open-weight model, Hy4. Tencent stated this is the first instance of a leading Chinese technology company disclosing third-party safety testing before releasing a model.

Evaluators from Alice reported that Hy4 demonstrated marked improvements in cyber defence capabilities compared to its previous version, while maintaining limited hacking abilities. Fangcun AI's evaluations indicated low rates of deception and loss of control. Regarding biological safety, Tencent reported that while Hy4 scored three times higher on a wet-lab experimental design test than its preview version, the company found no evidence that it significantly lowers the barrier to carrying out biological harm, as it still struggles with practical laboratory execution. These biological findings were based on Tencent's internal benchmarks rather than third-party reviews.
