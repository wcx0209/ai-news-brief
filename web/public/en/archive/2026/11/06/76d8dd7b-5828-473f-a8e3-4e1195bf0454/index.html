Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash brings 15-second videos and lower prices

According to Google AI: Google has launched Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash, its updated AI video generation model that now supports 15-second clips and costs 30% less than the previous version. The new model offers better reference consistency, conversational editing, and is now generally available across developer and enterprise platforms.

Google AI ↗Source published 2026-11-06 01:00 SGTOfficial releaseArtificial Intelligence

According to Google AI:

Google is rolling out Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash, bringing significant upgrades to its AI video generation tool. The new version supports native 15-second video creation, up from 10 seconds, and allows users to specify durations in one-second increments.

Pricing and Workflow

Google has dropped the price by 30%, bringing it down to $0.07 per second for 720p video. The updated workflow lets users draft quickly at lower resolutions, fix specific details through follow-up edits, and upscale the final results to 1080p or 4K.

Key Features and Availability

The model introduces enhanced reference adherence, allowing characters, products, and styles to stay consistent across different camera angles. Users can also perform conversational editing turn by turn, altering backgrounds or lighting without starting from scratch. The tool is now generally available through the Gemini API, Google Cloud, and consumer apps like Google Vids and the Gemini app.
