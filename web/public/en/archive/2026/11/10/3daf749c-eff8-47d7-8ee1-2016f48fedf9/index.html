Beam Therapeutics reports positive trial results for its CRISPR sickle cell treatment

STAT BiotechBiotech & Programmable LifeProgrammable LifeSource published2026-11-10 22:38 SGT
Media reportStage not established

Beam Therapeutics revealed that its experimental CRISPR-based therapy for sickle cell disease, called risto-cel, achieved the primary goal in a pivotal clinical trial by keeping patients free from severe pain crises for at least a full year. Although detailed data have not yet been publicly disclosed, the company is moving forward quickly with regulatory plans. Beam stated it intends to submit an FDA marketing application for the therapy as early as the end of this year, marking a significant milestone for its gene-editing pipeline.
