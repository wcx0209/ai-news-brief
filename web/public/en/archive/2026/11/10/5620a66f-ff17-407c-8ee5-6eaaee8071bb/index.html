Anthropic Launches Roadshow for IPO

According to Anthropic News: AI startup Anthropic has officially kicked off the roadshow for its initial public offering, planning to trade on the Nasdaq under the symbol ANT. The company aims to price its shares between $70.00 and $78.00, marking a massive milestone for the generative AI industry as it heads toward the public market.

Anthropic News ↗Source published 2026-11-10 08:00 SGTOfficial releaseArtificial Intelligence

According to Anthropic News:

Offering Details Anthropic is proposing to offer a total of 825,000,000 shares of Class A common stock, with the bulk coming directly from the company and a portion from existing stockholders. Additionally, underwriters have been granted a 30-day option to buy more shares. Market Debut The company has applied to list on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the ticker ANT. While the registration statement has been filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission, it is not yet effective, meaning shares cannot be sold just yet.
