D-Wave Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results with 21% Revenue Growth and Expanded Global Partnerships

D-Wave Quantum Inc. announced its financial and business results for the third quarter of 2026, showing continued commercial momentum alongside heavy investments in next-generation quantum technologies. During the quarter, the company generated $4.5 million in revenue, marking a 21% increase compared to the same period in 2025. Total bookings for the first nine months of 2026 surged to $37.4 million, representing a 611% year-over-year increase, while remaining performance obligations reached $38.3 million. On the technical and partnership fronts, D-Wave finalized an agreement to access up to $100 million in funding from the U.S. CHIPS and Science Act to advance both its annealing and gate-model quantum computers. Internationally, the company delivered Italy's first commercial annealing quantum system to Como and expanded enterprise applications, such as NTT DOCOMO deploying a second production hybrid quantum application that significantly optimized mobile network signaling loads.
