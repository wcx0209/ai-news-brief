Meta Announces Low-Cost AI Model Muse Ember

Meta has expanded its Muse AI family with Muse Ember, targeting high-volume, repetitive agentic tasks such as tool calling, coding assistance, information extraction, and document analysis. Featuring a context window of up to one million tokens, it handles text, images, audio, video, and PDFs. According to Artificial Analysis, Ember achieved a score of 36, closely approaching Spark 1.3's 37, while reducing per-task costs from $2.39 to $0.14. Ember is available immediately through the Meta Model API and Muse Code, priced at $0.10 per million input tokens and $0.50 per million output tokens.
