Historical brief · 2026-11-10

Three Central Asian countries strengthen water monitoring in the Isfara river basin

According to International Water Management Institute: The International Water Management Institute (IWMI) has handed over monitoring equipment to Tajikistan, Kyrgyzstan, and Uzbekistan through the Blue Peace Central Asia initiative.

International Water Management Institute ↗Source published 2026-11-10 10:30 SGTOfficial releaseClimate, Water & Environmental Engineering

According to International Water Management Institute:

Background and Challenges

The transboundary Isfara River Basin, flowing through Tajikistan, Kyrgyzstan, and Uzbekistan, faces significant challenges due to aging infrastructure and a lack of routine data exchange. A July 2026 assessment by Blue Peace Central Asia revealed that roughly 55% of hydrological monitoring posts need reconstruction following damage from floods and mudflows.

Equipment and Technical Support

IWMI provided portable electromagnetic flow meters, electric field vehicles, computers, and printers to local water organizations in Isfara, Batken, and Beshariq. These tools aim to enhance water discharge measurements and reduce reliance on damaged legacy instruments.

Institutional Cooperation

Beyond hardware, the initiative includes technical training in hydrometry and community participation. Representatives from the three countries are scheduled to meet in December 2026 to discuss data-sharing arrangements and set joint basin planning priorities for 2027.
