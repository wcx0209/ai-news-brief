Smartphone Chips Trapped in AI Boom

TMTPost 钛媒体Artificial IntelligenceBusinessSource published2026-11-10 10:43 SGT
Media reportStage not established

Cost and Shipment Squeeze Recent industry data shows global smartphone shipments dropping while total revenue and average selling prices rise. This price hike is partly driven by component cost inflation rather than purely consumer demand for premium models. Processor and SoC shipments have contracted, hitting mid-to-low-tier products hardest. AI Infrastructure Pressure AI data center expansion is soaking up memory and semiconductor manufacturing resources, keeping mobile DRAM supply tight. Meanwhile, phone chips face higher fabrication and packaging costs, leaving brands caught between rising retail prices and reluctant consumers. Shifting Horizons While phone chips struggle with consumer demand, chipmakers like MediaTek and Qualcomm are aggressively expanding into data center AI accelerators, leveraging mobile low-power design expertise to find growth outside traditional handset markets.
