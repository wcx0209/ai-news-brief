Why Agoda Teams Up with ANA and Hoshino Resorts in Japan

Background Agoda, a major player owned by Booking Holdings, has long tried to crack the Japanese accommodation market. While globally dominant, Japan presents unique cultural and operational hurdles that foreign platforms struggle to navigate independently. Strategy and Partnerships To overcome these barriers, Agoda is actively collaborating with trusted domestic brands like ANA and Hoshino Resorts. By integrating its robust technology and global user base with local expertise, Agoda aims to capture the specific demands of Japanese domestic travelers who heavily prefer localized payment methods, loyalty programs, and customer service standards. Industry Impact This collaboration reflects a broader trend where international tech giants must humble themselves and leverage local ecosystems to succeed in highly unique regional markets like Japan. It also pressures other foreign OTAs to rethink their standalone strategies.
