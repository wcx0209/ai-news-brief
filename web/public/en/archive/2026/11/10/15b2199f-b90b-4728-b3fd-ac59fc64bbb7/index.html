Adobe brings AI agents and new creative tools to Photoshop, Premiere, and Firefly

Adobe announced a massive wave of AI updates for its core software suite—including Photoshop, Premiere, Illustrator, and InDesign—at its MAX event in Miami. The updates introduce an always-on AI agent designed to understand user workflows and offer proactive assistance for quick tasks like background removal. In addition to application updates, Adobe's Firefly app is expanding with a prototyping tool called Builder to compete with Figma, alongside smart video assembly and editing features. Adobe is also releasing a local MCP (Model Context Protocol), allowing third-party AI assistants like ChatGPT and Claude to tap into Creative Cloud capabilities, though industry figures debate the open standard's balance of benefits.
