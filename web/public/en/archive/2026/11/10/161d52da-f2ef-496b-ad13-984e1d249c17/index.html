Ring is finally launching its flying indoor security drone

How It Works The new Ring Drone Cam is an autonomous indoor drone designed to replace multiple stationary security cameras. Powered by a new AI system called AI Pilot, it flies along preprogrammed routes when triggered by home sensors like motion detectors or door openings. It features a 2K camera with a 148-degree field of view and weighs just 165 grams. Development and Privacy Ring has spent years developing the device, finally redesigning it to rely on cloud processing to keep the price down. To address privacy concerns, the drone only records while flying, remains shuttered in its dock, and uses a rotating encryption system for its footage. However, it relies heavily on an internet connection and only offers a short flight time of five to eight minutes per charge.
