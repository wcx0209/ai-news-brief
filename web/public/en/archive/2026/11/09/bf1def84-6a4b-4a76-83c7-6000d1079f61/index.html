Jensen Huang's Personal Scandal, Mystery Partner, and $4.8B Chip Business

The Mystery of Alice Huang and Megaspeed The scandal centers on Alice Huang, whose career spans state media, investment banking, and game capital networks before she took the helm of Megaspeed. Originally a 2015-registered overseas entity connected to gaming, Megaspeed transformed into an AI cloud infrastructure provider in 2023 just as U.S. export controls tightened. Despite starting with modest cash reserves, the company's Malaysian affiliates managed to acquire billions in advanced GPUs, making it one of Nvidia's largest regional partners. Corporate Governance and Board Investigation What began as rumors escalated when U.S. lawmakers demanded answers regarding Nvidia's security and information sharing. Facing intense pressure, Huang issued a public apology for hurting his family, prompting Nvidia's board to establish a special committee to investigate whether his personal relationship created undisclosed conflicts of interest or influenced corporate decision-making. Political Fallout in Washington The controversy erupted right around the U.S. midterm elections, quickly drawing bipartisan attention. While hardline Republicans pushed to investigate export and security risks, Democrats targeted Nvidia's cozy ties with the White House. President Trump ultimately voiced support for Huang, emphasizing his indispensability to American AI leadership, leaving the tech titan fighting to protect both his company and his standing in Washington.
