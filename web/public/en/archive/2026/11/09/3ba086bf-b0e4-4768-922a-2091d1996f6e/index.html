Seedream 5.5: More Realistic Images and Stable Editing Across 100 Rounds

According to ByteDance Seed: Seedream reports that its Seedream 5.5 image generation model brings three major upgrades. It supports pixel-level preservation for up to 100 rounds of editing, introduces native RGBA output with transparent backgrounds, and delivers more realistic images with clearer text rendering.

ByteDance Seed ↗Source published 2026-11-09 00:00 SGTOfficial releaseArtificial Intelligence

According to ByteDance Seed:

Key Upgrades

Seedream reports that Seedream 5.5 focuses on professional visual production workflows, improving quality, reliability, and efficiency. According to the company, the model addresses common pain points in multi-turn editing, asset composition, and text rendering.

Multi-Turn Editing and RGBA Assets

The model uses targeted training to preserve unedited regions at the pixel level, allowing continuous work on the same image across 100 rounds of editing without color shifts or detail loss. Additionally, it introduces native RGBA generation, producing images with an alpha channel directly to eliminate the need for manual background removal.

Performance and Realism

Evaluations across six professional use cases show substantial gains in usability rates compared to the previous version. The model also reduces severe text corruption rates and achieves leading results in text-to-image realism in expert evaluations.
