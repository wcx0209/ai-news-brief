v0.34.0rc0: Migrating Gemma and StableLM to the Transformers backend

Detailed Features This release, version v0.34.0rc0, brings a crucial architectural shift for specific AI models. The core highlight is the backend migration of Gemma and StableLM. Instead of relying on custom or fragmented setups, these models are now fully integrated with the robust Transformers framework, ensuring they align with industry standards and benefit from ongoing ecosystem updates. System Architecture & Collaboration Under the hood, this update reflects modern collaborative software development. The commit was authored by Harry Mellor with co-authorship from Claude Opus 5.5, showcasing an interesting blend of human and AI engineering. This integration helps streamline the code paths, making the overall system easier to maintain and extend for future model additions. Installation & Usage To use these newly migrated models, developers simply need to update their repository to the v0.34.0rc0 release candidate (or subsequent stable versions). Ensure your environment points to the updated Transformers backend, then load Gemma or StableLM as usual to enjoy the enhanced performance and broader feature support.
