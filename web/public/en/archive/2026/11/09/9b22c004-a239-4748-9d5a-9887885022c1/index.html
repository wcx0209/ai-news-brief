Tencent Hunyuan Releases Hy4, Joining the Ranks of Top Open-Source Models

Tencent has released its latest large language model, Hy4, marking its entry into the top tier of open-source models. Hy4 brings significant upgrades in visual understanding, complex reasoning, and computer use capabilities. It is deeply in

Tencent's Hunyuan team has launched Hy4, an advanced large language model that will soon have its weights open-sourced. Compared to its predecessor, Hy4 scales up pre-training and post-training data, introducing native visual understanding and computer use capabilities. It can operate both command lines and graphical interfaces, streamlining professional tasks in software like Blender and CAD.

In practical applications, Hy4 powers productivity tools such as WorkBuddy, achieving top-tier scores in domestic benchmarks. It excels in professional financial analysis, undergoing rigorous expert testing to outperform competitors in specific domains. Furthermore, Hunyuan has optimized the model for front-end development, game creation, and scientific research while prioritizing network and biosecurity by design.

Despite these extensive upgrades, Hy4's pricing remains unchanged, and a cost-effective version named Hy4 Flash will be released soon. Tencent is also offering promotional discounts through cloud platforms to encourage wider adoption.
