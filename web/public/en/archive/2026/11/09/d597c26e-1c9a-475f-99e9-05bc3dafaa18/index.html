Europeans Protest Against AI Data Centers

Background A coalition named Stop Dirty Data Centres organized about 90 events across approximately 15 European nations on Friday and Saturday. These demonstrations targeted facilities deemed essential for the rollout of artificial intelligence. Details While most gatherings involved only a few dozen people, some locations saw thousands participate. In Poland, the Netherlands, and Spain, local groups gathered in small numbers, whereas France alone scheduled around 15 events. These included a citizens' march against a mega data center project in northern Brebières and Corbehem, and a rally outside a construction site in Les Pennes-Mirabeau. Impact Protesters are demanding a halt to projects and the launch of a democratic consultation on digital development. This wave of opposition underscores the friction between rapid technological advancement and environmental concerns.
