Europeans Protest Against AI Data Centers

Citizens in about 15 European countries held protests against data centers over the weekend, demanding an immediate moratorium on new large facilities. Organized by environmental and digital rights groups, the movement highlights growing concerns over the heavy toll these AI-supporting buildings take on local communities.

Le Monde AI ↗Source published 2026-11-09 17:41 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

Background

A coalition named Stop Dirty Data Centres organized about 90 events across approximately 15 European nations on Friday and Saturday. These demonstrations targeted facilities deemed essential for the rollout of artificial intelligence.

Details

While most gatherings involved only a few dozen people, some locations saw thousands participate. In Poland, the Netherlands, and Spain, local groups gathered in small numbers, whereas France alone scheduled around 15 events. These included a citizens' march against a mega data center project in northern Brebières and Corbehem, and a rally outside a construction site in Les Pennes-Mirabeau.

Impact

Protesters are demanding a halt to projects and the launch of a democratic consultation on digital development. This wave of opposition underscores the friction between rapid technological advancement and environmental concerns.
