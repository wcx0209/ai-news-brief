Sushiro Partners with Fujitsu to Use AI for Sustainable Fisheries

Sushiro is teaming up with Fujitsu to use AI technology to tackle the severe crisis in Japan's fishing industry. By analyzing ocean data, they hope to secure a stable supply of delicious fish and protect future seafood dining.

ITmedia AI+ ↗Source published 2026-11-09 06:00 SGTNews summaryArtificial Intelligence

Background

Japan's fishing industry is facing a severe crisis, with aging fishermen, shrinking catches, and rising global demand for seafood making it harder to source popular ingredients like tuna and salmon.

Details

Sushiro is collaborating with Fujitsu to leverage digital technology and AI to analyze ocean environments, track fish resources, and better understand marine ecosystems. This initiative aims to transform traditional fishing into a data-driven approach.

Impact

By improving the predictability of fish catches and promoting sustainable practices, the partnership hopes to prevent future seafood shortages and ensure the long-term survival of the sushi culture.
