Claude can design electronic prototypes from start to finish

Geometric Reasoning and Board Layout Newer versions of Claude have made massive leaps in handling complex geometric challenges, such as deciding where to place components and how to route copper connections on a circuit board. During tests, the AI produced compact layouts, prioritized high-speed and sensitive signals, and even wrote its own routing algorithm when existing software fell short. Real-World Manufacturing and Limitations Beyond just drawing circuits, Claude accounted for practical manufacturing constraints by running checks to ensure factories could actually build the devices. It also caught mechanical issues, such as a circuit board colliding with an enclosure wall, and fixed it by adding a tilted installation step. Despite these successes, the devices remain early prototypes that still require human assembly and lack mass production testing.
