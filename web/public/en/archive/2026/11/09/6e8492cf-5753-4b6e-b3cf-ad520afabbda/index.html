NVIDIA and Elith Release Million-Scale Synthetic Financial Persona Dataset for Japan

Background

In the financial sector, developing agentic AI is heavily constrained by strict privacy laws, compliance rules, and a lack of open, realistic data. To solve this, Elith and NVIDIA teamed up to create Nemotron-Personas-Finance-Japan, expanding on the earlier Nemotron-Personas-Japan collection with a specific focus on household finance and financial literacy.

Dataset Details

The dataset was built using NVIDIA's NeMo Data Designer and official Japanese statistics, featuring 9 million total personas across 50 detailed fields. It incorporates feedback from major Japanese financial institutions like Mizuho and Sompo Japan to ensure cultural authenticity and practical utility. Every record is entirely synthetic, meaning no real individuals' privacy is compromised.

Industry Impact

Released under a commercial-friendly CC BY 4.0 license, the dataset helps Japanese developers create localized AI agents, test for bias, and simulate customer interactions without using actual customer records. It paves the way for secure, sovereign AI development in the financial industry.
