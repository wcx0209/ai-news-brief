Nvidia investigates CEO Jensen Huang over foreign spy links

Background and Allegations Top US Republican lawmakers have asked Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang to brief Congress following reports that he was the target of a foreign intelligence operation. The New York Times previously alleged that Huang had a close relationship with a Chinese businesswoman who reportedly passed information to Beijing. Admissions and Investigation According to leaked drafts, Huang admitted to having a romantic relationship with the suspected spy and took full responsibility for his personal actions, apologizing for hurting his family. In response, Nvidia's board stated they are initiating an inquiry to examine all available information, though they currently have no reason to believe company secrets or national security were compromised. Political Fallout A memo from US officials had previously circulated regarding the intelligence concerns surrounding the businesswoman. Despite these warnings, Huang and his wife had attended a high-profile state dinner at the White House, highlighting the complex intersection of tech leadership and geopolitical tensions.
