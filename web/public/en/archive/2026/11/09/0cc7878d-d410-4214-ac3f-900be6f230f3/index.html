Background As artificial intelligence shifts from technological validation to large-scale implementation in 2026, the industry is increasingly focused on actual results rather than just model parameters. To record this transition, Quantum Bit is hosting its annual AI awards. Categories and Details The awards are divided into three main tracks: enterprise, product, and individual. The enterprise category features leading companies and high-potential startups; the product category highlights outstanding products and solutions; and the individual category focuses on emerging entrepreneurial talents under 40 years old. Significance Submissions will close on November 16, and the final results will be announced at the MEET2027 Intelligent Future Conference on December 9 in Beijing.
