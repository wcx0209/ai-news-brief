Sakana AI is developing its first mobile app and recruiting testers

Background Sakana AI is a prominent Japanese artificial intelligence startup known for developing innovative models such as the multi-model task handler 'Sakana Fugu' and the Japanese-optimized open-weight model 'Sakana Namazu', alongside expanding into finance and defense sectors. Details On November 9, the company announced the development of its first mobile application, tentatively named 'Sakana AI App'. CEO David Ha shared the news and called for testers on his X account. The testing program is open to users of both iOS and Android systems, and applications will be accepted until November 15. Impact This move marks a strategic shift for Sakana AI as it expands from underlying models and enterprise applications into the consumer-facing mobile space, potentially bringing its unique AI technology directly to everyday smartphone users.
