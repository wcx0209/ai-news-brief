CATL, BYD, and ACWA Join Forces in New Global Energy Storage Alliance at COP31

A new international storage alliance backed by 27 companies and institutions has been launched at the UN climate conference in Turkey. Supported by industry giants like CATL and BYD, the initiative aims to tackle market barriers and boost renewable energy projects in developing nations.

pv magazine Global ↗Source published 2026-11-21 17:30 SGTMedia reportEnergy & Storage

Background

Formally launched at the China Pavilion during COP31 in Antalya, Turkey, the Global Energy Storage Alliance (GESA) brings together 27 co-initiating organizations including battery manufacturers, power utilities, and research institutions from China and other countries. Details & Focus The alliance is founded by the China Energy Storage Alliance, China Renewable Energy Engineering Institute, and China Southern Power Grid Energy Storage Co., with international participants like Saudi Arabia's ACWA Power. GESA outlines six core areas, ranging from establishing global project databases to strengthening supply chains and improving market mechanisms for both short- and long-duration storage. Impact The initiative directly supports the global goal of tripling renewable capacity by 2030 by de-risking financing for emerging economies in regions like Southeast Asia and Latin America, marking a shift for China from exporting products to shaping global standards.
