How Japanese Companies Choose AI: ChatGPT Fades While Copilot and Gemini Rise

A survey by Tokyo-based AI startup AIworker reveals a major shift in enterprise AI adoption in Japan. In 2026, ChatGPT's share in new consultation requests dropped significantly to 28 percent from 66 percent in 2025, while Microsoft Copilot and Gemini both surged to 35 percent.

ITmedia AI+ ↗Source published 2026-11-21 13:22 SGTNews summaryArtificial Intelligence

According to a survey conducted by AIworker, an AI venture originating from the University of Tokyo, the landscape of enterprise AI selection in Japan has shifted dramatically between 2025 and 2026. The data, gathered from recorded consultation meetings, shows that ChatGPT fell from holding a dominant 66 percent share of mentions in 2025 down to 28 percent in 2026. Meanwhile, Microsoft Copilot and Google Gemini rose to share the lead at 35 percent each, with Anthropic's Claude also climbing to 27 percent. AIworker attributes this change to companies increasingly distributing bundled AI tools that come with their existing enterprise software, moving away from standalone AI selections.

Despite the broader adoption of AI across various tools, actual utilization presents significant hurdles. The primary reason for stalled AI projects is the lack of skilled personnel and internal champions, cited by 34 percent of respondents, followed by internal procedures and budget justifications. Furthermore, the usage pattern is evolving: individual personal use dropped from 71 percent to 57 percent, while cases where companies introduced AI organization-wide but failed to drive actual usage increased from 9 percent to 19 percent, highlighting the ongoing challenge of scaling AI adoption successfully.
