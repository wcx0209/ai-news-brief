GPT-6.2 Astra doubles robotics task success rate

According to XDOF, OpenAI's GPT-6.2 Astra achieved a 41.2% success rate across 16 robot operating tasks, more than double its predecessor. The model notably cleared previously unpassed tasks like tying knots and unfolding T-shirts, showcasing major progress in handling soft objects and complex physical reasoning.

In recent tests conducted by robotics infrastructure firm XDOF, OpenAI's GPT-6.2 Astra demonstrated significant improvements in embodied AI tasks. Using six YAM workstations for parallel testing with strict controls to filter out hardware bias, XDOF found that GPT-6.2 Astra's average success rate across 16 standardized Ember-0 benchmark tasks rose from 19.6% to 41.2%. Key Breakthroughs The most notable achievement is the model's ability to successfully complete soft-object manipulation tasks such as tying knots and unfolding T-shirts, which previously had a 0% success rate across 143 trials. Sub-tasks requiring fine force control, spatial reasoning, and tool use, like picking up bread with a clip, also saw dramatic accuracy improvements from 10% to 90%. Speed and Industry Impact Despite improvements in lowering the average prediction delay from 15 seconds to 10.1 seconds, latency remains a hurdle for real-time robotic response. Nevertheless, researchers including NUS Assistant Professor Jiafei Duan noted the model's exceptional performance in real-world benchmarks, underscoring how frontier multimodal models are rapidly accelerating physical world capabilities.
