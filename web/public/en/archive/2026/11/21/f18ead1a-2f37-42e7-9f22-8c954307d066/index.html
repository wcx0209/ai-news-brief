Apple is reportedly building an autonomous AI agent to take on rivals

According to Apple's recent job listings, the company is developing an autonomous AI agent that can browse the web, control apps, and complete complex tasks. Unlike standard chatbots, this system aims to take actions on behalf of users by breaking down goals into smaller steps.

Mint AI ↗Source published 2026-11-21 18:57 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

Apple is reportedly expanding its artificial intelligence strategy by developing an autonomous agent designed to operate computers like a person. Revealed through recent job postings for its Agentc AI and Siri agent platform teams, the system aims to handle tasks across various browsers, apps, and software services without requiring constant user guidance. The architecture reportedly involves specialised sub-agents that can divide complex assignments into manageable parts, running across both on-device hardware and Apple's Private Cloud Compute infrastructure.

While Apple has not yet officially announced a finished product or shared a launch date, industry observers note that the company's hardware ecosystem of iPhones, Macs, and iPads could provide a distinct distribution advantage. Furthermore, Apple continues to emphasize privacy as a foundational element of its AI framework, building upon its previous announcements regarding next-generation Siri capabilities that feature personal context understanding and on-screen awareness.
