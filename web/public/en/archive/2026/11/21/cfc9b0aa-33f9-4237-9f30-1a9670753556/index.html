Anthropic Hits Nasdaq with Record IPO as Tech Giants Report Massive AI Revenue and Spending Shifts

Anthropic has officially gone public on Nasdaq, raising over $100 billion in what marks the largest IPO in history. Meanwhile, industry heavyweights like Nvidia, Microsoft, OpenAI, and Baidu have released major model updates and financial reports, while emerging signs of cautious infrastructure investment ripple through the global market.

TMTPost 钛媒体 ↗Source published 2026-11-21 19:19 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

This week's tech roundup covers landmark capital market events and rapid technological iterations. Anthropic made history by ringing the Nasdaq opening bell, pulling in a total of over $107 billion through its IPO and private placements. In financial highlights, Nvidia reported its first-ever single-quarter revenue exceeding $100 billion, driven by booming data center demand, though its temporary CEO noted that future supply chain investments will become more rigorous. Similarly, major Chinese tech firms released strong results: Baidu's AI cloud infrastructure revenue grew by 94% year-over-year, and Alibaba's AI-related product revenue surpassed 17.9 billion yuan. On the model front, OpenAI launched GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol, Microsoft rolled out seven new proprietary AI models, and Tencent released Hy4 flash. However, shifting macroeconomic policies and supply chain constraints are taking shape, as the US introduced restrictions on remote access to advanced AI computing power, and research institutions like SemiAnalysis and JPMorgan projected potential slowdowns or diversification in future AI capital expenditures.
