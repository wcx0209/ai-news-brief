Anthropic Pulls Off Record-Breaking IPO While Battling Its Own Internal and External Roadblocks

AI lab Anthropic just wrapped up the largest initial public offering in history, but its road ahead is bumpy. The company faces massive bills for data centers, growing community backlash over environmental and power concerns, and internal debates over whether safety safeguards might slow down its growth.

WIRED AI ↗Source published 2026-11-19 20:00 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

Anthropic's public market debut on the Nasdaq brought in roughly $81 billion, valuing the AI company at around $1.5 trillion and leaving it with about $200 billion in the bank. However, the company has committed to paying vendors over $900 billion in the next 19 years, mostly for data centers. While recent quarterly sales hit $17 billion, the company remains unprofitable due to stock-based compensation.

Beyond finances, Anthropic faces fierce resistance from local communities over West Virginia data center projects and environmental impacts. CEO Dario Amodei insists that safety is the primary limiting factor for deployments, noting that prioritizing public benefit and screening for safety-oriented hires has sometimes stunted short-term growth. Furthermore, upcoming regulations and questions over whether AI can sustain its massive infrastructure costs cast shadows on its long-term dominance.
