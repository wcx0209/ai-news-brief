NeeevAI Founder on Bringing AI Education to Millions of Students

NeeevAI founder Arjun Singh Bedi discusses the company's rapid expansion into school AI education. The startup combines an AI curriculum, textbooks, and robotics kits, aiming to reach nearly 26,000 schools by 2027 to help students transition from AI users to creators and builders.

Robotics & Autonomous Systems

Expansion and Growth

NeeevAI reports that its program has grown from an initial two schools in April 2025 to a projected contract base of 25,786 schools and roughly 3.1 million students by April 2027.

Ecosystem and Curriculum

The company's offering integrates physical and digital components, including textbooks for Classes 1 to 12, robotics kits, teacher training, and dedicated platforms like the Nova hackathon app and Argo internship app. Founder Arjun Singh Bedi emphasizes that the curriculum incorporates the UNESCO Ethical AI Framework across all grade levels to address safety, ethics, and critical thinking.

Market Strategy and Partnerships

NeeevAI addresses varied school infrastructure and stakeholder concerns through structured classroom integration, compliance with India's data protection rules, and practical student showcases. Bedi views major tech giants and local firms as potential partners rather than competitors, serving as a localized distribution layer between advanced technology and students.
