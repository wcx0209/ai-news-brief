2026 Finance Quantum Index Shows Growing Bank Investment

The 2026 Finance Quantum Index covers 64 financial institutions, more than doubling from last year. According to the report, JPMorgan Chase leads with a score of 97, followed by HSBC and Wells Fargo. However, the index highlights a significant capability gap, as the median score remains at 26 across all participating institutions.

The Quantum Insider ↗Source published 2026-11-19 06:34 SGTMedia reportQuantum Technology

The 2026 Finance Quantum Index, authored by HorizonX founder Steve Suarez, has expanded its coverage to 64 financial institutions across eight regions, introducing 36 new additions to map quantum activity in banking, insurance, payments, and central banking.

Rankings and Highlights

JPMorgan Chase takes the top spot with 97 points due to strengths in workforce, research, and adoption, maintaining a 20-point lead. HSBC and Wells Fargo share second place with 77 points. Meanwhile, Mastercard ranks third with 62 points, and recently launched a Quantum Advisory Practice to help financial institutions handle post-quantum cryptography migration and security risks.

Methodology and Industry Gap

Using a revised methodology weighting workforce at 30 percent, R&D at 40 percent, and adoption at 30 percent, the index relies on documented evidence such as patents, papers, and partnerships. A notable finding is the widespread capability gap: only five institutions scored 60 or higher, showing that advanced quantum investments remain concentrated among a few market leaders.
