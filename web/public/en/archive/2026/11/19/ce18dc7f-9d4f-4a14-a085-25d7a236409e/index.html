The Untapped Potential of Drug Repurposing

In a recent episode of BioSpace's podcast Denatured, industry leaders discussed why the FDA is prioritizing drug repurposing and what it takes to turn existing molecules into viable new therapies.

BioSpace Drug Development ↗Source published 2026-11-19 19:23 SGTInterviewBiotech & Programmable Life

BioSpace Insights hosted an episode of its podcast Denatured featuring Daniel Tillett, CEO of Racura Oncology, and Sten Sörensen, CEO of Cereno Scientific. The discussion, hosted by Jennifer C. Smith-Parker, centered on the growing regulatory focus on drug repurposing. The guests explored the reasons behind the FDA's renewed emphasis on finding new uses for already existing molecules and examined the practical challenges involved in successfully transforming them into effective new treatments for patients.
