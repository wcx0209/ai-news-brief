XJet Launches On-Demand Manufacturing Services and AI Jewelry Tool at Formnext

XJet has introduced its XPARTS on-demand manufacturing service and the XJet Atelier AI-assisted jewelry creation offering to expand access to its NanoParticle Jetting technology. According to XJet, the new services provide customers with materials, engineering expertise, and production capabilities for prototyping and finished parts.

Service Expansion

XJet has rolled out two new offerings at the Formnext event to broaden market access to its NanoParticle Jetting (NPJ) technology. The newly launched XPARTS on-demand service allows users to leverage the company's materials, engineering support, and production capabilities for application assessment, prototyping, validation, and production.

Material Versatility

NPJ technology can print a wide variety of materials, including technical ceramics, stainless steel, copper alloys, and precious metals such as sterling silver, gold alloys, and Platinum 950. This material breadth has driven XJet into the jewelry sector over the past year, prompting the introduction of the XJet Atelier service for AI-assisted personalized jewelry creation.

Commercial Milestones

During the Formnext exhibition, XJet CEO Guy Zimmerman noted that the company is making its full capabilities accessible to more customers for finished parts, production systems, or personalized jewelry. Additionally, XJet confirmed the securing of its first customer orders for the Carmel Pro and Carmel ORO platforms.
