Anthropic Hits $2 Trillion Valuation in Nasdaq Debut After Shares Surge 34%

Anthropic became the world's first publicly traded frontier AI company by hitting a $2 trillion market value during its Nasdaq debut. The stock opened 34% higher than its IPO price, driven by massive investor demand. This successful listing, alongside Nvidia's recent earnings, acts as a major market test for the future of AI investments.

Mint AI ↗Source published 2026-11-19 23:05 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

IPO Performance and Valuation

According to Reuters, Anthropic priced its initial public offering at $83 per share after selling 980 million shares, raising substantial capital ahead of its public debut. When trading began, the stock opened at $111.5, representing a 34% surge over the IPO price and pushing the company's total market capitalization past the $2 trillion threshold. This milestone makes Anthropic the world's first publicly traded frontier AI company, positioning it alongside tech giants like Microsoft, Apple, and Nvidia in the trillion-dollar club.

Industry Implications and Market Sentiment

Market analysts view this blockbuster debut, alongside Nvidia's recent earnings report, as a crucial test of investor confidence in the AI sector amid ongoing debates over heavy spending and profitability. While early backers and founders, including CEO Dario Amodei, reaped massive financial gains, some market skeptics warn that Anthropic still faces heavy pressure to turn a profit and manage nearly $900 billion in future computing commitments with partners like Amazon and Google.
