Qatar and France Sign Digital Cooperation Agreement with Focus on Quantum Tech

Qatar and France have signed a bilateral Letter of Intent in Doha to strengthen cooperation in artificial intelligence, digital transformation, and emerging technologies.

Quantum Computing Report ↗Source published 2026-11-19 01:39 SGTMedia reportQuantum Technology
Artificial Intelligence

According to Qatar's Ministry of Communications and Information Technology, the agreement was signed in Doha on the sidelines of the ITU Plenipotentiary Conference 2026 by Qatar's Minister of Communications and Information Technology H.E. Mohammed bin Ali Al Mannai and France's Minister Delegate for Artificial Intelligence and Digital Affairs H.E. Anne Le Hénanff.

The framework establishes mechanisms for exchanging technical expertise, developing digital applications across priority economic sectors, and building local digital competencies. Minister Le Hénanff noted that both governments share a common vision of strategic autonomy in the digital sector and aligned ambitions in artificial intelligence and quantum technologies.

While the ministerial Letter of Intent is a broad information and communications technology framework without disclosed project funding or binding procurement targets, it follows closely behind growing commercial ties. Two days prior to the ministerial signing, French photonic quantum computing developer Quandela finalized a tripartite deployment alliance in Doha to bring cloud and on-premises quantum systems to the Gulf region.
