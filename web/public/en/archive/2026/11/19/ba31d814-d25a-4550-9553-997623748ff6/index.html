Siemens brings embedded AI to HVAC systems for self-adapting climate control

According to Siemens Blog: Siemens showcased its Autonomous Equipment Optimization technology at Chillventa 2026, using embedded machine learning directly in controllers to let HVAC equipment adapt to changing conditions.

Siemens Blog ↗Source published 2026-11-19 18:42 SGTOfficial releaseAdvanced Manufacturing & Industrial Infrastructure
Artificial Intelligence

According to Siemens Blog:

At Chillventa 2026, Siemens highlighted how the HVAC industry is shifting from generic, rule-based settings to practical, model-based adaptive control. Traditional HVAC equipment often relies on conservative thresholds, such as preventive defrost strategies that can increase winter heating-energy use. Siemens' Autonomous Equipment Optimization embeds machine learning directly inside the Climatix equipment controller.

For its intelligent defrost application, the system learns the relationship between operating conditions and expected exhaust-fan power during frost-free operation. This creates a virtual reference to continuously monitor deviations caused by frost buildup, allowing defrost cycles to trigger based on actual demand rather than fixed rules. According to Siemens, this approach can reduce winter heating-energy use by 20–30% in scenarios without frost sensors, depending on climate and operating conditions. Furthermore, the embedded model operates within strict engineer-defined safety and validation limits, aiming to reduce manual intervention during commissioning and support condition-based maintenance across the equipment lifecycle.
