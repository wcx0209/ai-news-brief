Amazon SageMaker AI Now Supports Full Fine-Tuning Without Infrastructure Management

According to AWS Machine Learning: Amazon SageMaker AI Serverless Model Customization now supports Full Fine-Tuning, allowing businesses to adapt large language models to complex domains without managing underlying GPU infrastructure.

AWS Machine Learning ↗Source published 2026-11-19 08:15 SGTOfficial releaseArtificial Intelligence

According to AWS Machine Learning:

Amazon SageMaker AI has updated its Serverless Model Customization service to support Full Fine-Tuning (FFT) alongside existing parameter-efficient methods like LoRA. While LoRA freezes pre-trained weights and injects small replacement matrices, its low-rank nature can hit an accuracy plateau on complex tasks. In contrast, FFT updates all model parameters simultaneously, leveraging the full representational capacity of the network to achieve stronger convergence and better layer activation alignment.

To evaluate these methods, tests were conducted using a Qwen3-4B base model trained on 110,000 coding instruction pairs. Results showed that as training data scales beyond 100,000 examples, FFT achieves superior accuracy on semantic correctness and surface-level text similarity metrics compared to LoRA. However, FFT also carries a higher risk of catastrophic forgetting, requiring careful tuning of learning rates and epoch counts to preserve general knowledge.
