Introducing Gemini 4 Neon

According to Google DeepMind: Google introduces Gemini 4 Neon, a frontier-level intelligence model designed for fast end-to-end task completion in software engineering, knowledge work, and science workflows. According to Google, it ranks #3 overall on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index and offers a 1 million-token context window with competitive pricing and enhanced steerability.

Google DeepMind ↗Source published 2026-11-19 05:35 SGTOfficial releaseArtificial Intelligence

According to Google DeepMind:

Google has announced Gemini 4 Neon, a model built to deliver frontier-level intelligence with high efficiency across complex enterprise, analytical, and scientific workflows.

Performance and Benchmarks

According to Google's evaluations, Neon ranks #3 overall on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at its xhigh thinking level and #2 on the Vals Index, landing on the Pareto frontier. It achieves high scores across specialized tasks, including #1 on Zapier's AutomationBench and strong results in scientific reasoning like Terminal-Bench Science. For developers, Neon supports long-running agent workflows, handles multi-file debugging, and leads the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index.

Cost and Availability

Neon is priced at $3 per million input tokens ($0.15 for cached inputs) and $15 per million output tokens, featuring a 1 million-token context window. It is rolling out via the Gemini API in Google AI Studio, Google for Business, Google Antigravity, and the Gemini app for paid subscribers, with specialized cybersecurity features available through the Fairwind Program.
