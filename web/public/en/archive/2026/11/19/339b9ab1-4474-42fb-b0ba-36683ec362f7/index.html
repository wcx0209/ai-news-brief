MIT Vice President for Campus Services Joe Higgins to Step Down

According to MIT Climate: MIT announces that Joe Higgins, vice president for campus services and stewardship, will step down at the end of January to relocate to the West Coast for family reasons. According to MIT Climate:

Joe Higgins, MIT's vice president for campus services and stewardship, will step down at the end of January and relocate to the West Coast to be closer to family, according to an announcement by Executive Vice President and Treasurer Glen Shor. Higgins joined MIT in 2016 and assumed the newly created VPCSS role in 2019, overseeing facilities, environment, health and safety, campus planning, and sustainability.

During his tenure, Higgins oversaw significant transformations, including the MIT 2030 Capital Plan projects such as the Schwarzman College of Computing and preparations for the MIT Quantum Initiative. He also contributed to reducing MIT's overall campus Facilities Condition Index by about 40 percent and advanced campus climate goals, helping the Institute approach net-zero carbon emissions through energy-efficiency projects and large-scale renewable energy partnerships.

Following his departure, Jon Alvarez will serve as interim VPCSS while executive search firm Isaacson, Miller leads a national search for a permanent successor.
