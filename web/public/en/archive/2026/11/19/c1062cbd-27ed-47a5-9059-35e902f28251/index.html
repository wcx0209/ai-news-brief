Flexell Space and ALATYR partner to study solar power for orbital data centers

Flexell Space and French orbital infrastructure firm ALATYR have signed an MoU to explore solar power solutions for space-based computing. The collaboration will assess the use of lightweight solar cells for orbital data centers designed to support artificial intelligence and heavy workloads.

SpaceNews ↗Source published 2026-11-19 06:02 SGTMedia reportSpace Economy
Artificial Intelligence × Energy & Storage

Flexell Space, a space energy company originating from Hanwha Systems, has signed a memorandum of understanding with French orbital infrastructure developer ALATYR. The partnership aims to investigate next-generation solar power solutions for space-based computing infrastructure.

ALATYR develops orbital data centers intended for artificial intelligence computing and mission-critical workloads. Its architecture features modular systems like the CITADEL infrastructure and BYLD robotics, allowing computing hardware to be replaced in orbit while retaining supporting systems. Reliable power generation is critical for these facilities, requiring solar technology to balance electrical performance, mass and launch efficiency, and long-term durability.

Flexell Space is developing next-generation space solar cells using CIGS and perovskite technologies, focusing on lightweight and flexible designs. Through this partnership, the companies will exchange technical data, assess power system compatibility, and explore potential joint development and validation opportunities for future orbital data centers.
