Google brings the Gemini app to lower-memory Android devices

According to Google AI: Google announces that the Gemini app is now rolling out to lower-memory Android (Go edition) devices. By re-architecting the application, Google aims to bring its personal AI assistant to hundreds of millions of users who previously relied on basic voice commands due to hardware limitations.

Google AI ↗Source published 2026-11-19 01:00 SGTOfficial releaseArtificial Intelligence

According to Google AI:

According to Angus Logan, Builder for the Gemini App at Google, the company has re-architected the Gemini app so that its core experience can run smoothly on lower-memory Android devices. This update targets hundreds of millions of global users whose everyday internet and business connections rely on budget hardware.

With this release, eligible devices gain access to features like Gemini Live, allowing users to talk things through or practice for interviews out loud in over 45 languages. Users can also utilize their device cameras to translate documents, or create custom images. Google notes that the app can be downloaded via Google Play, accessed by holding the power button on supported phones, or found preloaded on upcoming devices from partners such as Transsion.
