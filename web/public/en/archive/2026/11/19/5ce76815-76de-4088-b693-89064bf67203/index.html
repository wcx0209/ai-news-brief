Sakana AI launches independent Security Research Group for AI cyber defense

According to Sakana AI: Sakana AI is launching an independent research team called the Security Research Group to study AI-driven cyber defense and AI safety.

Sakana AI ↗Source published 2026-11-19 23:00 SGTOfficial releaseArtificial Intelligence

According to Sakana AI:

Sakana AI is establishing an independent R&D organization, the Security Research Group, dedicated to collective, AI-driven cyber defense and the safety of AI systems. According to the company, frontier AI has drastically accelerated the discovery of software flaws, with NIST reporting a 263% surge in CVE submissions between 2020 and 2025. Concurrently, attackers are weaponizing AI agents to launch simultaneous intrusions, putting immense pressure on traditional patching workflows.

To counter this, Sakana AI argues that faster response and routine patching are no longer enough. The group advocates for a secure-by-design approach, treating security as an essential Kano-model "must-be quality" built into software from its inception. Facing structural constraints in Japan such as outsourced operations, technical debt, and limited IT staff, the research group will focus on upstream prevention, AI-based collective defense, and securing AI agents against misuse.

Led by Akihiro Umegai and David Ha, the group plans to operate with vendor neutrality and maintain responsible disclosure principles, bridging the gap between national security policies and practical industry implementation.
