Google Returns to AI Center Stage with Gemini 4 Neon

Google DeepMind has released Gemini 4 Neon, the fourth element-themed model in seven weeks. According to Google and independent evaluations, it delivers near-flagship performance while cutting task costs roughly in half, winning widespread praise from developers.

GeekPark 极客公园 ↗Source published 2026-11-19 14:10 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

According to Google DeepMind and benchmark evaluations from Artificial Analysis, Gemini 4 Neon features a 1-million token context window and adjustable thinking depths. In the AA Intelligence Index v5.0.1, Neon secured the global third spot in its xhigh mode. In programming benchmarks like CursorBench 4.1 and SWE-sweep, it outperformed several competing flagship models while maintaining lower operational expenses.

At the same time, Google paired Neon with the Antigravity platform's /swarm mode for multi-agent coordination, allowing the model to handle long-running, multi-file engineering workflows. This release is supported by structural changes under the leadership of Koray Kavukcuoglu, who prioritized rapid iteration, and the cost advantages provided by Google's custom 7th-generation TPU Ironwood hardware.
