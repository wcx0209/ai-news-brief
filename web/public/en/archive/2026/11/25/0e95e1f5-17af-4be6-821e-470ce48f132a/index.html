Clearpath Introduces Husky A300 Mobile Robot Integrated with Fanuc Manipulator

According to Clearpath Robotics: Clearpath Robotics presents a customized integration of its Husky A300 mobile platform featuring a Fanuc collaborative robot arm, a Robotiq gripper, and Wiferion wireless charging. This system is designed to help researchers and developers accelerate mobile manipulation and industrial automation projects.

Clearpath Robotics ↗Source published 2026-11-25 01:57 SGTOfficial releaseRobotics & Autonomous Systems

According to Clearpath Robotics:

System Overview and Integration

Clearpath Robotics highlights a custom integration of the Husky A300, built as a flexible research and development platform. The system combines a mobile base with a robotic arm, end effector, wireless charging, and operator controls, offering a ready-to-use solution for autonomous systems.

Key Components and Hardware

The platform mounts a FANUC CRX-5iA collaborative robot arm featuring six axes of motion, a 5 kg payload, and a 994 mm reach. It uses a Robotiq adaptive gripper for handling objects of various shapes. Power is supplied by a 1 kW Wiferion wireless charging system that recharges the battery in two to three hours depending on the configuration. For control, a wired tablet with a magnetic mount provides operational access and an emergency stop interlock that halts both the base and the arm simultaneously.
