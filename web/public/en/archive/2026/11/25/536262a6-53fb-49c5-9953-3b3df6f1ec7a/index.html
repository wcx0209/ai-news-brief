Mamdani questions AI economy as Jensen Huang returns to Nvidia

New York City mayor Zohran Mamdani criticized the heavy reliance of the stock market on artificial intelligence following the corporate turbulence at Nvidia. He argued that working Americans should not have their pensions tied to the personal controversies of CEO Jensen Huang, whose temporary departure previously caused a sharp drop in share prices.

The Guardian AI ↗Source published 2026-11-25 00:30 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

According to Reuters and The Guardian, New York City mayor Zohran Mamdani has weighed in on the corporate drama surrounding Nvidia and its chief executive, Jensen Huang. Mamdani stated that working Americans did not sign up to have their pensions tied to questions regarding Huang's marital fidelity.

Jensen Huang previously stepped down as CEO on November 12 following a reported extramarital relationship with a suspected Chinese spy, which triggered a dramatic drop in Nvidia's share price. Following subsequent pressure and political developments, Huang has now returned to the company, highlighting the fragile relationship between corporate tech leadership and national politics.
