Xpeng boosts battery production as carmakers reduce reliance on CATL

Chinese electric vehicle maker Xpeng is expanding its battery pack manufacturing through a new 1.8 billion yuan agreement in Guangdong. As carmakers diversify their supply chains, major battery leader CATL's market share has declined while second-tier suppliers gain ground.

SCMP China Tech ↗Source published 2026-11-25 08:00 SGTMedia reportEnergy & Storage

According to a local government social media post, Xpeng recently signed a 1.8 billion yuan (US$270 million) agreement with the Zhaoqing municipal government to build an integrated automotive project. This project includes new battery pack production lines with an annual capacity of 200,000 units, alongside new vehicle models and a global after-sales spare parts center.

Industry experts note that while in-house battery assembly offers limited direct cost savings, it helps automakers make cells more interchangeable and strengthens negotiating positions against dominant suppliers like CATL and BYD. Xpeng has already shifted most of its battery sourcing away from CATL in recent years, relying more on other suppliers, even as its net loss widened by 129 per cent year on year in the third quarter. Meanwhile, data from the China Automotive Battery Innovation Alliance shows CATL's market share in China's EV battery sector declined for six consecutive months, dropping to 40 per cent in October.
