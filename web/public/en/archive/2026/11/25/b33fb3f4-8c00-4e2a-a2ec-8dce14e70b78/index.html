Historical brief · 2026-11-25

Arianespace Completes 10th Ariane 6 Flight and Plans Next Launch for December

Arianespace successfully completed the tenth flight of its Ariane 6 rocket, deploying 36 satellites for Amazon's constellation. This mission follows the resolution of a previous turbopump issue, with the final launch of 2026 scheduled for December.

European Spaceflight ↗Source published 2026-11-25 03:55 SGTMedia reportSpace Economy

Mission Details

Arianespace successfully launched its Ariane 6 rocket from the Guiana Space Centre, deploying 36 Amazon Leo satellites into low Earth orbit at an altitude of approximately 460 kilometres. This flight marks the fourth mission for Amazon in 2026, bringing the total number of deployed satellites for their broadband constellation to 136.

Technical Context and Schedule

The launch follows the recent discovery and correction of an issue affecting one of the rocket's turbopumps. Although resolved for this flight, a further engine test is planned for early December to fully validate the fixes. The final Ariane 6 flight of 2026 is currently expected on 17 December, carrying a weather satellite for EUMETSAT.
