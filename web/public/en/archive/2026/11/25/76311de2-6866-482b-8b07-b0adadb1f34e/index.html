Schrödinger acquires Enpicom for 22 million euros to expand into biologics discovery

Computational biotech firm Schrödinger has acquired Enpicom in an all-cash deal worth 22 million euros to expand from small molecules into biologics. Enpicom brings bioinformatics and AI software designed to accelerate sequence analysis, helping the combined platform support drug discovery from initial screening to atomic optimization.

Fierce Biotech ↗Source published 2026-11-25 22:08 SGTMedia reportBiotech & Programmable Life

Computational discovery biotech Schrödinger has finalized a deal to acquire Netherlands-based sequencing automation company Enpicom for 22 million euros, according to the companies. Schrödinger CEO Ramy Farid stated that the move allows the company to venture into biologics for the first time, having previously focused on small molecule drugs and advanced candidates currently in early-stage clinical trials.

Enpicom utilizes bioinformatics and artificial intelligence to speed up biologics discovery. Enpicom CEO Nicola Bonzanni noted that their software transforms weeks of complex sequence analysis into hours of decisive insights. The integration aims to combine Enpicom's software with Schrödinger's computational simulation platform to build an end-to-end infrastructure for future drug development modalities.
