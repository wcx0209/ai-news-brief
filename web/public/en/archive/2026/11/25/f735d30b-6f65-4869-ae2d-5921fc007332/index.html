Yale expert says Nvidia is hurting U.S. industrial might

Yale academic Jeffrey Sonnenfeld warns that Nvidia's board faces historic liability after reinstating CEO Jensen Huang under political pressure. According to Sonnenfeld, the move ignores mounting legal risks, export control violations, and evidence that advanced U.S. chips have reached Chinese hands.

TechCrunch AI ↗Source published 2026-11-25 08:12 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

In a recent interview, Yale business academic Jeffrey Sonnenfeld strongly criticized Nvidia’s board for caving to political pressure from the Trump administration and reinstating Jensen Huang as CEO and board member. Sonnenfeld argued that the board showed extreme incompetence and cowardice by failing to use independent experts and reversing course after a brief investigation by the Wachtell law firm. He noted that unlike companies heavily dependent on government contracts, Nvidia relies very little on U.S. federal business, making the board's surrender indefensible.

Sonnenfeld also warned that Nvidia has become a detriment to U.S. industrial might, pointing to research showing that its most advanced chips have bypassed export controls and ended up in Chinese institutions and systems. Furthermore, he highlighted that the board's decisions and ongoing scandals are triggering massive legal fallout, including shareholder lawsuits, SEC probes, and antitrust investigations, which will likely cost the company and its directors dearly.
