OS-Omni: Benchmarking Generalist Computer-Using Agents on Long-Horizon Workflows Across Platforms

Researchers introduce OS-Omni, a live cross-platform benchmark and execution environment spanning Windows, Ubuntu, macOS, iOS, Android, and Web. Evaluating frontier models shows they trail humans on iOS and macOS.

Hugging Face Daily Papers ↗Source published 2026-11-25 03:00 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

The authors present OS-Omni, a live cross-platform benchmark and execution environment designed to evaluate computer-using agents on long-horizon workflows across Windows, Ubuntu, macOS, iOS, Android, and Web environments. Version 1 of the benchmark features 48 tasks per platform alongside 12 cross-platform workflows pairing desktops with Android phones, incorporating controlled service-backed applications with persistent state. Human baseline studies indicate that OS-Omni tasks require an average of 67.54 interaction steps, significantly longer than prior benchmarks such as OSWorld (15.0 steps). Experimental results on v1 show that the strongest evaluated agent, Claude Opus 5.5, achieves a 68.5% average success rate, trailing human performance by 8.8 points. Performance disparities vary considerably across platforms: while frontier models exceed human performance on Linux, they lag behind by more than 25 points on iOS and macOS, illustrating that current agents struggle with platform robustness, sustained state tracking, and durable final-state changes across heterogeneous operating systems.
