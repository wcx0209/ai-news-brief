Jensen Huang Tells Nvidia Staff Trump Didn't Get Him Rehired

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang reportedly told employees in an all-hands meeting that claims of Donald Trump helping him get his job back are completely untrue. Huang admitted to violating company code and apologized for his actions, while the board evaluated his return alongside national security assurances.

The Verge AI ↗Source published 2026-11-25 04:02 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

According to reports from Bloomberg, The New York Times, and Marisa Kabas, Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang addressed staff during his first all-hands meeting since returning to the company. Huang stated that claims of President Donald Trump playing a direct role in his reinstatement are completely untrue, insisting that politics did not influence the decision. He also dismissed the narrative that he returned because he felt above the law as a tech bro.

Huang apologized to staff for his actions, admitting he made terrible mistakes, was unfaithful to his wife, and hurt his family and employees. He noted that he is currently attending daily therapy and does not believe in succession planning. Meanwhile, Nvidia continues to face investigations regarding accounting practices and an internal probe into a reported Chinese spy plot, and Huang is set to testify before Congress on December 1st.
