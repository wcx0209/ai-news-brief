Robot Talk Podcast: Programming Robot Behavior with Sebastian Castro

In this episode of Robot Talk, host Claire chats with roboticist Sebastian Castro about developing open-source software for robot task planning and behavior. Castro shares his extensive industry experience spanning Boston Dynamics, NASA's Johnson Space Center, and MIT CSAIL, offering a look into how engineers make robots move and act intelligently.

Background and Guest Robot Talk is a weekly podcast exploring robotics and autonomous systems. In Episode 171, host Claire sits down with Sebastian Castro, an independent roboticist specializing in robotic manipulation. Industry Experience Castro currently works with the Dexterous Robotics Group at NASA's Johnson Space Center. His impressive resume includes stints as a software engineer at Boston Dynamics, the Robotics and AI Institute, PickNik, and MIT's CSAIL, alongside educational marketing work at MathWorks. Beyond his professional roles, Castro actively contributes to robotics education through open-source software, blog posts, talks, and workshops.
