Historical brief · 2026-11-20

Revolution Medicines reports positive early survival data for pancreatic cancer drug Rasonque

Revolution Medicines shared early data showing that its pancreatic cancer drug Rasonque, when combined with chemotherapy, achieved a 60.7% response rate in initial treatment. Financial analysts at Truist Securities believe these strong results could push future sales beyond current projections, strengthening the drug's position as a new standard treatment.

BioSpace Drug Development ↗Source published 2026-11-20 22:51 SGTMedia reportBiotech & Programmable Life

Clinical Results and Trial Details According to Revolution Medicines, data from 61 patients in the ongoing Phase 1/2 RMC-GI-102 open-label trial presented at a recent medical meeting showed that Rasonque combined with two types of chemotherapy achieved an objective response rate of 60.7% in first-line pancreatic cancer patients. The trial reported a median progression-free survival of 12.8 months, outperforming analyst expectations, while overall survival reached 76.6% at the 12-month mark. Market Impact and Competition Following these findings, Truist Securities noted that the drug's profile sets a high efficacy bar and could boost Revolution Medicines beyond its currently estimated $14.5 billion in peak sales. Meanwhile, the company faces patent litigation and upcoming clinical readouts from competitors like Erasca, which is developing its own targeted therapies in the space.
