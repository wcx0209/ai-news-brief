ProMateria Starts Industrial Production of Additive Metal Parts

GMH Gruppe's metal additive manufacturing company, ProMateria, has officially started its production facility after a year of planning. The company will initially manufacture internal spare parts for GMH group companies before expanding its services to external industrial customers.

3D Printing Industry ↗Source published 2026-11-20 21:45 SGTMedia reportAdvanced Manufacturing & Industrial Infrastructure

ProMateria GmbH, a subsidiary launched by European metal-processing firm GMH Gruppe, has operationalized its production facility after about 12 months of planning. According to Commercial Director Philip Stöhr, the plant will first produce ready-to-install spare parts for GMH Gruppe companies. The facility currently utilizes MoldJet and sintering processes to handle serial production, development, and qualification projects.

GMH Gruppe operates more than 20 steel, forging, and casting sites with around 6,000 employees and produces steel from recycled scrap using electric arc furnaces. CEO Dr. Alexander Becker stated that ProMateria combines advanced metallurgical expertise with digital manufacturing. Following its initial internal projects, ProMateria plans to target external industrial customers for complex metal components and small production runs that are difficult to make economically using conventional methods.
