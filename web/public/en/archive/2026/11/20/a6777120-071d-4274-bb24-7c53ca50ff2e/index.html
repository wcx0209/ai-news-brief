Historical brief · 2026-11-20

Anthropic shares close 17% higher on first day of trading

AI giant Anthropic became the first major model developer to list on Wall Street, raising a record $81.3 billion in its Nasdaq debut. Despite market jitters and tech bubble fears, the company's shares closed 17% higher, giving ordinary investors their first direct stake in a leading standalone AI developer.

Le Monde AI ↗Source published 2026-11-20 01:52 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

On Thursday, November 19, 2026, AI maker Anthropic made history as the first leading model developer to go public on Wall Street, defying a broad market sell-off and tech bubble concerns. The company priced its shares at $83, but they opened at $111.50 and ultimately closed around $97, up 17% for the day. Record Fundraising and Valuation Anthropic raised an unprecedented $81.3 billion by selling 980 million shares, comfortably surpassing previous megadebuts like SpaceX. The IPO valued the five-year-old creator of the Claude chatbot at roughly $1.5 trillion initially, with its opening valuation surging to around $2 trillion. Chief Executive Dario Amodei stated that the robust financial position reinforces the company's commitment to building safe AI. Explosive Growth and Market Jitters The historic listing comes amid rapid financial growth, with Anthropic reporting third-quarter revenue surging to $17 billion, up dramatically from $1.4 billion a year prior, largely driven by coding applications. However, the debut also drew scrutiny: critics and protesters voiced concerns over potential existential risks and financial market stability, while the broader tech sector navigated ongoing turbulence surrounding chip giant Nvidia.
