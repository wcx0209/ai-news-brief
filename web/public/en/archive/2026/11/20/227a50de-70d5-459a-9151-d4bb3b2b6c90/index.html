Inkling 2 Mini: A compact multimodal AI model with loop architecture for local setups

Thinking Machines Lab has released Inkling 2 Mini, a multimodal mixture-of-experts model designed for local AI setups. With around 26 billion total physical parameters and 3 billion active parameters per token, it uses a clever looping mechanism to simulate a much larger model.

Hugging Face ↗Source published 2026-11-20 08:00 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

Thinking Machines Lab reports that Inkling 2 Mini features 24 decoder layers, with the 12 middle layers running three times in a loop to act as 48 logical layers. This design allows the model to maintain a compact physical size while retaining high capacity. It uses NVFP4 and MXFP8 expert weights and supports a 1-million-token context size.

In benchmark tests, the model leads in 32 out of 48 categories against similar mid-sized models, excelling in coding, agentic search, and vision interpretation. Inference support is available out-of-the-box through transformers, llama.cpp, MLX, and SGLang, alongside a DSpark drafter for speculative decoding speed-ups.
