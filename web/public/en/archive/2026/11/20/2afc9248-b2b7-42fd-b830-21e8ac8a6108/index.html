Historical brief · 2026-11-20

Anthropic makes history with $81bn IPO as shares close up 17%

Anthropic pulled off the largest IPO in history, raising $81bn and briefly hitting a $2tn valuation on its first day of trading. Despite the massive debut and a closing price up about 17% at $97, it wasn't enough to rescue the broader stock market, which closed sharply lower amid ongoing investor worries about the AI boom.

The Guardian AI ↗Source published 2026-11-20 06:13 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

Trading Debut and Market Valuation

Anthropic officially went public on the Nasdaq, pricing its shares at $83 before opening at $111.50. During its first trading session, the creator of the Claude chatbot briefly crossed a $2tn valuation, though it settled at a closing price of roughly $97, leaving its market cap around $1.3tn to $1.75tn. The blockbuster offering raised $81bn, surpassing SpaceX's previous record.

Wider Market Slump

Despite the massive enthusiasm for Anthropic, Wall Street closed sharply lower, with the S&P 500 falling for its sixth straight session. Broader investor anxiety about the sustainability of the AI boom, combined with recent tech sector turbulence involving Nvidia, dragged down major indices. Meanwhile, IMF chief Kristalina Georgieva warned that surging global demand for AI infrastructure is driving up inflation and could force central banks to raise interest rates.

Founder Fortunes and Safety Warnings

The IPO generated massive paper wealth for Anthropic's seven co-founders, whose stakes are now valued at over $180bn, with plans to donate significant portions to philanthropic causes. Shortly before ringing the opening bell, CEO Dario Amodei issued a public warning acknowledging the real risks of AI, from economic disruption to loss of control, emphasizing that avoiding these dangers depends entirely on prioritizing safety.
