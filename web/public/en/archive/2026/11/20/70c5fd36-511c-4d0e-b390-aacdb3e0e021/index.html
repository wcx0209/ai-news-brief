OpenAI details how it blocks bad actors from stealing AI reasoning capabilities

According to OpenAI News: OpenAI explains how it detects and stops adversarial distillation, a technique bad actors use to copy advanced AI capabilities without safety protections. The company highlights new defenses in GPT-6.2 Astra that dramatically lower the success rate of stealing the AI's internal reasoning steps.

OpenAI News ↗Source published 2026-11-20 18:00 SGTOfficial releaseArtificial Intelligence

According to OpenAI News: OpenAI reports that unauthorized distillation allows bad actors to reproduce frontier AI capabilities, such as advanced coding or cyber skills, without safety safeguards. Attackers often target a model's protected reasoning, which is the internal chain of thought generated while working toward an answer. To counter this, OpenAI combines adversarial training, input and output classifiers, and strict API-level protections. In tests with GPT-6.2 Astra, the average attack success rate for extracting reasoning from an earlier request dropped from 54.0% to 0.2%. OpenAI also conducts campaign-level investigations to disrupt coordinated extraction attempts and supports U.S. legislation and export controls to address national security risks.
