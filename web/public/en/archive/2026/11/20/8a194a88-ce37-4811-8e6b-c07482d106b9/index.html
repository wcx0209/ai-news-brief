Meta's AI Assistant Muse Is Fueling Instagram Unfollow Drama

Meta's AI assistant Muse is being used by Instagram users to identify and mass-unfollow accounts that don't follow them back. This trend allows people to easily automate social audits and cut ties without getting flagged for spam, exposing the shifting etiquette of digital relationships.

WIRED AI ↗Source published 2026-11-20 20:00 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

According to WIRED, Meta's AI assistant Muse has inadvertently sparked a viral trend where users leverage the chatbot to audit their social circles on Instagram. Users like Khalil Roberts and tech worker Kimberly Munoz have used simple prompts to instruct Muse to generate lists of non-reciprocal followers and automate unfollow actions. While some view this as a convenient way to clean up cluttered feeds or clear out dormant connections, researchers note it highlights a broader shift toward outsourcing emotionally uncomfortable decisions to technology. Meanwhile, Meta previously removed an advertising campaign promoting Muse's unfollow tracking features in October, though the company did not comment on the decision.
