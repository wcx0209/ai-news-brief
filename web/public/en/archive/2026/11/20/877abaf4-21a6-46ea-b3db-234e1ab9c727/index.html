Apple Developing AI Agents to Control Devices, While Anthropic Tops Trillion-Dollar Valuation After IPO

Apple is recruiting engineers to build AI agents capable of writing code, browsing the web, and controlling applications. Meanwhile, Claude developer Anthropic went public on Nasdaq, with its market value briefly surging past $2 trillion on its first trading day before closing at around $1.27 trillion.

iFanr 爱范儿 ↗Source published 2026-11-20 08:29 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

Apple has added a job posting for a senior engineering manager to develop proactive AI assistants capable of executing multi-step tasks across apps, browsers, and codebases, with a target timeline around late next year. Concurrently, Apple is tightening third-party AI permissions in macOS, increasing verification steps for tools like ChatGPT.

In the AI industry, Anthropic completed its historic IPO on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT. Priced at $83 per share, the stock surged on debut, pushing its valuation briefly past $2 trillion before closing up 17% at $97.17, yielding a market cap of roughly $1.27 billion.

Other notable developments include Google bringing Computer Use to Mac versions of Gemini, allowing background app control, and DeepSeek completing an equity restructuring that brought in major industry backers like Tencent and Contemporary Amperex Technology.
