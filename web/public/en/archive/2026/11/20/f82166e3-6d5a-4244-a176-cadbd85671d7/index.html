SK hynix Showcases Memory-Centric AI Vision at Supercomputing 2026

According to SK hynix Newsroom: SK hynix presented its next-generation memory technologies at Supercomputing 2026, highlighting how memory-centric systems and CXL-based pooled memory can solve AI infrastructure bottlenecks. The company demonstrated live solutions including MARS architecture and computational DRAM to improve data processing efficiency for large language models.

SK hynix Newsroom ↗Source published 2026-11-20 07:57 SGTOfficial releaseArtificial Intelligence

According to SK hynix Newsroom:

At Supercomputing 2026 in Chicago, SK hynix introduced its technology vision focused on reshaping the AI ecosystem around advanced memory solutions. The company showcased its Memory-centric AI Rack System (MARS) alongside three key CXL-based demonstrations, including rack-level KV cache sharing, computational memory pools for AI agents, and token-sharing across CPU servers to reduce GPU idle time.

Additionally, SK hynix presented advanced hardware zones featuring KV Cache Offloading over CXL, Compute-using-DRAM, and Custom HBM Array. Alongside these conceptual designs, the company displayed its core product portfolio for AI infrastructure, featuring next-generation HBM4 and HBM4E, high-capacity 256GB 3DS DDR5 RDIMM modules, and liquid-cooling-supported eSSDs.
