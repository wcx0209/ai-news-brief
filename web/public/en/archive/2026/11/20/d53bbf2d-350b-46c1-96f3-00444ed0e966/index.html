Meta's Upcoming Muse Charm AI Device May Cost $300

According to reports, Meta plans to price its upcoming Muse Charm AI wearable at $300. This Tamagotchi-like pocket device lets users interact with an AI assistant physically, though AI wearables face tough market competition and varying price points.

TechCrunch AI ↗Source published 2026-11-20 05:58 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

Meta is reportedly preparing to price its upcoming AI hardware product, Muse Charm, at $300, based on information from The Information. When contacted by TechCrunch, a Meta spokesperson did not deny the pricing report, stating they are charmed by the interest and will share more details soon.

Muse Charm functions as a physical companion for Meta's Muse AI agent, allowing users to carry it in their pocket or wear it around their neck to chat and give commands. While Meta has seen relative success with its software-based AI tools and avatars, hardware is a new frontier.

The $300 price tag places it on the higher end of the current AI wearable market, comparable to devices like Friend at $250, but significantly more expensive than Amazon's Bee Frontier wearable, which lists for $75.
