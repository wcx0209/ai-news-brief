How Developers Use Frontier AI Agents to Build Simulations in NVIDIA Omniverse

According to NVIDIA Robotics: NVIDIA details how developers are combining frontier AI models with Omniverse libraries to build and test simulations. By directing AI agents through natural-language prompts, engineers can quickly turn 3D assets, CAD data, and site scans into functional digital twins for robotics testing, industrial automation, and virtual reality workflows.

Artificial Intelligence × Advanced Manufacturing & Industrial Infrastructure

According to NVIDIA Robotics:

NVIDIA highlights a series of developer projects demonstrating how frontier AI models and Omniverse libraries are transforming simulation workflows. Engineers and developers are using natural-language instructions to guide AI agents in assembling 3D assets, configuring physics engines, and running tests across diverse domains.

Key examples include building facility-scale digital twins from engineering data, creating robot pick-and-place simulations using standardized asset libraries, and testing humanoids and robotic cable-routing tasks in virtual environments. Developers also report challenges, noting that automated tests sometimes miss tactile issues like object shaking, which require manual inspection and prompt adjustments to resolve.

These workflows leverage OpenUSD frameworks and specialized Omniverse libraries to connect rendering, physics, and sensor simulation, enabling teams to evaluate industrial operations and autonomous systems before deploying them in the physical world.
