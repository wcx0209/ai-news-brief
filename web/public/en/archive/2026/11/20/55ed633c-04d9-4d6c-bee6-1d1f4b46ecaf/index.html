Li Auto built a chip that made it to TSMC's stage, but wants you to totally forget about it while driving

Li Auto has developed its proprietary "Mach M100" AI chip using a dynamic dataflow architecture, achieving high compute utilization. The company shared its design at TSMC's OIP Forum. This self-developed chip tackles the memory wall problem to power advanced vision-language-action driving models, aiming to make assisted driving feel smoother and more human-like.

iFanr 爱范儿 ↗Source published 2026-11-20 18:39 SGTMedia reportArtificial Intelligence

Traditional chips struggle with AI workloads due to the von Neumann architecture bottleneck, where data is constantly shuttled back and forth between processors and memory, wasting a huge portion of compute power. Li Auto's Mach M100 replaces this with a dynamic dataflow architecture that streamlines data processing directly across computing units. Built on a 5nm automotive-grade process, the 1280 TOPS chip achieves over 82% compute utilization. This high efficiency allows vehicles to run advanced AI models and native 3D visual encoders smoothly, removing the hesitation and jerks often found in traditional smart driving systems.
