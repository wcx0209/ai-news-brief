Biopharma Executives Gather for London Life Sciences Week Amid Strong M&A and China Policy Clouds

At London Life Sciences Week, biopharma executives and investors reported a very healthy sector driven by major multibillion-dollar acquisitions and record venture funds. However, industry players warned that looming U.S. restrictions on investments in China and Europe's persistent lack of a local Nasdaq equivalent continue to shape global strategic planning.

Fierce Biotech ↗Source published 2026-11-20 21:40 SGTMedia reportBiotech & Programmable Life

During London Life Sciences Week, heavy dealmaking stole the spotlight as pharmaceutical giants like Johnson & Johnson and Eli Lilly announced massive acquisitions, including a $2.2 billion deal by J&J and multi-billion-dollar oncology and fibrosis pacts by Lilly. Legal and financial experts from Baker McKenzie, Sofinnova Partners, and Forbion noted that M&A activity is expected to remain robust, bolstered by record-breaking European venture capital funds such as Sofinnova’s $815 million raise and Forbion’s 2.3 billion euro fund.

Despite the optimistic outlook, significant headwinds remain. Investors pointed out that Europe still lacks a local equivalent to Nasdaq, forcing growing biotechs to look across the Atlantic for major public listings. Furthermore, the rapid rise of China's automated clinical trial infrastructure has become a central topic of discussion, with U.S. investors rushing to lock in deals ahead of potential regulatory clampdowns by the U.S. Treasury regarding outbound investments in pharmaceutical compounds.
