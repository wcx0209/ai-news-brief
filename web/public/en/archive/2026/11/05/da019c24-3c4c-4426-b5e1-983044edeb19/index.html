Open model safety: lessons from training and evaluating Beam

Reflection's new open-weight model, Beam, represents a significant step in balancing safety and utility for frontier AI. Through a technique called multi-teacher on-policy distillation, the model combines the capability of a performance-focused teacher with the guardrails of a safety-focused teacher. This enables Beam to refuse harmful requests while remaining helpful on legitimate tasks. To rigorously test Beam, Reflection developed Iris Safety Evaluations (ISE) and the Open Safety Framework (OSF). These frameworks assess potential risks across multiple categories, including chemical, biological, and cyber threats, alongside agentic challenges like indirect prompt injection. Independent testing showed that Beam matches or exceeds the safety standards of existing Western and Chinese open-weight models, without expanding overall system risks.
