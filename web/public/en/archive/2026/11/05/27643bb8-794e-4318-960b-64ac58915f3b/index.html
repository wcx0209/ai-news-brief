Mistral Updates Studio to Help Companies Build AI Agents and Apps

Mistral has announced major upgrades to Mistral Studio, its enterprise AI platform, alongside the release of its new flagship model, Mistral Large 4 (affectionately nicknamed Le Chonk). The updated Studio platform introduces managed agents, automated workflows, and enterprise application building tools designed to move corporate AI projects out of the pilot phase and into active production. Core Features The new Studio platform brings together agents, workflows, and apps on a shared infrastructure. Agents can investigate complex tasks and take actions, workflows coordinate multi-step business processes with human approvals, and custom apps give employees direct access to these AI systems. Developers can build locally using familiar coding agents and deploy directly to Studio with a single command. Enterprise Security and Control A major focus of the release is data sovereignty and security. Enterprises can deploy Mistral Studio in their own cloud environments, private VPCs, or entirely on-premises. The platform provides OpenTelemetry traces for full observability, allowing companies to audit model calls, tool usage, and approval steps to ensure safe and reliable operations.
