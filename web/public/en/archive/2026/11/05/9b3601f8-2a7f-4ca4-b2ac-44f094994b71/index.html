Atlas World Model Gets Real-Time Exploration with Frameboy

According to World Labs: Researchers have released Frameboy, a new demo that lets users explore 3D worlds generated by the Atlas world model in real-time at up to 15 fps. By streaming frames from cloud GPUs straight to the browser, the tool removes the frustrating wait times of traditional AI video generation.

World Labs ↗Source published 2026-11-05 08:00 SGTOfficial releaseArtificial Intelligence

According to World Labs:

Background

Two months ago, the team introduced Atlas, an autoregressive world model capable of generating frames one at a time with precise camera control. However, generating videos previously required waiting tens of seconds or minutes, forcing users to be very cautious with their inputs and severely limiting creative freedom.

Details and Features

The newly released Frameboy demo streams optimized Atlas-generated frames from cloud GPUs directly to web browsers at up to 15 fps. Additionally, the team integrated this capability into Viewfinder, a web app that allows users to build custom scenes frame-by-frame using a single input image or text caption, and then explore them live.

Impact

By dropping latency and making generation nearly instantaneous, the cost of a bad idea becomes zero. Users no longer perceive a distinct act of waiting for generation, turning what used to be a tedious trial-and-error process into an intuitive exploration experience that paves the way for future generative 3D renderers.
