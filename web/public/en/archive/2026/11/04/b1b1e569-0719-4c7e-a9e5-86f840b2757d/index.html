Tencent Hunyuan Releases Hy4, Joining the Ranks of Top Open Source Models

Tencent Hunyuan has released its latest large language model, Hy4, marking its entry into the top tier of open-source models. The model introduces visual understanding and computer-use capabilities, significantly boosting performance across

www.tencent.com ↗Archive date 2026-11-04News summaryArtificial Intelligence

Key Upgrades Compared to earlier preview versions, Hy4 significantly scales up training data and introduces core capabilities like visual understanding, complex chart analysis, and computer use. It handles professional software like Blender and CAD, alongside long-term cross-application tasks. Productivity and Scenario Integration Hy4 deeply integrates with Tencent's product ecosystem, powering tools like WorkBuddy. It excels in financial analysis—ranking first in open-source evaluations by human experts—as well as frontend and game development across engines like Unreal and Unity. Safety and Pricing The model emphasizes responsible open sourcing by filtering out aggressive cybersecurity data and auditing biological risks. Tencent keeps the pricing unchanged, offering high cost-effectiveness alongside an upcoming 'Flash' version.
