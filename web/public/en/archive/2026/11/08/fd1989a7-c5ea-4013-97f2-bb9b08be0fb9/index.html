GitHub, Google, and Siemens Share 15 Design Patterns for AI Agents

Tech giants GitHub, Google, and Siemens have pooled their expertise to outline 15 design patterns for building reliable AI agent systems. These patterns help developers manage complex tasks, ensure data accuracy, and make AI systems safer and more practical for real-world business applications.

ITmedia AI+ ↗Source published 2026-11-08 07:00 SGTNews summaryArtificial Intelligence

Background

As AI agents move beyond simple chat interfaces into complex, multi-step workflows, ensuring reliability and structural control has become a major challenge for developers. Key Details GitHub, Google, and Siemens have released a comprehensive guide detailing 15 design patterns for agentic systems. These include data validation schemas, error-handling loops, human-in-the-loop verification, and advanced context management using tools like Model Context Protocol (MCP). The patterns address common failure points such as infinite loops, formatting errors, and unverified data modifications. Impact These standardized patterns provide a practical roadmap for companies looking to deploy AI agents securely. By establishing clear boundaries and verification steps, they minimize the risk of costly errors and pave the way for autonomous AI systems in software development and enterprise operations.
