Capital cools on AI world models as investors get pickier

QbitAI 量子位Artificial IntelligenceBusinessSource published2026-11-08 12:09 SGT
Media reportStage not established

Background

Earlier this year, world models became one of the hottest topics in AI, drawing massive attention and capital from eager investors trying not to miss the next big thing. However, after major industry events and a reality check from secondary markets, the initial excitement quickly evaporated. Key Details Many early investments were driven by FOMO, leading to inflated valuations. As public market benchmarks faced adjustments and funding timelines slowed down, investors started scrutinizing startups much more carefully. Despite the overall slowdown, elite founders with strong academic backgrounds in top institutions still managed to pull in hundreds of millions. Impact The market shift marks the end of an era where companies could easily raise funds just by slapping a buzzword on a pitch deck. The industry is now entering a phase that demands rigorous validation, pushing startups to prove their technical capabilities and practical applications in the physical world.
