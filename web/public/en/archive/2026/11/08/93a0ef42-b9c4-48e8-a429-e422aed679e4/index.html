US Midterm Vote Puts Brakes on AI Datacentre Expansion

The Public Backlash Recent US midterm elections brought public frustration with the AI boom to the ballot box. Voters in multiple areas passed measures to limit datacentre expansion, reacting to disruptions like glaring light pollution from mega-sheds and growing economic inequality while tech leaders amass massive fortunes. Market Realities This grassroots pushback coincides with a growing market rethink. Analysts and financial institutions like the IMF are questioning whether the astronomical investments in physical AI infrastructure are justified, especially as cheaper models emerge and businesses start pushing back against soaring costs. A Broader Reckoning While artificial intelligence remains a powerful tool, the combination of public annoyance, environmental concerns, and shifting financial sentiment suggests the unchecked tech rush may finally face meaningful boundaries set by society.
