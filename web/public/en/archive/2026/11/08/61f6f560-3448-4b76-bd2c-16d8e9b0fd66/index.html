Why a former Vice Chairman of the Joint Chiefs co-founded a defense AI startup

Former Vice Chairman of the Joint Chiefs General Paul Selva explains how manual logistics during Hurricane Katrina inspired him to co-found DEFCON AI. The startup builds AI software designed to help military planners make faster decisions in logistics and mobility, addressing the fragmentation of critical operational data.

The Origins of DEFCON AI

Former Vice Chairman of the Joint Chiefs of Staff, General Paul Selva, co-founded DEFCON AI after a 39-year career in the United States Air Force. The venture is rooted in a pivotal realization during Hurricane Katrina in 2005. While watching the disaster unfold from Iraq, Selva observed that critical information needed by his team was scattered across disparate systems, and data aggregation remained entirely manual. Modernizing Military Logistics with AI

DEFCON AI focuses on building artificial intelligence software, modeling, simulation, and analysis toolsets specifically tailored for military transportation and logistics. According to the company's background, the software aims to provide resilient optimization of complex systems, empowering military planners to anticipate, assess, and mitigate network disruptions in dynamically changing operational environments.
