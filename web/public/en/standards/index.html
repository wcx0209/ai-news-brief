Who we are

Polyemergence is published and operated by PolyEmergence Pte Ltd., a Singapore-based technology startup whose principal activities are data and investment. The publication covers eleven emerging-technology fields for English- and Chinese-language readers, including AI, programmable life, energy, robotics and quantum technology. Its domain registry supports further expansion.

The organizational byline used on briefs is Polyemergence Editorial Desk. It identifies the accountable publishing organization; it is not a fictional individual journalist.

How AI is used

Automation and large language models assist with source discovery, relevance filtering, translation, summarisation, classification, tagging and scoring. They may also help format archive and topic pages.

AI assistance is disclosed on every brief. Automated output can be incomplete or wrong. The original source link is retained so readers can inspect the underlying material, and reader reports can trigger review and correction.

Sources and verification

  • We prioritize official company, research, government and regulatory sources, alongside established international and regional publishers.
  • Each brief identifies its publisher, source type, original URL and original publication time.
  • A linked summary is not an independent fact-check. Unless a brief explicitly states otherwise, Polyemergence has not independently corroborated every claim in the source.
  • Material claims should not be strengthened beyond what the linked source supports. Uncertainty in the source should remain uncertainty in the summary.

Evidence and related fields

Visible evidence labels identify official releases, preprints, research papers, interviews, open-source material or publisher reports. An institution's news release is not itself a peer-reviewed paper; an official source is not independent verification. A maturity label is only added when the supplied material explicitly supports it. Otherwise the stage is unspecified.

Each item keeps one primary field and at most two related fields. Reading context is an editorial lens, not an additional verified fact. Opinion, analysis, advertising and sponsored material must be labeled explicitly before publication. Polyemergence currently carries no unlabeled sponsored editorial content.

Ranking method

Importance = 35% Signal + 35% Structure + 30% Consequence. Signal considers novelty, timing and traceability. Structure is an assessment of a single item's possible systemic relevance, not proof of a historical trend. Consequence considers possible business, policy and social effects.

The Daily Brief uses original publication times within the past 48 hours and an initial minimum importance of 65. Empty fields are not filled with below-threshold stories. Academic items retain Importance 70, Confidence 65 and a maximum of two per brief. These are revisable editorial thresholds, not scientific probabilities.

Confidence reflects evidence visible in the supplied material, not the probability that every claim is true. Momentum reflects attention, not accuracy. The detailed assessment can be expanded on an item; neither score establishes independent verification.

Ongoing observations

A separate observation section links matching archived evidence from the past 30 days; it is not part of the 48-hour news list. In this initial release every card remains a tentative signal. Heuristic report groups cannot confirm distinct events or publisher independence, especially across languages. They are not automatically promoted to trends, forecasts or proof of causation.

Observations are generated using explicit editorial topic rules without additional LLM inference. Each lists its sources, uncertainty and a next check. Sparse evidence is disclosed and empty topics are not filled with invented examples. Undated trending-list appearances are not used as publication evidence. A stronger observation label would require explicit event-identity review and multiple distinct events and publishers.

Conflicts and commercial activity

PolyEmergence Pte Ltd. declares no current financial or commercial conflict of interest in the selection or presentation of Polyemergence coverage.

If the company, its principals or a sponsor later has a material investment or commercial relationship with a covered entity, the relationship must be disclosed on the relevant brief or the item must be excluded. Advertising or sponsorship may not change an editorial score without a visible explanation.

Corrections and feedback

Material errors are corrected promptly after review. A corrected brief should retain a visible note describing what changed and when; material corrections are also recorded in the public Corrections log. Minor spelling or formatting fixes may be made without a log entry.

Readers can contact the editorial desk through the private feedback form.

Images and attribution

Article images are loaded from source-page metadata or established video thumbnail services and remain attributed through the linked source. Broken, incomplete or low-resolution images are replaced with category artwork. An image illustrates the source item; it does not independently verify the claims in the story.
